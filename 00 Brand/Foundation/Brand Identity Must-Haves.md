# Brand Identity Must-Haves

A comprehensive checklist of what every brand needs to implement for quality branding, organized by what actually moves the needle. Use this as an audit framework: each item is something a complete brand has documented, decided, or operationalized.

> [!info] How to use this
> Treat each section as a gate. A brand is not "done" until every must-have is either built or consciously deferred. For Completeful's own answers to these items, see the canon notes linked under Related.

---

## Strategic Foundation

- A clear brand purpose and mission (why you exist beyond making money)
- A defined target audience with documented personas
- A unique value proposition that articulates what makes you different
- Brand positioning that stakes out a specific spot in the market
- Core brand values that guide decisions and behavior

---

## Verbal Identity

- A memorable, ownable brand name
- A tagline or slogan that captures the essence
- A documented brand voice (formal vs. casual, playful vs. serious, etc.)
- Tone guidelines for different contexts (sales, support, social, crisis)
- A messaging framework with key proof points and elevator pitches
- A vocabulary list: words you use, words you avoid, terminology standards

---

## Visual Identity

- A primary logo plus variations (horizontal, stacked, icon-only, monochrome)
- Clear space and minimum size rules for the logo
- A defined color palette (primary, secondary, accent) with hex/RGB/CMYK/Pantone values
- Typography system (display, body, accent fonts) with hierarchy rules
- Photography and illustration style guidelines
- Iconography system with consistent stroke weight and style
- Graphic elements, patterns, or motifs unique to the brand

---

## Documentation

- A comprehensive brand guidelines document (the "brand bible")
- A do's and don'ts reference with visual examples
- Templates for common assets (decks, social posts, email signatures, business cards)
- A digital asset library where everything lives in one accessible place

---

## Application Across Touchpoints

- Consistent presence on website, social channels, email, packaging, and physical spaces
- Branded customer experience standards (how you greet, respond, resolve issues)
- Internal brand training so employees actually know how to represent the brand
- Co-branding rules for partnerships and sponsorships

---

## Governance and Evolution

- A brand owner or team responsible for consistency
- A review process for new assets before they go public
- Regular brand audits to spot drift
- A plan for evolution: brands need refreshes every 5 to 10 years to stay relevant
