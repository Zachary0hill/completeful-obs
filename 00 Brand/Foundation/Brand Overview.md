## What Completeful Is

Completeful is a US-based print-on-demand, third-party logistics, and in-house manufacturing company headquartered in Lafayette, Louisiana. Operating out of a 228,000 square foot facility, Completeful handles the full production and fulfillment lifecycle so that independent sellers and emerging brands never have to touch inventory, manage a warehouse, or own a single piece of equipment.

The model is straightforward: sellers design and sell. Completeful creates and fulfills.

## The Elevator Pitch

Most POD sellers eventually hit the same ceiling. Quality is inconsistent. Delivery times are slow because production happens overseas. Pricing is opaque. Order minimums lock out anyone still building volume. Completeful was built to remove every one of those friction points under one roof in the United States.

When a customer places an order in a seller's Shopify, Etsy, TikTok Shop, or WooCommerce store, Completeful receives it, produces it using in-house equipment, and ships it. The seller collects the margin. Completeful handles everything between the checkout confirmation and the delivered package.

## The In-House US Manufacturing Edge

This is the core operational differentiator. Completeful does not route orders to a network of third-party print partners. The company owns and operates its own production equipment inside its Lafayette facility. That includes:

- Six industrial fiber lasers for metal work and powder coating
- CNC routers and fabrication equipment for wood products
- UV printers for hard-surface items including mats and frames
- Sixteen commercial embroidery machines for textiles and leather goods
- Twenty-eight engraving machines capable of handling approximately 600 orders per day at peak
- Sublimation printing for mugs, socks, totes, and puzzles
- Direct-to-garment (DTG) printing on Gildan, Hanes, and Bella Canvas apparel

Owning the equipment means Completeful controls quality at every stage and can compress turnaround times in ways that offshore or distributed-network competitors simply cannot match. During peak holiday periods, the engraving operation alone has shipped more than 10,000 orders in a single month.

## The Catalog

Completeful offers between 500 and 900-plus products with no order minimums. A seller testing a new niche can order a single unit. A seller running a holiday promotion can order tens of thousands. The pricing structure is transparent by design, because sellers cannot confidently price their own products without knowing their costs.

The catalog spans personalized gifts, drinkware, apparel, home goods, bags, cutting boards, jewelry, and more. Many of these products can carry private-label branding or custom packaging for sellers who want to build a brand rather than just a product.

## Services at a Glance

Completeful operates across five service lines that can be used independently or in combination:

**Print-on-demand.** Individual orders produced and shipped as they arrive, with no inventory commitment from the seller.

**3PL warehousing and kitting.** Brands that carry their own inventory can store it at the Lafayette facility and have Completeful pick, kit, and ship on their behalf.

**In-house manufacturing.** Custom production at scale using the equipment categories described above, with quality control managed internally rather than delegated to a vendor network.

**Personalization.** Laser engraving, embroidery, UV printing, and other methods applied to individual orders, a capability that most POD platforms cannot offer at production speed.

**Private label.** Custom branding, packaging, and product development for sellers who want their customers to receive a fully branded unboxing experience.

## Why Completeful Exists

Independent sellers and small brands are underserved by the existing fulfillment landscape. Overseas POD providers offer low prices but sacrifice speed and quality control. Domestic warehousing solutions are built for established brands with volume commitments. There was no platform built from the ground up to serve a Etsy or TikTok Shop seller on day one of their business and still be the right partner when that seller is doing six figures a year.

Completeful fills that gap. It is designed to grow with sellers, not price them out or require them to graduate to a different provider as they scale. The tagline is not just positioning language. It describes an actual division of labor that is built into every system Completeful operates.
