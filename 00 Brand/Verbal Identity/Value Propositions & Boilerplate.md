## Company Boilerplate

### Short (1 sentence)

Completeful is a US-based print-on-demand and fulfillment company with in-house manufacturing in Lafayette, Louisiana, offering sellers on Etsy, Shopify, TikTok Shop, and WooCommerce access to 500-plus products with no order minimums and transparent pricing.

### Long (1 paragraph)

Completeful is a print-on-demand, third-party logistics, and in-house manufacturing company headquartered in Lafayette, Louisiana. Operating from a 228,000 square foot facility, Completeful produces and ships products for independent sellers and emerging brands on Etsy, Shopify, TikTok Shop, and WooCommerce. With in-house production spanning laser engraving, embroidery, UV printing, sublimation, direct-to-garment printing, and CNC woodworking, Completeful offers over 500 products with no order minimums and published transparent pricing. The company's domestic manufacturing model delivers faster turnaround, consistent quality, and personalization capabilities at commercial scale. Sellers design and sell. Completeful creates and fulfills.

## Core Value Propositions

These are the six most essential value claims Completeful can make. Each is grounded in a specific operational or structural fact and should be used as the basis for content, ad copy, landing pages, and sales communications.

**1. In-house US manufacturing**
Every product is made in Lafayette, Louisiana, using equipment Completeful owns and operates. No overseas production, no partner network, no quality variance between facilities. This is the root of every other advantage: speed, consistency, and personalization capability all flow from owning the equipment.

**2. No order minimums**
A seller can order one unit. There is no volume threshold to unlock access to any product, pricing tier, or production method. This makes Completeful genuinely useful on day one of a business, not just after a seller has already achieved scale.

**3. Transparent pricing**
Pricing is published. Sellers know their cost before they set their listing price. There are no surprise fees, no volume thresholds that change unit economics without warning, and no rates that require a sales call to access.

**4. Personalization at production scale**
Completeful's engraving, embroidery, and laser operations run at commercial volume. At peak, the engraving department handles approximately 600 orders per day. This means personalized products, which command higher prices and generate stronger customer loyalty, are available to any seller without requiring their own production infrastructure.

**5. Catalog breadth from one partner**
More than 500 products spanning apparel, drinkware, home goods, personalized gifts, wood, metal, leather, and more are available through a single platform, login, and shipping account. Sellers can expand their product offering without adding vendor complexity.

**6. Private label and 3PL**
Completeful goes beyond POD. Sellers who want a branded customer experience can access private label packaging. Sellers with their own inventory can store it at the Lafayette facility and use Completeful for 3PL warehousing and kitting. These services extend the platform's usefulness well beyond a standard print-on-demand relationship.

## Elevator Pitches by Audience

### For a beginner side-hustler on Etsy or TikTok Shop

"Completeful is a print-on-demand platform that makes everything in the USA. No order minimums, so you can test products without any upfront commitment. They handle production and shipping, you handle your store and your customers. Pricing is published so you always know your margin before you list."

### For a scaling seller looking to switch from Printify or Printful

"Completeful makes everything in-house at its own facility in Louisiana, which means no quality variance from different print partners. They also do personalization at scale, including laser engraving and embroidery, which most platforms cannot handle at production volume. If you are in the gifting or wedding niche, or if you want a more reliable fulfillment partner as you scale, it is worth a look."

### For an established brand evaluating 3PL

"Completeful runs a 228,000 square foot facility in Lafayette, Louisiana with both print-on-demand and 3PL warehousing under one roof. They can handle your POD orders, store your own inventory, kit custom packages, and do private label. One vendor, one integration, one shipping relationship."

### For a media or press context

"Completeful is a print-on-demand and fulfillment company based in Lafayette, Louisiana. Unlike platforms that route orders through overseas or third-party production networks, Completeful manufactures everything in-house using its own equipment, giving sellers consistent quality, domestic shipping speeds, and access to personalization capabilities at commercial scale. The company serves sellers on Etsy, Shopify, TikTok Shop, and WooCommerce with no order minimums."

## CTA Language Reference

All calls to action should point to one of two destinations depending on context:

- **completeful.com** for general brand discovery, blog content, and homepage-level CTAs
- **The Completeful app** for in-platform actions, onboarding prompts, and existing seller communications

Standard CTA phrases in order of preference by context:

- "Start selling at completeful.com"
- "Get started at completeful.com"
- "Browse the catalog at completeful.com"
- "Connect your store at completeful.com"
- "Talk to our team at sales@completeful.com"

For support-related CTAs: support@completeful.com
