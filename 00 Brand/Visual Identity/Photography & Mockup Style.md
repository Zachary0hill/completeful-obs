## Overview

Visual consistency across product photography and AI-generated mockups is what makes Completeful's content immediately recognizable and trustworthy to POD sellers. Every image should communicate quality, clarity, and the ease of selling with Completeful. The visual standard applies whether the image is a studio flat-lay, a lifestyle shot, or an AI mockup generated through Photoroom or Nano Banana Pro.

## Product Photography Standards

### Guiding Principle
Product shots exist to give the seller confidence that Completeful's output is high-quality and print-accurate. Every image should show the product as the hero, with nothing detracting from the design, print quality, or fabric/material appearance.

### Flat-Lay Photography
Flat-lay is the default format for catalog imagery, website product listings, and infographic inserts.

- **Background:** Clean, solid backgrounds preferred. White and light neutral (off-white or light warm gray) for catalog and web. Navy or deep charcoal for social content and promotional overlays.
- **Lighting:** Soft, diffused natural light from a 45-degree angle or a studio softbox setup. Avoid harsh direct flash, which creates overexposed hotspots on prints. Shadows should be soft and directional, not deep or distracting.
- **Product position:** Center-frame or slightly above center. Garments should be wrinkle-free and fully unfolded. For non-apparel products (mugs, bags, wall art), keep spacing consistent within a product category shoot.
- **Zoom level:** Product should fill 70-85% of the frame. Leave enough padding around the edges for text overlays in social/carousel use.
- **Angle:** Straight top-down for most flat-lays. Slight 15-degree angle acceptable for supplemental lifestyle-adjacent shots.

### Lifestyle Photography
Lifestyle photography is used for social content, brand storytelling, and content that places the product in context of a real seller's success.

- **Models and settings:** Authentic, relatable environments. A person wearing a custom tee at a farmers market, a small business owner packaging orders, a content creator reviewing a freshly fulfilled order. Avoid overly staged stock-photo aesthetics.
- **Tone:** Warm, optimistic, achievable. The seller should look like they are winning, not struggling.
- **Color temperature:** Warm-neutral (5000-5500K). Avoid cool-toned blue-white processing that makes apparel look cheap or clinical.
- **Brand color presence:** Where possible, ensure the product design or background elements echo Navy, Teal, or Accent Blue. This does not need to be forced, but look for natural opportunities to align.
- **People:** Diverse representation across age, background, and business type. Completeful's seller base is broad.

## AI Mockup Standards

Completeful uses two primary AI mockup tools: Photoroom and Nano Banana Pro. Both require consistent setup and output review to maintain brand-quality visuals.

### Photoroom
Used for background removal, background replacement, and quick composite mockups where a product design is placed on a clean model or object image.

- **Background replacement defaults:** Use brand-aligned backgrounds: Navy, off-white, or lifestyle photography sourced from the Clothing Mockups Drive. Avoid auto-generated fantasy or irrelevant AI backgrounds.
- **Shadow and reflection:** Enable realistic shadow (soft ground shadow, not drop shadow) to anchor the product. Reflection is optional; use only on hard goods, never on apparel.
- **Consistency per campaign:** Within a single product launch or content series, use the same background color and lighting style across all Photoroom outputs.
- **Resolution output:** Export at 2000px minimum on the longest edge for all web and social uses.

### Nano Banana Pro
Used for AI-powered mockup generation that places designs on garments or products with photorealistic rendering.

- **Design placement accuracy:** Always verify that the AI-rendered design placement matches the actual print area dimensions. Compare a Nano Banana Pro mockup against a known physical sample before publishing at scale.
- **Fabric texture and fold fidelity:** Review each mockup for unnatural texture artifacts. Discard outputs where the print appears to "float" on the fabric or where fold distortion makes the design unreadable.
- **Color accuracy:** AI mockup colors should match the product's actual print output. If a mockup renders significantly different saturation or hue, adjust the input design file or flag the image for manual color correction.
- **Use case:** Nano Banana Pro mockups are approved for social content, carousel slides, and website thumbnails. They are not approved for use in legally binding product listings that imply exact color-match guarantees without physical sample verification.

## Consistency Checklist

Before any product image is published or added to a content workflow, verify:

- [ ] Background is brand-approved (white, off-white, Navy, dark charcoal, or lifestyle)
- [ ] Product is sharp and in focus, not motion-blurred
- [ ] Lighting is soft and even; no harsh overexposed highlights on the print area
- [ ] Print design is fully visible and readable at the final display size
- [ ] Image resolution is 2000px minimum on the longest edge (2000x2000 for square social formats)
- [ ] No distracting props, competitor products, or off-brand elements in frame
- [ ] AI mockup has been reviewed for color accuracy and placement fidelity
- [ ] File is named with a consistent convention: `[ProductType]-[Colorway]-[Version]-[YYYYMMDD]`
- [ ] Image is saved in the correct Drive folder for the product category

---

## What to Avoid

- Overprocessed, heavily filtered, or HDR-look images that make the product look artificial
- Dark, underexposed shots where the print design is hard to read
- AI mockup backgrounds that are fantasy landscapes, irrelevant environments, or heavily stylized scenes disconnected from seller reality
- Inconsistent mockup styles within a single content series (e.g., mixing flat-lay and Nano Banana Pro renders in the same carousel)
- Watermarks, other logos, or platform UI visible in product shots used in Completeful's own marketing
