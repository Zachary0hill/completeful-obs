## Overview

All content produced for Completeful follows consistent dimension and layout standards. This document defines the pixel specifications, safe zones, grid systems, and brand element placement for every recurring content format. These specs apply whether content is built in Canva, Figma, Adobe Express, or assembled programmatically through Remotion or n8n automation pipelines.

---

## 7-Slide Carousel (Instagram / LinkedIn / TikTok Slides)

The carousel is Completeful's highest-performing educational content format. Every carousel follows a fixed structure: one hook slide, five content/listicle slides, and one CTA slide.

**Canvas dimensions:** 1080 x 1080px (square) — primary format
**Alternative:** 1080 x 1350px (4:5 portrait) for Instagram feed optimization

**Layout grid:**
- Margins: 80px on all sides
- Content area: 920 x 920px (square), 920 x 1190px (portrait)
- Gutter between text columns (if two-column layout on content slides): 40px

**Slide 1: Hook**
- Background: Deep Charcoal (#111827) or Navy (#1B2A4A)
- Optional: Orange-gold gradient accent bar (16px height) at top or bottom edge within margin
- Headline: Bebas Neue, 64-80px, White, centered or left-aligned
- Sub-headline: Montserrat Bold, 28-32px, Teal (#1ABC9C)
- Brand logo (icon or wordmark): bottom-right corner, white reversed variant, 120px wide, 80px from edges
- No body text on the hook slide; the headline carries the full message

**Slides 2-6: Content / Listicle**
- Background: #1E2D40 (Elevated Surface) or Navy
- Slide number indicator: Montserrat SemiBold, 14px, Teal, top-left corner within margin
- Section headline: Montserrat Bold, 26-32px, White
- Body text: Inter Regular or Medium, 20-22px, White or light gray (#E8ECF0)
- Accent bar or divider: 4px horizontal rule, Teal, between headline and body
- Icon or small illustration: optional, 48-64px, Teal-colored or white, left-aligned before list item

**Slide 7: CTA**
- Background: Navy or a full orange-gold gradient overlay at 85% opacity over Navy
- CTA headline: Bebas Neue, 60-72px, White
- CTA sub-copy: Montserrat SemiBold, 24px, White or Teal
- URL or handle: Inter Regular, 18px, Teal or White
- Logo: centered, white reversed variant, 160px wide
- Visual element: optional product mockup or arrow motif, Teal or white

**Safe area note:** Keep all critical text within the 80px margin. Instagram and TikTok may crop the outer 40px on certain device sizes; no text should touch within 80px of any edge.

## Reel / Short-Form Video

Used for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Text overlays are produced in Remotion or Canva and exported as MP4.

**Canvas dimensions:** 1080 x 1920px (9:16 vertical)
**Frame rate:** 30fps standard; 60fps for product reveal and transition-heavy cuts

**Text safe zone:** 96px margins on left and right; 160px top margin (account for TikTok/Reels UI overlays); 300px bottom margin (account for caption, follow button, and like/comment icons)
**Active content zone:** 888 x 1460px centered in the canvas

**Text overlay standards:**
- Hook text (first 1-2 seconds): Bebas Neue, 72-96px, White, centered, optionally backed by a semi-transparent dark pill (#111827 at 70% opacity, 20px border radius)
- Body/list text: Montserrat Bold, 36-44px, White with dark backing pill
- CTA end card: Bebas Neue 80px headline + Montserrat SemiBold 32px sub-copy; full-frame Navy overlay at 75% opacity with Teal accent

**Logo placement:** Lower-left or lower-right of the active zone, icon-only variant, white, 80px wide. Present throughout or fade in at the CTA card.

## Story (Instagram / TikTok Story)

**Canvas dimensions:** 1080 x 1920px (identical to Reel canvas)
**Safe zone:** Same 96px left/right; 250px top; 340px bottom (both platforms place interactive elements here)
**Active content zone:** 888 x 1330px

Stories are typically single-message, fast-read formats. Limit to one headline, one supporting line, and one visual. Use the full-bleed background approach: either a product mockup photo full-bleed with a Navy or dark scrim overlay, or a flat Navy/Charcoal background with graphic elements.

## Infographic

**Canvas dimensions:** 1080 x 1920px (tall format for feed and Pinterest); 1200 x 628px for blog/LinkedIn header infographic

**Grid:** 4-column grid for tall format; 60px margins; 20px gutters
**Section dividers:** 4px Teal horizontal rule
**Headline font:** Bebas Neue at 64px
**Section titles:** Montserrat Bold at 28px
**Body/stat text:** Inter Regular at 18px
**Data callout boxes:** #1E2D40 background, Teal border (2px), stat figure in Bebas Neue 48px, label in Inter Medium 14px below

---

## Blog Header / YouTube Thumbnail

**Blog header dimensions:** 1200 x 628px
**YouTube thumbnail:** 1280 x 720px (16:9)

Both formats share the same composition logic:
- Background: dark (Navy or Charcoal) with product image or lifestyle photo occupying 50-60% of the frame
- Headline text left-aligned: Bebas Neue for main statement (52-64px), Montserrat Bold for subtitle (24-28px), both in White with Teal accent word where appropriate
- Logo: bottom-left, white reversed, 140px wide
- Visual hierarchy ensures the headline is readable at thumbnail size (consider legibility at 320px wide)

## Color and Type Quick Reference for Templates

| Template Element | Color | Font |
|---|---|---|
| Slide/Card background | Navy or Dark Charcoal | N/A |
| Hook headline | White | Bebas Neue |
| Section title | White or Teal | Montserrat Bold |
| Body copy | White or #E8ECF0 | Inter Regular |
| CTA text | White | Bebas Neue + Montserrat |
| Accent / divider | Teal #1ABC9C | N/A |
| Promo callout | Orange-Gold gradient | Bebas Neue or Montserrat ExtraBold |
| Logo (dark bg) | White (reversed) | N/A |
