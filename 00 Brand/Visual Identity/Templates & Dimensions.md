# Templates & Dimensions

Pixel specs, safe zones, grids and brand element placement for every recurring content format. Applies in Canva, Figma, Adobe Express, Remotion and n8n pipelines.

> [!important] System
> Colors and type follow [[00 Brand/Visual Identity/_Completeful Brand System|Completeful Brand System]]. Pink `#ED128C` is the accent. The gradient `#FF2B92 to #FF8433` appears once per frame. Headlines in Owners (Archivo fallback), body in Inter.

## Template tokens

| Token | Value |
|---|---|
| Dark background | `#0A0A0A` or Ink `#111827` |
| Dark card surface | `#111111` |
| Dark border | `#262626` |
| Light background | `#FFFFFF` or `#F9FAFB`, optional pastel wash |
| Accent | Pink `#ED128C` |
| Pink text on light under 18px | `#C4106A` |
| Signature gradient | `135deg #FF2B92 to #FF8433`, one element per frame |
| Text on dark | `#FFFFFF`, secondary `#E8E8E8` / `#A3A3A3` |
| Text on light | Headline `#111111`, body `#444444` |
| Display font | Owners ExtraBold 800, uppercase, -0.5px tracking |
| Heading font | Owners Bold 700, uppercase |
| Body font | Inter 400 / 500 |
| Corner radius | Cards 24px, pills and chips full, buttons 8px (scale up on 1080 canvases) |

## 7-Slide Carousel (Instagram / LinkedIn / TikTok Slides)

Structure: 1 hook slide, 5 content slides, 1 CTA slide.

- Canvas: 1080 x 1080px (primary), 1080 x 1350px (4:5 feed)
- Margins: 80px all sides
- Content area: 920 x 920px (square), 920 x 1190px (portrait)
- Two column gutter: 40px

**Slide 1: Hook**
- Background: `#0A0A0A`, or dark warehouse photo under a black gradient scrim
- Eyebrow (optional): Inter SemiBold 20px, uppercase, 3px tracking, pink `#ED128C`
- Headline: Owners ExtraBold 800, 72 to 88px, uppercase, white, one word in gradient text
- No body text. The headline carries the message
- Logo: white knockout lockup bottom right, 180px wide, 80px from edges

**Slides 2 to 6: Content**
- Background: `#0A0A0A` with `#111111` cards, 1px `#262626` border, 24px radius. Light alternate: white with `#E5E7EB` border
- Slide number: Owners Bold 20px, pink, top left inside margin
- Section headline: Owners Bold 700, 36 to 44px, uppercase, white
- Body: Inter Regular or Medium, 24 to 28px, `#E8E8E8`
- Divider: 4px rule, pink `#ED128C`, between headline and body
- Icons: 56 to 64px from [[00 Brand/Visual Identity/Iconography|Iconography]] pink set, or white

**Slide 7: CTA**
- Background: `#0A0A0A`, or white with a pastel wash
- CTA headline: Owners ExtraBold 800, 64 to 80px, uppercase
- CTA button: gradient fill, Owners Bold uppercase 32 to 36px, 1px tracking, 16px radius
- URL or handle: Inter Medium 22px, pink or white
- Logo: centered, white knockout on dark, gradient lockup on light, 240px wide

Safe area: nothing critical within 80px of any edge. Some devices crop the outer 40px.

## Reel / Short-Form Video

TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts. Overlays built in Remotion or Canva.

- Canvas: 1080 x 1920px (9:16)
- Frame rate: 30fps standard, 60fps for reveals and fast transitions
- Safe zone: 96px left and right, 160px top, 300px bottom
- Active zone: 888 x 1460px centered

Overlays:

- Hook (first 1 to 2 seconds): Owners ExtraBold, 88 to 110px, uppercase, white, centered. Optional backing pill `#0A0A0A` at 70%, 24px radius
- Body and list text: Inter SemiBold 40 to 48px, white on dark pill
- CTA end card: Owners ExtraBold 88px headline + Inter Medium 36px sub copy. Full frame `#0A0A0A` at 80% with one gradient element (button or underline)
- Logo: emblem only, white or gradient, 96px wide, lower corner of the active zone

## Story (Instagram / TikTok)

- Canvas: 1080 x 1920px
- Safe zone: 96px left and right, 250px top, 340px bottom
- Active zone: 888 x 1330px
- One headline, one support line, one visual
- Full bleed product photo under a black gradient scrim, or flat `#0A0A0A` with one pink or gradient element

## Infographic

- Canvas: 1080 x 1920px (feed, Pinterest), 1200 x 628px (blog, LinkedIn header)
- Grid: 4 columns, 60px margins, 20px gutters
- Section dividers: 4px pink rule
- Headline: Owners ExtraBold 64px uppercase
- Section titles: Owners Bold 32px uppercase
- Body and stats: Inter Regular 20px
- Data callout box: `#111111` fill, 2px pink border, 24px radius, stat in Owners ExtraBold 56px (gradient text allowed on one stat), label Inter Medium 16px
- Light version: white fill, `#E5E7EB` border, pastel wash behind hero stat, pink text `#C4106A` under 18px
- Process graphics keep four steps: Connect your store, Send us your inventory, We pick, personalize and pack, Fast shipping

## Blog Header / YouTube Thumbnail

- Blog header: 1200 x 628px
- YouTube thumbnail: 1280 x 720px
- Background: `#0A0A0A` or dark photo with scrim. Product or warehouse photo fills 50 to 60%
- Headline left aligned: Owners ExtraBold 56 to 72px uppercase, white, one word gradient
- Subtitle: Inter SemiBold 26 to 30px
- Logo: white knockout lockup bottom left, 180px
- Test legibility at 320px wide
