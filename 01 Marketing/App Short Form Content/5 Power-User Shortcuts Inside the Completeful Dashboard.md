**POSTABLE CAPTION (Instagram / TikTok)**

5 dashboard shortcuts that cut the busywork out of running your whole store.

Most sellers do everything the long way, rebuilding designs and hunting for files every session. These five shortcuts move you from manual labor to running the store like a system.

One, reuse Design Templates so new products start from a saved layout instead of a blank canvas. Two, organize My Designs into folders and use search to find any file in seconds, even as the library grows.

Three, let AI Onboarding and AI Upscale handle the heavy design lifting so you spend less time fighting artwork. Four, lean on order syncing so sales route to fulfillment without you touching them.

Five, check the Orders dashboard to track sales at a glance, and use Earnings to follow your synced Etsy ledger instead of guessing. Same store, far less manual time, and more output per session.

Stack a couple of these and your sessions get shorter while the store keeps growing.

Save this and try one shortcut on your next session.
