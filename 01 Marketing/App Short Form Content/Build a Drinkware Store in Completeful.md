**HOOK**
*Five screens to a full drinkware store in Completeful, from empty catalog to a live store.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Filter the Product Catalog to mugs, tumblers, and bottles by print method.
2. Check the print area in the Design Editor so wraps and handles line up.
3. Design once and reuse across the drinkware lineup with My Designs.
4. Generate mockups in the Mockup Generator to preview each vessel.
5. Publish to My Products and connect a store so orders sync with fast domestic fulfillment.
