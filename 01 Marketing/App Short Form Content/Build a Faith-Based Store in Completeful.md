**KEY POINTS**
1. Filter the Product Catalog to apparel and home items that suit faith designs.
2. Design in the Design Editor with custom fonts that give scripture a premium look.
3. Save a Design Template so the whole store feels intentional and consistent.
4. Generate mockups in the Mockup Generator to preview each piece.
5. Publish to My Products and connect a store so orders sync with fast domestic fulfillment.
