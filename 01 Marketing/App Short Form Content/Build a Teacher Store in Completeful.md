**HOOK**
*Five screens to build a teacher store in Completeful, from empty catalog to a live store.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Filter the Product Catalog to gift-ready teacher products like mugs, totes, and tees.
2. Design in the Design Editor using Design Templates built for quick edits.
3. Save versions to My Designs so a full classroom lineup stays organized.
4. Generate mockups in the Mockup Generator that look like real teacher gifts.
5. Publish to My Products and connect a store so orders sync with fast domestic fulfillment.
