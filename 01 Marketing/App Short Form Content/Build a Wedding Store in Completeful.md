**KEY POINTS**
1. Filter the Product Catalog to wedding-party items like tumblers, tees, and totes.
2. Design in the Design Editor and save a Design Template so the suite looks cohesive.
3. Use Design Templates for bridesmaid and groomsman variations without rebuilding.
4. Generate mockups in the Mockup Generator that look gift-ready.
5. Publish to My Products and connect a store so orders sync with fast domestic fulfillment.
