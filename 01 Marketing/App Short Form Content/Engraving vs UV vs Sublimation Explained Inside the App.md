**HOOK**
*Pick the right print method and your design looks the way you imagined on the finished product.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Laser engraving and laser etching cut the design into the surface for a clean, lasting mark.
2. UV printing lays down full color and works across many hard goods.
3. Sublimation infuses color into the material for vivid, all-over results.
4. Filter the catalog by print method so you only see products that fit your design.
5. Matching method to design upfront prevents a flat or muddy result later.

**CALL TO ACTION**
