Turn one design into a full product line in minutes by applying it across multiple products at once.

Recreating the same artwork on every product one at a time is the slow way to build a collection. The multi-design feature puts one design onto multiple products in a single flow.

Here is how I use it. I open my design and choose the multi-design option, then select several products from the catalog to apply it to. The design lands on each one at the same time.

From there I review how it sits on every product before committing, so I catch anything that needs adjusting on a specific item. This is where a single piece of artwork becomes a whole collection without rebuilding it product by product. Pair it with mockups to preview the full lineup fast, then save them all to My Products.
