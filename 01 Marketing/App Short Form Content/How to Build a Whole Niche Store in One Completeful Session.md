**POSTABLE CAPTION (Instagram / TikTok)**

Stand up a focused niche store in one sitting instead of dragging it out for weeks.

Most people build one product, lose steam, and the store never fills out. A single focused session gets you to a real lineup before the motivation fades.

Start by filtering the catalog to one niche so every product points at the same buyer. Then use AI Onboarding to draft a design and listing fast, so you are editing instead of staring at a blank canvas.

Save a Design Template so the entire store reads as one brand with consistent colors and fonts. Use Multi-design to expand a few products into a fuller catalog without rebuilding each one from scratch.

Finally, publish to My Products and connect a store so orders sync from the start. You finish the session with a real niche store and a working pipeline, not a single lonely product and a half-built plan.

Save this and build a focused niche store in one sitting.
