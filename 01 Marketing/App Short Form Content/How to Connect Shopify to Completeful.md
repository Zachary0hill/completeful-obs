*Connect Shopify once and every order syncs into Completeful on its own, so fulfillment runs without you chasing it.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Automatic order syncing keeps your Shopify sales moving without manual entry.
2. The connection starts on the Sales Channels page of your dashboard.
3. You approve access through Shopify, so the link stays in your control.
4. After connecting, orders appear in the Orders dashboard with live statuses.
5. Production happens in-house in Lafayette with fast domestic fulfillment.

**CALL TO ACTION**
Open Completeful, go to Sales Channels, and link your Shopify store today.
