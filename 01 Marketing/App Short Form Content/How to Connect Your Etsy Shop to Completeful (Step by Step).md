**POSTABLE CAPTION (Instagram / TikTok)**

Stop retyping every Etsy order by hand. Connect your shop once and do this first.

If you sell on Etsy, manually moving each order into fulfillment is the slow part that quietly eats your day. Connecting once fixes it for good, because automatic order syncing sends your Etsy sales into production without you touching a thing.

The connection lives on the Sales Channels page of your dashboard. You choose Etsy, and it sends you to Etsy's own approval screen where you confirm access, so the link stays secure and in your control. Back in Completeful, your shop shows as connected.

From there, two things happen on their own. Orders sync automatically and route to in-house fulfillment in the USA with no overseas transit. Your Etsy ledger also feeds the Earnings page, so you can filter later by date range and by account. That means less retyping and a cleaner view of what you actually earned, while you spend your time on designs instead of data entry.
