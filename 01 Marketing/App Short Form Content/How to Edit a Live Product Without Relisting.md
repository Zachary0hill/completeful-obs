**POSTABLE CAPTION (Instagram / TikTok)**

Stop relisting over a typo. Fix the design or swap a mockup on a live product without starting over.

Spotting a typo after a product goes live should not mean rebuilding it from zero. You can edit a live product right from the My Products page and keep everything you already have.

Here is how I do it. I open the My Products page, find the live product, and open it. From there I jump into the Design Editor and fix the text, then update the mockup if it needs it.

I save the changes to the same product, so there is no new listing and no lost history. That is the whole point: small fixes take seconds instead of a full relist, and the product stays exactly where it was. Less rework like this adds up, and the time you save goes straight back into creating new products instead of redoing old ones.

Edit a live product in the Completeful app the next time you spot a fix.
