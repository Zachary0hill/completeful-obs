**POSTABLE CAPTION (Instagram / TikTok)**

Stop picking colors from memory. A store that looks like one brand earns more trust.

When every product uses slightly different colors and fonts, a shop looks thrown together and buyers feel it. Consistency is what makes a small store read as a real brand.

Upload your Custom Fonts once so your type stays the same on every product instead of drifting from listing to listing. Then build a master design in the Design Editor with your set colors and type, rather than guessing a shade each time.

Save that finished look as a Design Template so new products start from the same palette and type. File those templates in My Designs folders so your on-brand work stays organized and easy to reuse next drop. For the next product, start from the saved template instead of building from scratch.

Do this once and every new product matches the last one. The store stops looking like a collection of one-offs and starts looking like one brand.

Save this and build one reusable Design Template for your store this week.
