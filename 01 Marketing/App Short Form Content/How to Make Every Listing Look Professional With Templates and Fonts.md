**POSTABLE CAPTION (Instagram / TikTok)**

Stop guessing fonts and layout on every listing. A saved template makes your shop look established.

When every listing uses different fonts and spacing, the shop looks scattered and buyers hesitate. Consistency is what makes a small shop read as established and trustworthy, and you can get it without any brand-kit feature.

Two steps do it. Upload your brand fonts in Completeful so the same type is ready in the Design Editor every time. Then build one clean design with your colors and type, and save it as a template.

From there, every new product starts from that template instead of a blank canvas, so the whole catalog matches. Custom fonts give you a signature style that look-alike listings cannot copy quickly. The best part is that you set it once, and every future design starts on brand.

Save this and set up your fonts and a template before your next design.
