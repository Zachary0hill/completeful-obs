3 ways to keep My Designs findable so you never rebuild work you already made.

Once you have a few dozen designs, a messy library quietly costs you time every single day. An organized one means you never rebuild work you already did, and it only takes three habits.

First, folders. I group my work by niche, season, or product type and move designs into them, so related pieces live together. Second, search. When I need a specific design, I type a few characters and it surfaces in seconds instead of forcing me to scroll.

Third, save. Every design I make goes into My Designs so nothing slips away. The whole library stays calm and findable. As my catalog grows, this system grows with it, which means my time goes to creating instead of hunting for files. Set this up early, before the mess starts, and your future self will thank you.
