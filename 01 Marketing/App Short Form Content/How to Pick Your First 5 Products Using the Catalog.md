**POSTABLE CAPTION (Instagram / TikTok)**

Pick your first 5 products in minutes by letting catalog filters do the narrowing.

Scrolling a catalog of hundreds of products is how people stall for a week and pick nothing. The fix is to filter in a fixed order so the choices shrink fast.

Start with the niche filter so every product speaks to one buyer instead of a random mix. Add a price filter to keep unit cost where it makes sense. Many products personalize under fifteen dollars, which leaves room between cost and retail.

Then set the print method filter so your designs match what each product can actually hold. With a catalog this deep, the filters matter far more than scrolling.

Finally, choose five that share a theme so they read like a small store, not five strays. You leave with a focused shortlist that already looks like a brand instead of a random grab bag.

Save this and shortlist five products in one niche today.
