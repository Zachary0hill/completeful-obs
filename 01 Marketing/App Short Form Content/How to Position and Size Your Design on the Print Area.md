**POSTABLE CAPTION (Instagram / TikTok)**

Most sellers think the design wins the sale. Placement on the print area is what actually does.

A great design placed badly still looks amateur, and a simple design placed well can look premium. Placement is the quiet skill that turns a draft into a product people actually buy.

In the Design Editor your product shows the print area as a clear frame. That frame is the exact space your design can live in. I drag my artwork to center it, then pull a corner to size it so it sits comfortably inside the area without crowding the edges.

Keeping the design inside that frame is what avoids the cut off surprises that ruin a print. The whole time, the live preview updates with every move, so I stop when the balance feels right and confirm it before I save.
