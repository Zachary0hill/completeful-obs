**KEY POINTS**
1. The Earnings page syncs your Etsy ledger, so revenue and fees sit in one view instead of two tabs.
2. Filter by date to compare a slow week against a launch week and see what really moved.
3. Filter by account to separate two stores and stop guessing which one carries the profit.
4. Most products personalize at a unit price under $15, so margin is easy to read line by line.
5. Read net, not gross. The number that matters is what lands after fulfillment and fees.

**POSTABLE CAPTION (Instagram / TikTok)**

Stop reading gross sales. Your Earnings page shows what you actually keep.

Most sellers stare at gross revenue and feel busy, then the fees and costs show up later and the month feels smaller than expected. The number that pays you is net, not gross.

Open your Earnings page and turn on Etsy ledger sync so revenue and fees sit in one view instead of two tabs. Filter by date to compare a slow week against a launch week and you will see what actually moved the needle. Filter by account to separate two stores so you stop guessing which one carries the profit.

Then read each line against your unit cost. Most products personalize at a unit price under fifteen dollars, so margin is easy to read product by product. You will spot the listing that looks busy but barely clears, and the quiet one that pays.

Once you read net by line, you stop reacting to vanity numbers and start running the store like an operator.

Save this so your next Earnings review takes five minutes, not fifty.
