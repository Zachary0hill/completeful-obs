**POSTABLE CAPTION (Instagram / TikTok)**

Stop pricing by feel. Read your margin before you ever list the product.

Most sellers set a price by feel and only learn the real margin after the sale clears. By then it is too late to fix, and a thin product is already live in your shop.

Do the check first. The Catalog in Completeful shows the unit cost, and most products personalize for under fifteen dollars a unit, so you can plan your price with real numbers. Set your sale price against that cost and read the gap, and your margin is right there before you publish.

Once your listings go live, the Earnings page tracks the actual results. It syncs your Etsy ledger and filters by date and account, so you see what is really landing in your pocket. You price from the cost, not from a hunch, and you adjust with data instead of guesswork.

Save this and run the margin check before you publish.
