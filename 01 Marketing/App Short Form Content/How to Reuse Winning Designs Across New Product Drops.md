**POSTABLE CAPTION (Instagram / TikTok)**

Stop rebuilding a winning design from scratch. Reuse it and the next drop is faster.

Sellers find a winner, then rebuild it from zero for the next product and lose all the momentum. A proven design should be an asset you carry forward, not a one-time effort.

Save your winning design as a Design Template so it is ready to reuse the moment you need it. Store it in a My Designs folder and use search to find it fast, even months later when your library has grown.

Next drop, apply the template to a new catalog product in seconds. Adjust the placement for that product's print method so the design sits right on the new surface, then publish.

When you want scale, repeat the same template across more Catalog products so the winner spreads across the whole drop. One proven design becomes a whole new drop in a fraction of the time it took to make the first one.

Save this and turn your best design into a reusable template.
