**POSTABLE CAPTION (Instagram / TikTok)**

Stop picking products by looks. Read the catalog by unit cost to spot real margin.

Most sellers pick whatever looks cool, then find the margin is thin once fees come out. The product that pays is the one where the numbers work before you ever design it.

Open the Catalog and filter by price first so you see unit cost before you fall for a design. Note the products that personalize under fifteen dollars, because that leaves room between cost and retail.

Then filter by niche and ask which of those buyers will pay a premium to personalize an item. Personalization raises perceived value, and that is where the margin actually lives. The overlap between a low unit cost and a willing-to-spend niche is your shortlist.

Choose products from that overlap and you end up with items that look good and actually pay, instead of pretty listings that barely clear.

Save this and filter the catalog by price to find your margin.
