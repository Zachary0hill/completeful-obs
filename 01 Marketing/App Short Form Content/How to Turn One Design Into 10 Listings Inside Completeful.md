**POSTABLE CAPTION (Instagram / TikTok)**

Turn one design that sells into 10 listings without redrawing anything.

Most sellers stop at one product when a proven design could carry a whole line. If the artwork already converts, you are leaving listings on the table.

Here is how I spread it. Save your artwork in My Designs in Completeful so you can reuse it across several products. Then browse the Catalog and pick items that fit the same design, filtering by print method so the art reproduces well on each one.

Once your products are selected, run the Mockup generator to get photos for every new product. Now you have a full set ready to publish. More listings from one design means more chances to sell, and you never touched the artwork again. One design, ten listings, same afternoon.

Save this and apply your best design across the Catalog.
