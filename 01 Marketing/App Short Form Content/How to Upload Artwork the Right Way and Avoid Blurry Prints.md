**HOOK**
*Sharp prints start with the upload, and a quick check inside Completeful saves you from blurry products and unhappy buyers.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Clean artwork protects your reputation and your reviews.
2. Higher resolution files give you more room to size up without softness.
3. You upload directly into the Designer Tool on your chosen product.
4. The preview is your warning system if a file is too small for the area.
5. AI Upscale is there when a file needs more resolution before printing.

**CALL TO ACTION**
Upload your artwork in the Completeful Designer Tool and check the preview before you publish.
