**POSTABLE CAPTION (Instagram / TikTok)**

4 Design Editor moves that are enough to build a clean product from day one.

The editor can look busy when you first open it, but confidence comes from knowing you only need a handful of controls. Here are the four I use every time.

First I choose the product and variant, because the editor builds around that choice. Second I upload my artwork and it drops straight onto the print area. Third I drag to position it and pull the corners to resize, which is all the placement most products need.

Fourth I keep an eye on the live preview, which shows me the result the whole way through, so I never save something I have not actually seen. When it looks right, I save it to My Designs. That is the core loop. Choose, upload, place, preview. Once those four moves are muscle memory, every product you build after this one gets faster, and the editor stops feeling intimidating.
