**POSTABLE CAPTION (Instagram / TikTok)**

Stop copying order details by hand. Connect Etsy once and the first order syncs itself.

The slow way is copying order details between Etsy and your fulfillment one line at a time, which is where mistakes and delays creep in. Connecting once removes that step for good.

Connect Etsy in Sales Channels so order syncing pulls orders in for you automatically. Before any sale lands, fund your Wallet so the synced order is not held on payment when it arrives. Then publish a product to My Products so there is something ready to sell.

Now watch the Order Dashboard as the first order lands and routes to production on its own. There is no copy and paste between tabs, and nothing waits on a manual step from you.

Finally, confirm the ledger flows to your Earnings page so your numbers stay clean and you can read profit later. From sale to fulfillment, the whole path runs hands-free from day one.

Save this and connect Etsy before your next listing goes live.
