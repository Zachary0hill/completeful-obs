**POSTABLE CAPTION (Instagram / TikTok)**

Your fulfillment partner is usually a faceless address. This one is a real building you could visit.

When you sell online, the company making your products is often a mystery. A logo, maybe a country, and that is it. In most print-on-demand setups you never know who actually makes your orders, and that distance makes problems hard to solve and trust hard to build.

Completeful is the opposite of that. It is one physical place, a 228,000 sq ft facility in Lafayette, Louisiana, full of fiber lasers, UV printers, engraving and embroidery machines, and the people running them. Not a network of strangers.

Your customer's order is made here and shipped with fast domestic fulfillment. This is a real building you could literally drive to. When the maker is a real, knowable place, you can vouch for your products honestly and fix things fast when you need to.

Meet the maker at completeful.com.
