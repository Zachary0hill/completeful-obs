**HOOK**
*Multi-design lets you turn one product into several offers without building each one from scratch.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Multi-design places more than one design on a single product setup.
2. You expand a listing into variants without rebuilding the whole product.
3. Pair it with Design Templates so each placement starts from a saved layout.
4. Keep it on brand by pulling type and color from your Brand Kit.
5. Preview every version in the Mockup Generator before you publish.

**CALL TO ACTION**
Open Multi-design and add a second placement to one product you already sell.
