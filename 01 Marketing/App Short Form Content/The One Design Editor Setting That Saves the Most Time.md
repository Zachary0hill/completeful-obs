**POSTABLE CAPTION (Instagram / TikTok)**

Stop rebuilding the same layout on every product. One editor setting fixes it.

Most sellers nudge the same text box and resize the same graphic on every single product. It feels like work, but it is just rework you already did last week.

Here is the setting that changes it. In the Design Editor, finish one clean layout, then save it as a reusable Design Template. The template keeps your text placement, sizing, and spacing locked in, so every new design starts from a finished structure.

From there you only swap the artwork. Templates also pair with Custom Fonts, so your type carries over automatically and nothing drifts. Less rework means more listings live in the same amount of time, and the layout you perfected once keeps paying off on every product after it.
