**POSTABLE CAPTION (Instagram / TikTok)**

Stop designing products first. Connect your store first and your setup pays off faster.

Most people design a product, then scramble to connect a store after a sale comes in. That order is backwards, and it is why the first order feels chaotic.

Start by connecting your store under Sales Channels so order syncing is live from day one. It works with Etsy, Shopify, and TikTok Shop, which means a sale can route to production without a manual step from you. Next, set up your Wallet so orders never stall on payment later.

Then save a Design Template and your Custom Fonts so every design stays consistent and the store reads as one brand. Only after that do you pick products from the Catalog, design them, and publish to My Products.

With no minimums and a free start, you can have a working pipeline before you spend a dollar. Connect, fund, set your template, then build, and your first sale fulfills itself.
