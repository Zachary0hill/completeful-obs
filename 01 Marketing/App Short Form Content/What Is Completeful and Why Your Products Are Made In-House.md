**POSTABLE CAPTION (Instagram / TikTok)**

Myth: print-on-demand products come from a mystery factory overseas. Here one company makes it all.

I help people sell custom products without ever touching a factory, and the first thing I clear up is where their items actually come from. Most print-on-demand looks like a black box. An order goes in, a finished mug or shirt shows up at a customer's door, and nobody can tell you who made it.

Completeful is the company on the other side of that black box. We are a print-on-demand and fulfillment maker, and everything happens in-house at a 228,000 sq ft facility in Lafayette, Louisiana. The split is simple. You design and sell it, we create and fulfill it. That means no factory hunting, no warehouse lease, and no inventory sitting in your spare room.

In-house also changes the quality story. When printing, engraving, and embroidery all live under one roof, the same team controls the standard on every order. It is made and shipped in-house in the USA with no overseas transit, and you can start with no order minimums, so one product is a perfectly fine place to begin.

Follow for how print-on-demand actually works behind the scenes.
