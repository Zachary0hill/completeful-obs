**POSTABLE CAPTION (Instagram / TikTok)**

Myth: your Etsy orders ship from a mystery warehouse. Here is the real building they come from.

When you sell a custom product on Etsy, it usually feels like magic. An order comes in, and somehow a finished item shows up at a customer's door. Most sellers never see the place that actually makes their products.

Behind that magic is a real building. Completeful produces orders in-house at a 228,000 sq ft facility in Lafayette, Louisiana. Your Etsy shop connects with automatic order syncing, so when a sale happens the order lands in production without you forwarding anything. Items are made and shipped in-house in the USA with no overseas transit, which means you always know exactly who makes your products and where.

That visibility is the part sellers tell me they value most. You get to stay focused on listings, photos, and the parts of the shop you control, while production happens for you in the background. No factory hunting, no guessing, no mystery warehouse on another continent.

Follow for how Etsy print-on-demand actually works.

#EtsySeller #PrintOnDemand #PODFulfillment #MadeInUSA
