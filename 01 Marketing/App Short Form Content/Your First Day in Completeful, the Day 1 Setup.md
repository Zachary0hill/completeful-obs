**HOOK**
*Here is my screen on Day 1 in Completeful, set up start to finish.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Save your Custom Fonts and a Design Template first so every design stays consistent.
2. Browse the Catalog and pick one product to start.
3. Design it in the Design Editor using a Design Template.
4. Generate a mockup in the Mockup generator and save to My Designs.
5. Publish to My Products and connect a store under Sales Channels so orders sync with fast domestic fulfillment.

**CALL TO ACTION**
Start your store at completeful.com.
