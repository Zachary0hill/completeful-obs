**KEY POINTS**
1. Pick a niche and your first products from the Catalog.
2. Build a small lineup in the Design Editor with Custom Fonts and a Design Template.
3. Generate mockups in the Mockup generator and organize them in My Designs.
4. Place a Sample Order so you see the quality before customers do.
5. Publish to My Products and connect a store under Sales Channels so orders sync with fast domestic fulfillment.

Start your store at completeful.com.
