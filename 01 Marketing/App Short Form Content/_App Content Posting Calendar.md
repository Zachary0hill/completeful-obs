# App Content Posting Calendar

A 25-week rollout that sequences the 99 core files (Buckets A to C) in `App Short Form Content` while keeping the whole account inside Completeful's content-mix rules. The 12 Bucket D use-case walkthroughs slot in as needed during launches and seasonal pushes. Every Reel is shot 9:16 so it triple-posts as Instagram Reel, TikTok, and YouTube Short. Captions are ready in each file under POSTABLE CAPTION.

## Mix and cadence rules this calendar respects
- Cadence: 6 short-form posts per week per the Channel Plan (5 to 7 is the stated range).
- Content type mix target: about 70% educational, 20% behind the scenes or culture, 10% promotional. Never exceed 20% promotional.
- App tutorials (Bucket A) and tips (Bucket B) count as EDUCATIONAL. Awareness clips (Bucket C) are split: facility, process, and where-it-is-made angles count as BEHIND THE SCENES; brand and offer angles count as PROMOTIONAL.
- Two of the six weekly slots are non-app, pulled from the existing `Content Month` drafts and the footage library, so the account never becomes 100% app content. This is what holds the 70/20/10.
- One CTA per post. Cold-discovery posts lean save, share, and follow. Warm tutorials use comment-to-DM triggers.

## Weekly template (6 posts)
| Slot | Day | Source | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mon | App tutorial (Bucket A) | Educational |
| 2 | Tue | App tip (Bucket B) | Educational |
| 3 | Wed | App awareness (Bucket C) | BTS or Promotional |
| 4 | Thu | Non-app educational (Content Month draft) | Educational |
| 5 | Fri | App tutorial (Bucket A) | Educational |
| 6 | Sat | Non-app BTS footage or seller social proof (footage library) | BTS / Social proof |

Resulting weekly type mix: about 67% educational, 17% BTS, 16% promotional. Inside the rules.

Note on Bucket B weeks: a few weeks below run two tips and one tutorial (or two awareness and one tutorial) to consume the full library evenly. The Mon/Tue/Wed/Fri app slots still total four per week.

## How to sustain after Week 25
- The 99 core app videos are now placed once each, with the 12 Bucket D walkthroughs held in reserve for launches and seasonal pushes. After the cycle, repost the top performers (judge by saves and DM sends, the signals your Instagram Writing Style weights highest) and refresh hooks on the rest.
- Keep the 6-slot weekly template. Four app slots plus two non-app slots is what keeps promotional under 20%.
- During a product launch, swap a Wednesday awareness slot for the relevant Product Showcase. During Q4, lean the Tuesday tip slot toward seasonal-drop and margin tips.
