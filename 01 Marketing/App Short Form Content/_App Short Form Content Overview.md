# Completeful App — Short Form Content (111 Outlines)

Short-form video outlines for showing people how to use the Completeful app, sharing tips and tricks, and moving an unaware viewer to an intrigued user. Same format as `01 Marketing/Content Month`: each file has HOOK, KEY POINTS, CALL TO ACTION, and a 60-SECOND REEL SCRIPT.

## Voice rules (non-negotiable)
- No emojis, no dashes, no hype, no ALL CAPS.
- Hook lands in the first 125 characters.
- One CTA per video. Max 5 hashtags.
- Lead with the seller benefit, not the feature spec.
- CTAs point to completeful.com or the Completeful app.

## Grounding facts used across these scripts
- "You Design and Sell It, We Create and Fulfill It."
- In-house manufacturing in Lafayette, Louisiana. 228,000 sq ft facility. Fast domestic fulfillment, no overseas transit.
- No order minimums. Start free. 500 to 900+ product catalog (the 900+ figure counts every color and size variant; the catalog holds roughly 123 distinct products across accessories, drinkware, home, and apparel).
- Print methods: laser engraving, laser etching, UV printing, sublimation, embroidery.
- Sales channels that connect for automatic order syncing: Etsy and Shopify (live) and TikTok Shop (rolling out). WooCommerce is not supported.
- Core app features, by their real in-app names: Catalog, Design Editor (Konva canvas), Mockup generator (lifestyle Scenes), Infographics, Design Templates ("Start with a curated design"), My Designs with folders and search, Custom Fonts (upload your own), AI Onboarding (guided chat that helps build your first listing), AI Assistant (Completeful AI in-app help), AI Upscale / Edit, My Products, Kits (Beta) for product bundles, My Listings (publish to Etsy, Shopify, TikTok Shop), Sample Orders ("Place an order" on your own product), Wallet and payment methods, Earnings (Etsy ledger sync only), Orders (the order dashboard), and Sales Channels for connecting stores.
- Note: there is no standalone "Tools tab," no feature literally named "AI Product Creator," and no live "Brand Kit" feature (the Branding page is a coming-soon placeholder). Brand consistency is achieved with Custom Fonts, Design Templates, and reusable saved designs.
