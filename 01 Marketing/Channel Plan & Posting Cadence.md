# Channel Plan & Posting Cadence

This document defines the role of each channel in Completeful's marketing strategy, the content types that belong on each platform, and the suggested weekly posting cadence. All content production runs through the pipeline described in [[Content Workflow & Automation Pipeline]].

---

## Channel Overview

### Instagram (@completeful)

**Role:** Brand-building, visual education, and community.
Instagram is Completeful's primary visual channel. The audience here skews toward POD sellers who are already engaged in the creator-commerce space and actively follow brands that help them grow. The platform rewards consistent posting, Reels performance, and saves — all of which the strategy directly targets.

**Content types:**
- Educational carousels (top products by niche, pricing tips, platform comparisons)
- Reels: quick tutorials, catalog showcases, before/after design-to-product demos
- Static product mockup posts with educational captions
- Social proof: seller features, milestones, quotes

**Suggested cadence:** 5–7 posts per week (mix of Reels and carousels, 1–2 static)

---

### TikTok (@completeful)

**Role:** Top-of-funnel discovery, creator education, and TikTok Shop positioning.
TikTok is Completeful's highest-reach potential channel and the most direct connection to the TikTok Shop seller audience. Content here is native, informal in pacing, and education-heavy. The goal is discoverability — reaching sellers who do not yet know Completeful exists.

**Content types:**
- How-to videos: starting a POD store, setting up TikTok Shop, scaling an Etsy store
- Behind-the-scenes of fulfillment and production in Lafayette
- Product spotlights and catalog reveals
- Trend-reactive content where a POD angle is authentic

**Suggested cadence:** 5–7 posts per week. Frequency matters more on TikTok than any other platform. Consistency beats perfection.

---

### X / Twitter (@completefultech)

**Role:** Industry commentary, real-time engagement, and community voice.
X is used for shorter-form commentary, sharing content and links to the blog or YouTube, and engaging in e-commerce and POD conversations. The audience here is smaller but typically more technically-engaged and includes sellers, developers, and e-commerce enthusiasts.

**Content types:**
- Single-insight posts with a business or product angle
- Blog or YouTube link shares with a one-line hook
- Reactions to platform news (Etsy algorithm changes, TikTok Shop updates, Shopify announcements)
- Thread-format how-tos where the topic warrants depth

**Suggested cadence:** 3–5 posts per week, including at least 1 link share to owned content.

---

### LinkedIn (/company/completeful)

**Role:** B2B credibility, industry authority, and operator-to-operator content.
LinkedIn reaches a different segment of the audience: serious e-commerce operators, brand founders, people evaluating fulfillment partners at a business level. Content here is more analytical and business-oriented than TikTok or Instagram.

**Content types:**
- Industry data and POD market insights
- Case studies and seller success narratives framed as business stories
- Product and platform updates (new integrations, catalog expansions) framed for a business audience
- Behind-the-scenes of Completeful's operations (the 228,000 sq ft facility, manufacturing capabilities)

**Suggested cadence:** 3 posts per week.

---

### YouTube (@Completeful)

**Role:** Long-form education, SEO, and deep-value content that builds lasting authority.
YouTube is the long game. A library of well-titled tutorial videos creates compounding organic traffic over months and years. A seller who finds a Completeful YouTube tutorial and gets value from it is a qualified, warmed prospect.

**Content types:**
- Full tutorials: "How to set up TikTok Shop with a POD fulfillment partner," "How to price POD products for profit on Etsy"
- Platform walkthroughs of the Completeful app
- Catalog deep-dives: what products work for what niches
- Live or recorded Q&A / seller education sessions

**Suggested cadence:** 1–2 videos per week. Consistency matters; frequency is secondary to quality and keyword targeting.

---

### Blog (completeful.com/blog)

**Role:** SEO foundation, owned media, and long-form content that supports all other channels.
The blog is the anchor for long-form content. Blog posts produced by the Long-Form Writer agent drive organic search traffic, serve as the source material for Atomizer agent repurposing across social channels, and build the kind of deep topical authority that social posts alone cannot achieve.

**Content types:**
- How-to guides: starting a POD business, niche selection, platform comparisons
- Product category explainers
- SEO-targeted articles around terms like "print on demand fulfillment," "Etsy POD supplier," "TikTok Shop fulfillment"
- Updates on new integrations, catalog additions, or platform news

**Suggested cadence:** 2–3 posts per week, with SEO keyword targeting driving topic selection.

---

## Content Cross-Posting Policy

Content is adapted for each platform, not simply reposted. The Formatter agent handles platform-specific adjustments. Captions written for LinkedIn are not appropriate for TikTok; a Reel script is not a YouTube tutorial. The Atomizer agent is responsible for repurposing a piece of long-form content (YouTube video or blog post) into multiple short-form formats — this is the right model for cross-posting, not copy-paste.
