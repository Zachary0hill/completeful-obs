## How to Use This Backlog

Pull ideas from here into the Monthly Planning Template when filling the weekly grid. Mark ideas with a status when they move into production. Add new ideas at the bottom of the relevant category. Each idea includes a one-line angle and a target channel to accelerate triage during planning.

## Educational / How-To

1. **How POD fulfillment actually works**
   Angle: Demystify the end-to-end process from order placed on Etsy to box on a customer's doorstep, positioning Completeful as the silent partner behind the scenes.
   Channel: YouTube (long-form) + IG Carousel repurpose

2. **What "no order minimums" really means for your cash flow**
   Angle: Show a seller the math: listing a new design costs $0 upfront, so testing products is pure upside with no warehouse risk.
   Channel: TikTok, IG Reel

3. **How to price your POD products for profit, not just sales**
   Angle: Walk through a real pricing formula using Completeful's transparent pricing as the cost baseline, targeting Etsy and TikTok Shop sellers.
   Channel: Blog (long-form) + LinkedIn Article

4. **3 things to check before choosing a POD supplier**
   Angle: Catalog size, fulfillment speed, and pricing transparency — Completeful addresses all three; let the framework sell the solution.
   Channel: IG Carousel (7-slide), X Thread

5. **How US-based manufacturing affects your delivery times and reviews**
   Angle: Concrete example comparing domestic vs overseas fulfillment timelines and how faster shipping drives repeat buyers on Etsy.
   Channel: TikTok, IG Reel

6. **Setting up your first Shopify store with POD from day one**
   Angle: Step-by-step beginner walkthrough covering product setup, mockups, and connecting fulfillment.
   Channel: YouTube, Blog

7. **What is 3PL and why does it matter for growing sellers?**
   Angle: Plain-language explainer positioning Completeful's 3PL offering as the natural next step after a seller outgrows standard POD.
   Channel: Blog, LinkedIn
