## How to Use This Template

When a content idea surfaces, copy the fields below into a new note or a Notion card and fill them in before the idea slips away. The goal is to capture enough context that anyone picking it up later can act on it without needing to track down the originator. Fields marked with an asterisk are required; others are optional but strongly recommended.

---

## Idea Capture Fields

```
Working Title*:
(A short, descriptive title — not necessarily the final headline. Make it clear enough that future-you knows what this idea is about at a glance.)

Hook*:
(The opening line or the core angle that makes someone stop scrolling. Write it as if you were going to use it. One or two sentences.)

Format*:
Options: Short-form Reel | 7-Slide Carousel | Static Graphic | Infographic | Long-form Video | YouTube Short | Blog Post | X Thread | LinkedIn Article | TikTok Native Video

Target Channel*:
Options: Instagram | TikTok | X | LinkedIn | YouTube | Blog | Multi-platform

Content Pillar*:
Options: Education | Product Showcase | Seller Success | Behind the Scenes | Platform Tips | Campaign | Seasonal

CTA:
(What do you want the viewer to do after engaging? Examples: Follow for more seller tips. Link in bio to browse the full catalog. Comment your best-selling product category.)

Visual Direction:
(What should this look like? Examples: lifestyle mockup with Nano Banana Pro, screen recording of dashboard, B-roll from production floor, talking-head video.)

Inspiration Source:
(Where did this idea come from? Examples: customer DM, competitor post, trending audio, team brainstorm, comment section, keyword research.)

Notes:
(Anything else relevant — timing, dependencies, linked ideas, data or stats to research.)

Captured By:
Captured On:
Status: Backlog
```

---

## Example Filled Entry

```
Working Title: No Order Minimums Means Zero Risk Testing

Hook: Most sellers are sitting on product ideas they are afraid to test because they don't want to buy inventory that might not sell. There is a better way.

Format: Short-form Reel

Target Channel: TikTok

Content Pillar: Education

CTA: Drop the product category you have been afraid to test in the comments.

Visual Direction: Talking head or text-on-screen with B-roll of product variety from the Completeful catalog. Fast cuts. On-screen text reinforces each point.

Inspiration Source: Recurring question in Etsy seller Facebook groups about testing new niches without buying bulk inventory.

Notes: Pair with a carousel repurpose for Instagram. Could also become a blog section inside a "getting started with POD" post.

Captured By: Zach
Captured On: 2025-07-15
Status: Backlog
```
