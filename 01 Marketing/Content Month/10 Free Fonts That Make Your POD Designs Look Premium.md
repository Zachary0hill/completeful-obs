**HOOK**
*The font is the difference between a $12 design and a $30 one. Here are 10 free fonts that instantly make your POD look premium.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Pair a bold display font (like Anton or Bebas Neue) with a clean sans for contrast that reads premium
2. For minimalist quote tees, use a thin serif like Cormorant or Playfair Display, luxury brands lean serif for a reason
3. Hand-script fonts (Sail, Great Vibes) sell in the mom and wedding niches, but keep them to a single line so they stay legible
4. Always check the license, fonts from Google Fonts and Fontshare are free for commercial print; "free for personal use" fonts will get your listings pulled
5. Limit any one design to two fonts max, amateur designs are spotted instantly by font soup with five mismatched typefaces

**CALL TO ACTION**
Great fonts make designs sell, once they look premium, print and ship them with Completeful
