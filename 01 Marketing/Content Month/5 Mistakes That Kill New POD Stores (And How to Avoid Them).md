**Full breakdown**

**HOOK:**
"Your print-on-demand store isn't failing because of the market. It's failing because you're making at least one of these five mistakes and you probably don't even realize it."

**LISTICLE:**

**1. Your niche is way too broad**
Selling "funny t-shirts" to everyone is selling to no one. The stores that win pick a specific audience, cat-loving nurses, gym dads, retired teachers, and speak directly to them. Narrow down and watch your conversions go up.

**2. Your mockups look like it's 2016**
If you're still using flat-lay blanks with no lifestyle context, customers are scrolling right past you. Invest in realistic, high-quality mockups that make people picture themselves wearing or using the product. First impressions sell.

**3. You're completely ignoring SEO**
You designed a great shirt but titled it "Cool Design 03." Nobody is searching for that. Your titles, tags, and descriptions should be loaded with keywords your target customer is actually typing into the search bar.

**4. Your pricing is bleeding you dry**
You priced low to "get sales" but forgot about platform fees, shipping, ad costs, and returns. Now you're busy but broke. Price for profit, not just volume. Every sale should put real money in your pocket.

**5. You're choosing the wrong products**
Not every product is worth selling. That 47th mug design doesn't matter if your audience buys hoodies. Look at what your niche actually spends money on and double down on those products instead of spreading yourself thin.
