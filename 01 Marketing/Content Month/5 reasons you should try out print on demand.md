**HOOK**
*No inventory, no upfront cash, no warehouse. Print-on-demand is the lowest-risk way to start an online business in 2026.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Zero upfront inventory — products are only made after a customer pays, so you never buy stock you can't sell
2. Near-zero startup cost — you can launch a store for free and pay nothing until you make a sale
3. You design once and sell forever — one good design can run across mugs, tees, hoodies, and posters
4. No packing or shipping — your supplier prints, packs, and ships under your brand while you focus on designs and marketing
5. Test ideas with no risk — list ten concepts, see what sells, and double down without ever gambling on inventory
