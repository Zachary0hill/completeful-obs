**HOOK**
*A teacher built a side store around one niche, working evenings and weekends.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. A teacher who sells on Etsy started with a niche they understood: classroom themes.
2. They designed for one audience instead of trying to please everyone.
3. Print-on-demand meant no upfront stock and no risk to their main income.
4. Orders fit around grading and lesson planning, not the other way around.
5. Completeful handled production and shipping, so the side store stayed manageable.
