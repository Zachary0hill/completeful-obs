**WHY THIS ANGLE IS WORTH KEEPING**
The existing back-to-school tee notes all target teachers. This targets the parent-child pair at the photo moment, which is a distinct buyer, a distinct emotional trigger, and a built-in multi-unit order. If tees ever land in the catalog, this is a stronger angle than another grade-level teacher design, because the teacher tee market is saturated and the matching-set market is not.

**CATALOG EXPANSION CASE**
- Apparel is the single largest POD category industry-wide and Completeful carries two products in it
- Back to school is apparel-heavy, so the catalog is structurally weak in exactly the season this batch targets
- Every competitor platform carries tees, making this the most visible gap in a seller comparison
- This is the strategic story underneath the whole Back to School 2026 batch: eleven good ideas exist because the catalog is deep in engraving and embroidery, and four ideas are blocked because it is not deep in apparel
