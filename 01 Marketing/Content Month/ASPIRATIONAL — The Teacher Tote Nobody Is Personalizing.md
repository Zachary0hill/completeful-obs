> [!warning] This note is blocked
> Completeful does not carry a tote bag. This idea is retained as demand signal for a catalog expansion conversation, not as publishable content. Shipping it would direct sellers to list a product that cannot be fulfilled.
>
> **Nearest catalog substitutes:** Duffel Bag ($18.50, embroidery) or Fanny Pack ($8.50, UV). Neither reads as a teacher tote.

**HOOK**
*A teacher carries the same bag 180 days a year. Nobody has put her name on it.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. The tote is the single most visible teacher accessory and it is always generic
2. Embroidered name and subject turns a commodity bag into a keepsake
3. Teacher totes are both a self-purchase and a gift purchase, which is rare
4. Grade-team bulk orders apply, same as hats
5. Year-round product with an August spike

**WHY THIS IS HERE**
Back to school is an apparel-heavy and bag-heavy season across the entire POD industry. The Completeful catalog is thin in exactly those categories: two apparel products total, and no tote. This note exists to make that gap concrete and costed rather than abstract. If apparel and bags expand, this is a first-week script.

**CATALOG EXPANSION CASE**
- Teacher niche has three annual spikes: August, May, December
- Tote is a self-purchase and a gift purchase simultaneously
- Embroidery capacity already exists in-house at 16 machines, so a tote SKU would use idle-adjacent capability rather than new equipment
- Competing POD platforms carry totes as standard, so the gap is visible to any seller comparison shopping
