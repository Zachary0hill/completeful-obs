*A product listing is a dead end. A funnel turns one visitor into a repeat customer. Here's the difference.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Top of funnel: TikTok/Reels content drives awareness and traffic
2. Middle of funnel: product page with lifestyle photos, reviews, and urgency
3. Bottom of funnel: checkout with upsell/cross-sell recommendations
4. Post-purchase: thank you email with discount code for next order
5. Retention: monthly email with new designs keeps customers coming back
