**KEY POINTS**
1. The classic bundle: tee + mug at 15% off individual pricing
2. The gift set: 3 products in themed packaging ("Dog Mom Gift Box")
3. Free shipping threshold: set it just above your average single product price
4. Listing bundles as separate SKUs gives you more Etsy search surface area
