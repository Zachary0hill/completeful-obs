**HOOK**
*A $25 t-shirt nets you $8. A canvas print nets you $30+, and the buyer thinks they got a steal. Wall art is the AOV upgrade hiding in your catalog.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Canvas and framed prints retail for $40-90, with margins that dwarf apparel on a single sale
2. Wall art is perceived as decor, not merch, buyers don't price-compare it the way they do t-shirts
3. Your existing designs already work: typography quotes, line art, and map prints convert straight onto canvas
4. Best niches are home, family, faith, and nursery, anywhere people decorate a room they care about
5. Offer multiple sizes and framed vs. unframed options so one design becomes four price points (and a built-in upsell)
