# Completeful — 4-Week Content Plan (Jun 30 – Jul 27, 2026)

Built from the renamed Completeful footage library + the vault's brand system
([[Content Pillars & Categories]], [[Channel Plan & Posting Cadence]], [[Instagram Writing Style]], [[Hook Formulas Library]]).

## How this plan works
- **Backbone = your footage.** The reorganized clips give us a real, ownable Behind-the-Scenes / Manufacturing engine that overseas POD brands cannot match. Most reels below cut from clips that already exist and are scene-named.
- **Primary channels:** Instagram + TikTok (5 posts/week each). Every Reel is shot 9:16, so it triple-posts as IG Reel, TikTok, and YouTube Short. Carousels are IG-first, repurposed to X/LinkedIn.
- **Pillar mix this month (20 posts):** Education 30% (6) · Product Showcase 20% (4) · Seller Success 20% (4) · Behind the Scenes 15% (3) · Platform Tips 15% (3). Footage powers the Product Showcase, BTS, and Seller Success slots; Education/Tips slots reuse existing drafts in `01 Marketing/Content Month`.
- **Voice rules (non-negotiable):** no emojis, no dashes, no hype, no ALL CAPS. Hook lands in the first 125 characters. One CTA. Max 5 hashtags. Lead with the seller benefit, not the product spec.
- **Promotional ceiling:** never exceed 20% promotional. CTAs point to completeful.com or the app.

1. Pull the named clips listed per post from the library (names describe the shot, so no scrubbing).
2. Cut to 7 to 15 seconds, hook frame first, trending-audio optional.
3. Add on-screen hook text (matches the caption hook, under 125 characters).
4. Paste caption, set the one CTA, add the 5 hashtags.
