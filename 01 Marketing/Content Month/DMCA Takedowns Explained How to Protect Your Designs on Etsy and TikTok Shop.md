**HOOK**
*Someone just stole your bestselling design. Don't panic, here's exactly how to file a takedown on every major platform.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Etsy: use their Intellectual Property Report form (response in 24-72 hours)
2. TikTok Shop: submit through Seller Center's IP Infringement Report
3. Shopify: email their designated DMCA agent with evidence
4. Document first: screenshot the infringing listing with timestamps
5. Proactive protection: register your top designs with US Copyright Office ($45)
