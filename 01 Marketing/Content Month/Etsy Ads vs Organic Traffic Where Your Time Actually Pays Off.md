**KEY POINTS**
1. Organic SEO compounds, a well-optimized listing generates traffic for years
2. Ads give instant visibility but stop the moment you stop paying
3. Best strategy: optimize 100% organically first, then add ads to proven winners
4. Organic ROI timeline: 4-8 weeks to see results
5. Ad ROI timeline: same day, but only profitable on listings with strong CTR
