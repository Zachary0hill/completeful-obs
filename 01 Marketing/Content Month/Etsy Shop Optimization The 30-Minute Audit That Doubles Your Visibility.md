*Your Etsy shop has hidden problems killing your visibility. This 30-minute audit finds and fixes all of them.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Check your shop banner and logo (blurry or missing = instant trust killer)
2. Audit your first 3 listing photos (this is where buyers decide to click or scroll)
3. Review all 13 tags on your top 5 listings (are they long-tail or generic?)
4. Update your About section with your brand story and shipping info
5. Add shop policies (free shipping, returns), Etsy rewards complete profiles
