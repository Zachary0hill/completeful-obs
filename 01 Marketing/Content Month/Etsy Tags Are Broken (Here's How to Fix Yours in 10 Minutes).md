**HOOK**
*You're using all 13 tags but still not getting found. The problem isn't quantity, it's strategy.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Stop using single-word tags ("mug") and use long-tail phrases ("funny nurse mug gift")
2. Never repeat words that are already in your title
3. Use Etsy's search bar autocomplete to find real buyer search terms
4. Mix broad tags ("gift for mom") with specific tags ("golden retriever mom tee")
5. Update tags monthly based on seasonal search trends

**CALL TO ACTION**
Great tags drive traffic, great products close sales, list your POD products with Completeful
