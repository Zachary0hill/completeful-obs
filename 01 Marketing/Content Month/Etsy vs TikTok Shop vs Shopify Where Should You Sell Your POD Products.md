**KEY POINTS**
1. Etsy: built-in traffic, gifting audience, SEO-driven, fees add up
2. TikTok Shop: viral potential, younger audience, content-driven, fast scaling
3. Shopify: full brand control, higher margins, but you drive all traffic
4. Best starter platform: Etsy (for validation) + TikTok Shop (for growth)
5. When to add Shopify: once you hit $2K/month and want to own your audience
