*Funny tees, meme shirts, and fan-style art are quietly outselling "pretty" designs. Here's what our top sellers actually make.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Relatable beats beautiful, a dumb-funny one-liner tee outsells a gorgeous floral design because people buy the joke, not the art
2. Niche-specific humor wins: "funny nurse," "tired teacher," "dog mom energy", the more specific the inside joke, the higher the conversion
3. Lean into trend-driven memes fast, but design around the THEME (not the copyrighted character) to stay takedown-safe
4. Text-based designs are the cheapest to make and easiest to test, bold font, punchy phrase, done in minutes
5. Bundle the joke across products, same one-liner on a tee, mug, and tote turns one idea into three sales
