**KEY POINTS**
1. The seller began with one design they believed in, not a full store.
2. Print-on-demand let them place that design on many product types without buying inventory.
3. They tested which products the design sold best on before expanding.
4. The catalog grew product by product, guided by real orders.
5. Completeful handled fulfillment so the seller could focus on designs and listings.
