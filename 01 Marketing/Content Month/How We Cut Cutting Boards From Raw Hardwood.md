**HOOK**
*Raw hardwood goes in. A finished cutting board comes out. Here is every step in between.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Cutting boards start as raw hardwood, not a pre-made blank.
2. A miter saw and panel saw cut boards down to size.
3. A band saw can split a board to the right thickness.
4. CNC routers shape and detail the board.
5. A dedicated sanding room smooths every board before it ships in-house from the USA.
