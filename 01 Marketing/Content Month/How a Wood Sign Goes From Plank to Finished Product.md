**KEY POINTS**
1. A wood sign starts as a raw UV plank, cut to size on the panel saw.
2. The band saw can split the plank to the right thickness.
3. CNC routers shape the plank and add detail.
4. The sanding room smooths the surface so the print lands clean.
5. UV printers lay the design onto the plank, all made and shipped in-house in the USA.
