**HOOK**
*Analysis paralysis kills more POD businesses than bad designs. Use this framework to pick your first 5 products in 10 minutes.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Start with one apparel item (tee or sweatshirt) — it's the POD staple
2. Add one drinkware item (mug or tumbler) — lower price point, gift appeal
3. Add one bag (tote) — eco-friendly, universal, low shipping cost
4. Add one home item (poster or pillow) — different buyer, different niche angle
5. Add one seasonal item based on timing (tank for summer, hoodie for fall)

**CALL TO ACTION**
Browse all product categories at [completeful.com](http://completeful.com) and pick your first five
