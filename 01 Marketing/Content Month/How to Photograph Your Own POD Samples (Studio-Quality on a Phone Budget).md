## INSTAGRAM

**Caption:**
You don't need a DSLR, a studio, or a photographer to get product photos that actually convert.

Most POD sellers either skip real product photos entirely or spend hundreds getting them done. Neither is necessary. Your phone can do the job — if you know how to use it.

Here's the 5-step framework for studio-quality POD product photography on a phone budget:

1. NATURAL LIGHT IS EVERYTHING
Shoot next to a large window during the morning or late afternoon. Indirect light is your best friend — it's soft, even, and free. Avoid overhead room lighting and never use your phone flash. If the light is too harsh, hang a white sheet over the window to diffuse it.

2. DIY YOUR BACKDROP
A $3 sheet of white poster board from the dollar store works for flat lays. A clean wall or hung fabric works for lifestyle shots. Wrinkles and clutter kill photos — keep it minimal. Marble contact paper and wooden cutting boards are cheap texture options.

3. LOCK YOUR CAMERA SETTINGS
Open your phone camera and tap on the product to lock focus. Hold down to lock exposure. Shoot at 1x zoom — never pinch to zoom, it destroys quality. Turn off HDR if your photos look over-processed. Use a 2-second timer or a free Bluetooth remote to eliminate shake.

4. STYLE THE SHOT
Flat lay: Shoot directly overhead for t-shirts, mugs, stickers. On-body: Have someone wear the product in a natural setting. Add 1-2 small props that relate to the niche — a stethoscope for nurse merch, tools for trade workers. Less is more. The product is the star.

5. EDIT LIKE A PRO FOR FREE
Snapseed (free) or Lightroom Mobile (free) can transform your shots. Bump exposure slightly. Increase contrast. Fix white balance so whites actually look white. Sharpen just a touch. Don't over-filter — clean and accurate sells better than stylized.

Bonus tips:
- Shoot more than you think you need — 20+ angles per product
- Always shoot horizontal AND vertical for different platforms
- Batch your shoots — do all products in one session
- White background shots for listings, lifestyle shots for social

Your product photos are often the first thing a customer sees. They don't need to be perfect. They need to be REAL and CLEAR.

## FACEBOOK

**Caption:**
One of the biggest mistakes POD sellers make: using only mockups and never showing real product photos.

Mockups are fine for listings. But when it comes to social media, ads, and building trust with customers — real photos win every time. People want to see the actual product. The print quality. How it fits. How the colors look in real life.

The good news: you don't need expensive equipment to get great shots.

Here's what you actually need:

A phone (any smartphone made in the last 3-4 years works), a window with natural light, a clean backdrop (poster board, white sheet, or a blank wall), and a free editing app.

The process is simple:

Shoot near a window in the morning or late afternoon when the light is soft and indirect. Use poster board or fabric as your backdrop — keep it clean and minimal. Lock your phone's focus and exposure on the product, and always shoot at 1x zoom. Style the shot with 1-2 niche-relevant props. Then edit in Snapseed or Lightroom Mobile — adjust exposure, contrast, white balance, and add a touch of sharpening.

That's it. No studio. No photographer. No expensive gear.

A few extra tips that make a big difference:
- Shoot horizontal and vertical versions of every product
- Take way more shots than you think you need
- Batch all your products into one shoot session
- Use white backgrounds for marketplace listings and lifestyle setups for social content

Your photos don't need to be perfect. They just need to be real, clear, and well-lit. That alone puts you ahead of 90% of POD sellers who rely solely on mockups.
