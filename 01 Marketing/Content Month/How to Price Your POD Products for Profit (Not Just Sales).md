**LISTICLE:**

**1. Know your TRUE base cost**
Don't just look at the product cost. Factor in the platform fees, shipping, transaction fees, and any app subscriptions eating into your margin. That $12 tee actually costs you closer to $18.

**2. Stop using the "double it" method**
Doubling your base cost sounds simple, but simple doesn't mean profitable. A 2x markup on a $20 product leaves you razor-thin after ads and returns.

**3. Price for your PROFIT margin, not your sale price**
Decide how much profit you want per unit FIRST, then reverse-engineer the price. If you need $10 profit per sale, your price has to cover every cost plus that $10. Period.

**4. Factor in ad spend per unit**
If you're spending $500/month on ads and selling 50 units, that's $10 per unit in ad cost alone. Most sellers completely ignore this and wonder why revenue doesn't hit their bank account.

**5. Test perceived value, not just low prices**
Better mockups, stronger branding, and niche targeting let you charge more. Customers don't buy the cheapest product — they buy the one that feels worth it.
