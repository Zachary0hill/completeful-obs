**HOOK**
*Sales work — but most sellers discount too deep and end up losing money. Here's how to do it right.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Never discount more than 15-20% (deeper cuts train customers to wait for sales)
2. Run sales for 48-72 hours max to create real urgency
3. Bundle products instead of discounting singles (increases AOV)
4. Use free shipping thresholds instead of price cuts
5. Time sales around paydays, holidays, or seasonal transitions

**CALL TO ACTION**
