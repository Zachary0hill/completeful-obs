**HOOK**
*By the time a product is trending on YouTube, it's too late. Here's how to find trends 30-60 days early.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Monitor Etsy's trending searches weekly (not monthly)
2. Watch TikTok hashtags under 10M views, that's early-stage trending
3. Use Google Trends to compare search volume trajectory
4. Check Amazon Movers & Shakers in relevant categories
5. Follow niche subreddits for emerging cultural moments
