**HOOK**
*Your best marketing copy is already written, by your customers. Here's how to turn reviews into sales machines.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Screenshot 5-star reviews and post them as Instagram/TikTok content
2. Pull the best quote from a review and add it to your product description
3. Create a "Wall of Love" highlight on Instagram with customer testimonials
4. Use review keywords in your Etsy tags (customers use natural search language)
5. Respond to every review publicly, potential buyers read your responses

**CALL TO ACTION**
Great products earn great reviews, Completeful's quality speaks for itself

(0:00–0:03) HOOK: Your best marketing copy is already written, by your customers.
