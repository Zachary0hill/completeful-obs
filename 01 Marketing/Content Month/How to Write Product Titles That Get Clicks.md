**HOOK**
*Your product is great. Your title is killing it. The first few words decide whether anyone ever clicks.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Front-load your most important keyword, search engines and buyers both weight the first words heaviest, so lead with what people actually type
2. Write for a human, not a keyword dump, "Personalized Dog Mom Mug, Funny Gift for Pet Lovers" beats "Mug Dog Mom Custom Gift Pet Coffee Cup Ceramic"
3. Stack three layers: product + who it's for + occasion ("Funny Nurse Tumbler, Gift for Night Shift RN, Graduation Present")
4. Match the platform, Etsy rewards long, keyword-rich titles; TikTok Shop and Shopify reward short, punchy, scannable ones
5. Test and swap underperformers monthly; if a listing gets impressions but no clicks, the title is the problem, not the product

**CALL TO ACTION**
A great title earns the click, Completeful prints and ships the product that closes the sale
