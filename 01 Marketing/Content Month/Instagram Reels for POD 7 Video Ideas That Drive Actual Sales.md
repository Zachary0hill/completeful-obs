**HOOK**
*Instagram isn't dead for POD, you're just posting the wrong content. These 7 Reel formats convert followers to buyers.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. "New in shop" product reveal with trending audio
2. Design process time-lapse (screen recording sped up)
3. Customer unboxing repost (UGC gold)
4. "Which design should I make next?" polls and engagement bait
5. Behind-the-scenes of your order being packed and shipped

**CALL TO ACTION**
Create content, let Completeful handle fulfillment, you focus on growing your brand

**60-SECOND REEL SCRIPT**
(0:00–0:03) HOOK: Instagram is NOT dead for POD, you're just posting the wrong stuff. These are the 7 Reels that actually sell.
(0:03–0:15) Most sellers post a flat product photo with no caption and wonder why nobody buys. Reels reward movement, story, and trending sound, not stock images.
(0:15–0:45) So film these instead: a "new in shop" reveal over a trending audio. A sped-up screen recording of your design process, people love watching it come together. Repost every customer unboxing you get; that UGC is gold and builds trust instantly. Run a "which design should I make next?" poll to farm engagement and ideas at the same time. And show behind-the-scenes of an order getting packed and shipped, proof you're a real brand.
(0:45–0:55) Pick two of these, post consistently for two weeks, and watch your saves and DMs turn into sales.
(0:55–0:60) CTA: You create the content, let Completeful handle the printing and fulfillment so you can focus on growing the brand.
