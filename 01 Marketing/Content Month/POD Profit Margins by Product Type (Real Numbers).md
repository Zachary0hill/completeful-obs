**HOOK**
*Not all POD products are created equal. Here are the real profit margins by product type, and which ones are quietly making sellers the most money.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Posters and wall art: highest margin in POD, $3, 7 production, $20, 35 retail, often 60, 70% margin with flat, cheap shipping
2. Mugs: low cost ($5, 8) but heavy shipping eats profit, price at $18+ to clear a healthy margin, great as a starter and bundle add-on
3. T-shirts: the volume play, not the margin play, ~$8, 12 cost, $24, 28 retail, roughly 40, 55% before ads, so they win on quantity
4. Hoodies and sweatshirts: highest dollar profit per sale, $20, 28 cost, $45, 60 retail, $20, 35 profit each even at a lower percentage
5. Personalized products (name mugs, pet portraits, monograms): the margin multiplier, a small customization fee lets you charge a premium buyers happily pay
