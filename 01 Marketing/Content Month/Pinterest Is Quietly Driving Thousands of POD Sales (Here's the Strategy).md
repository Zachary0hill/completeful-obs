**HOOK**
*Pinterest is quietly sending thousands of buyers to POD stores while everyone fights over TikTok. Here's the strategy top sellers won't talk about.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Pinterest is a visual search engine, not social media, pins keep driving traffic for months, unlike a post that dies in a day
2. Structure boards around buyer intent ("gifts for dog moms," "teacher classroom decor"), not vague themes
3. Design tall pins (2:3 ratio, 1000x1500) with a clear product shot plus bold readable text overlay
4. Do keyword research right in the Pinterest search bar and use those terms in pin titles, descriptions, and board names
5. Repurpose existing product photos into Idea Pins and fresh-pin every listing weekly to keep the algorithm feeding you
