**HOOK**
*Returns kill margins. But no return policy kills trust. Here's the sweet spot that protects both you and your customers.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Personalized and custom products: clearly state "no returns on custom items" (industry standard)
2. Non-personalized items: offer exchange or store credit (not full refund)
3. Quality defects: always replace or refund (your fulfillment partner should cover this)
4. Prevention: accurate size charts, detailed photos, and honest descriptions reduce returns 60%
5. State your policy clearly on every listing, hidden policies create angry customers

**CALL TO ACTION**
