**HOOK**
*Etsy Ads can be profitable, but 80% of sellers set them up wrong. Here's the fix that actually works.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Never run ads on listings that don't convert organically first
2. Start with a $1-3/day budget per listing and scale winners only
3. Turn off ads on low-margin products (mugs under $18 rarely profit from ads)
4. Check your ROAS weekly, anything under 3x should be paused
5. Ads amplify good listings; they can't fix bad ones

**CALL TO ACTION**
Focus on products with strong margins, Completeful's pricing gives you room for ads
