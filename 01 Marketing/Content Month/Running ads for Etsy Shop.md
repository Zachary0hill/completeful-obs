**HOOK**
*Most sellers turn on Etsy Ads, lose money, and quit. The ones who profit follow a framework, here it is.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Don't run ads until you have at least 5 sales and a 4.8+ star rating, Etsy's algorithm rewards proven listings with cheaper clicks
2. Start at $1, 3/day, never your whole budget, let it run 2 weeks before judging anything
3. Only advertise your 3, 5 best converting listings, not your whole shop, pushing a weak listing just burns clicks
4. Read the right metric: target a 3x+ ROAS (return on ad spend); if you spend $1 to make $3+, scale it
5. Kill any listing with lots of clicks and zero sales after 100 clicks, the problem is the listing, not the budget
