**HOOK**
*More products = more sales, but only if you don't burn out at product #12. Here's the scaling system that works.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Design in batches: create 5-10 designs in one sitting, not one at a time
2. Use templates: create one base design and swap text/colors for variations
3. Expand by product, not just design (put 1 design on 5 product types)
4. Automate listings: use copy-paste templates for descriptions and tags
5. Schedule listing days: batch upload on one day per week

**CALL TO ACTION**
Completeful's multi-product catalog makes scaling easy, one design file, dozens of products
