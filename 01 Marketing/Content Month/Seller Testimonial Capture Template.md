## Permission and Consent Note

Before recording, confirm the seller agrees to be filmed and to have their words and store used in Completeful marketing. Capture consent verbally on camera at the start and in writing afterward. Do not publish a seller's real name, store name, or any revenue figure without their written permission. If a number comes up, mark it as a [verified figure] and confirm the exact value and the seller's approval before it appears in any post. Avoid income projections or claims the seller cannot back up.

## Pre-Interview Checklist

- [ ] Seller has agreed to be recorded and understands where the content may appear.
- [ ] Written consent or release form sent and signed (see reminder below).
- [ ] Seller knows which details are optional, including real name, store name, and figures.
- [ ] Quiet space, decent lighting, and a clean background confirmed.
- [ ] Test recording checked for audio and framing before the real take.

## Questions to Ask on Camera

Ask these in order. Let the seller talk. Aim for short, specific answers.

1. Before state: What was your business like before you started using print-on-demand? What was frustrating or holding you back?
2. Why they switched: What made you decide to change how you produce or fulfill your products? What were you hoping to fix?
3. The moment it clicked: Was there a specific moment when you realized this was working? What happened?
4. The result: What is different now? Describe the change in your own words. If a number comes up, confirm it as a [verified figure] for later approval.
5. Advice for a beginner: If you were talking to someone on day one, what would you tell them?

Optional follow-ups: How does fulfillment fit into your week now? What surprised you about the product quality? What would you say to someone still on the fence?

## B-Roll Shot List to Request

- [ ] Talking-head seller answering each question, framed at chest height.
- [ ] Screen recording of the seller's store or product listings.
- [ ] Product in hand, close-up of print detail and feel.
- [ ] Seller working at their desk or workspace.
- [ ] A sample order being unboxed, if available.
- [ ] Any packaging or shipping moment the seller is comfortable showing.
- [ ] Optional facility B-roll to pair in the edit, sourced from Completeful's library.

## Release Form Reminder

Do not publish any clip until a signed release form is on file. The release should cover the seller's name and likeness, their store name, any quotes, and any figures shared. Confirm the seller has approved the final cut before it goes live. When in doubt, anonymize the story and frame it as one seller or a representative example rather than risk publishing an unverified or unapproved detail.
