**HOOK**
*That "free shipping" you're offering might be quietly eating your entire profit margin. Here's how shipping zones really work.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Shipping is priced by zones, the farther a package travels from the print facility, the more it costs you, so a flat rate that works coast-to-coast can lose money on the long hauls
2. "Free shipping" is never free, you're either baking it into the product price or absorbing it; bake it in, because buyers convert better when the cart says $0 shipping
3. Etsy's algorithm boosts listings with free shipping over $35, so price your products to clear that threshold and win both the buyer and the search rank
4. Lightweight products (stickers, posters, tees) cost almost the same to ship everywhere, start there if you want predictable shipping math
5. Use a fulfillment partner with multiple facilities so orders print closer to the buyer, shrinking the zone distance and your shipping cost on every order
