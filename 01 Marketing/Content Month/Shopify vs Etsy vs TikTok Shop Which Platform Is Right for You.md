**HOOK**
*Etsy, Shopify, or TikTok Shop? Pick the wrong one and you'll spend months grinding for sales that were never coming. Here's how to choose.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Etsy = built-in buyer traffic; people are already there searching to buy, so it's the fastest path to your first sale, but you pay fees and compete on a crowded shelf
2. TikTok Shop = video-driven discovery; if you can make short videos, the algorithm puts your product in front of millions for free, but no video means no sales
3. Shopify = total control and zero marketplace fees, but you bring ALL your own traffic, only worth it once you have an audience or ad budget
4. Match the platform to your strength: good at SEO and listings → Etsy; comfortable on camera → TikTok Shop; already have a following or email list → Shopify
5. The smart sequence: start on Etsy for proof of concept, add TikTok Shop for discovery volume, then launch Shopify to own the customer relationship
