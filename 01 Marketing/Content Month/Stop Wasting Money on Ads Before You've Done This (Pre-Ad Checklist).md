**HOOK**
*Running ads on a broken store is like pouring water into a bucket with holes. Fix these 5 things first.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Your listing photos must be professional (lifestyle mockups, not flat images)
2. Your pricing must have room for ad costs (minimum 40% margin after COGS)
3. Your product descriptions need to be complete and keyword-rich
4. You need at least 5-10 reviews before ads become profitable
5. Your shipping time must be competitive (slow shipping kills repeat purchases)

**CALL TO ACTION**
Fix the foundation first, then scale, Completeful gives you the shipping speed ads require

**60-SECOND REEL SCRIPT**
(0:00–0:03) HOOK: Running ads on a broken store is like pouring water into a bucket full of holes. Plug these five first.
(0:03–0:15) Everybody wants to flip on ads and scale. But ads don't fix a weak store, they just spotlight it and charge you for the privilege. Patch the leaks first.
(0:15–0:45) Five checks before you spend a dollar. One: your photos have to look professional, lifestyle mockups, not flat images. Two: your price needs room for ad costs, so at least a forty percent margin after what the product costs you. Three: your descriptions must be complete and keyword-rich so paid clicks actually convert. Four: get at least five to ten reviews first, ads to a review-less listing just burn cash. Five: your shipping has to be fast, because slow delivery kills the repeat buyers that make ads worth it.
(0:45–0:55) Fix those five and every ad dollar starts compounding instead of leaking out the bottom.
(0:55–0:60) CTA: Fix the foundation first, then scale, Completeful gives you the shipping speed ads require.
