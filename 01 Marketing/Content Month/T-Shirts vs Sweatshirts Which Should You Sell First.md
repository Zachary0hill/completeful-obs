**HOOK**
*Every new POD seller asks this. The answer depends on your niche, your audience, and what season you're launching in.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. T-shirts: lower price point, impulse buy, year-round demand
2. Sweatshirts: higher AOV ($35, 45), seasonal Q4 spike, premium feel
3. T-shirts have more competition but higher volume
4. Sweatshirts have better margins per unit
5. The move: launch tees first for volume, add sweatshirts for Q4 profit
