**HOOK**
*We raised prices $5 across the board and sales went UP. Here's the counterintuitive psychology behind it.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Low prices signal low quality to buyers (especially in the gift market)
2. A $5 increase on a $22 tee adds $5 pure profit with no additional cost
3. Higher prices attract better customers who leave better reviews
4. Premium pricing gives you room for promotions (10% off $28 feels better than $23)
5. Test it: raise prices on 5 listings for 2 weeks and compare conversion rates

**CALL TO ACTION**
Price for value, not volume, Completeful's low costs give you margin to play with
