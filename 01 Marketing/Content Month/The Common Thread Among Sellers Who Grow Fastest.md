**HOOK**

**KEY POINTS**
1. They commit to one niche before they expand.
2. They test new products with real orders instead of opinions.
3. They treat consistent quality as the engine of repeat sales.
4. They keep fulfillment off their plate so they can focus on selling.
5. Completeful handles production so sellers spend their time on growth.
