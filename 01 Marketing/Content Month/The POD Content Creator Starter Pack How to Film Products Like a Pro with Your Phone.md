**HOOK**
*You don't need a studio. You need a phone, natural light, and these 5 simple setups.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Flat lay setup: clean background, product centered, overhead shot
2. Lifestyle shot: wear it or use it in a real environment
3. Unboxing video: open the package on camera with good lighting
4. Design process timelapse: screen record your Canva workflow at 4x speed
5. Before/after: show the blank product vs the finished printed version
