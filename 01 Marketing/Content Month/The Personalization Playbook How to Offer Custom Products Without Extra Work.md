**HOOK**
*Personalized products convert 2.5x higher. Most sellers think it's complicated. It's actually dead simple.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Create one design template with a text placeholder in Canva/Photoshop
2. Add "personalization" as a listing option on Etsy (free, built-in feature)
3. When an order comes in, swap the placeholder text (takes 30 seconds)
4. Upload the custom file to Completeful and the order ships automatically
5. Charge $3-8 extra for personalization, buyers happily pay for custom

**CALL TO ACTION**
Add personalization to every listing, Completeful prints custom orders seamlessly
