**HOOK**
*Stop buying inventory before you know what sells. One seller did, and never went back.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. The seller used to buy stock up front and guess what would sell.
2. Unsold boxes tied up cash and space.
3. Switching to print-on-demand meant products were made only after a sale.
4. They could test new designs without financial risk.
5. Completeful produced and shipped each order, so no garage full of stock.
