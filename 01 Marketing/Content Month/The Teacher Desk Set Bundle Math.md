**HOOK**
*Three products at 16.50 total base. One gift set. Watch what happens to the margin.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Leather Journal 6.00, Redwood Pen/Case 6.50 to 8.00, Acrylic Bookmark 4.00
2. Combined base lands around 16.50 to 18.00 engraved
3. A bundle is not compared to anything. A single journal is compared to everything
4. Same name engraved across all three reads as intentional, not padded
5. Bundles raise average order value without raising acquisition cost
