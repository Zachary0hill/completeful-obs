**HOOK**
*You don't need to draw. The best-selling POD designs are just words, and they're the easiest thing you'll ever make.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Text-only designs sell because buyers shop for a message, not artwork, the words do the converting
2. One bold free font (think Anton, Bebas Neue, or a clean script) is enough for a professional-looking design
3. Niche the phrase, not the art: "Plant Mom," "Trauma Nurse," "Girl Dad", specific beats pretty every time
4. Black text on white, white on black, high contrast prints cleanly on every product and color
5. One phrase becomes ten products in minutes: tee, hoodie, mug, tote, sticker, all from the same file
