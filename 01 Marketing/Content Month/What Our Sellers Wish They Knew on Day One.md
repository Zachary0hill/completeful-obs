**HOOK**
*Five things our sellers wish they knew on day one.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Start with a niche, not a giant catalog.
2. Order a sample before you list, so you trust your own product.
3. You do not need inventory or minimums to begin.
4. Consistent quality earns repeat buyers more than flashy launches.
5. Fast domestic fulfillment keeps customers calm and reviews positive.
