**HOOK**
*Myth: overseas is always cheaper. This Etsy seller found the real trade-off.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. The seller assumed overseas fulfillment was the only affordable option.
2. Long shipping times led to anxious buyers and cancelled orders.
3. Switching to US production meant fast domestic fulfillment for their customers.
4. In-house manufacturing in Lafayette, Louisiana gave them consistent quality.
5. Completeful supported the move without forcing minimums.
