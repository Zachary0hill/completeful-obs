*Customers judge your store in 3 seconds. If it looks unprofessional, they bounce. Here are the fastest fixes.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Swap flat white-background mockups for lifestyle mockups (free on Placeit)
2. Write product descriptions that are at least 150 words with bullet points
3. Use consistent branding: same fonts, colors, and photo style across all listings
4. Add a professional banner/logo (Canva template takes 10 minutes)
5. Fill out your About page and shipping policies completely
