**HOOK**
*If your product description is one sentence, you're leaving money on the table. Here's what converts.*

**KEY POINTS**
1. Lead with the emotional benefit, not the product specs
2. Include sizing information and care instructions (reduces returns)
3. Use bullet points for scanability (buyers don't read paragraphs)
4. Add gift-related keywords ("perfect gift for," "great for birthdays")
5. End with a trust signal (shipping speed, quality guarantee, return policy)

**CALL TO ACTION**
Great descriptions + great products = sales, list with Completeful and we handle the rest

(0:03–0:15) Buyers can't touch your product, so the words have to do the selling. Most sellers waste them listing specs nobody cares about.
(0:15–0:45) So flip it. Lead with the emotional benefit, how it makes them feel or who they're gifting it to, not the fabric weight. Then add sizing and care instructions, because clarity up front quietly slashes your returns. Break it into bullet points; nobody reads a wall of text. Sprinkle in gift keywords like "perfect gift for" and "great for birthdays" so you show up in those searches.
