## What This Is

Fifteen back-to-school content ideas, reel-first, generated against the live Completeful Product Catalog (123 distinct products, pulled 2026-06-26). Each idea names the exact product it anchors to, with base price and personalization method, so the script can carry real numbers instead of vague claims.

Eleven ideas are anchored to products the catalog actually carries. Four are marked **ASPIRATIONAL** and reference products Completeful does not currently stock. Read the two warnings below before publishing anything from this batch.

---

## Warning 1: The Aspirational Four

Ideas 12 through 15 anchor to t-shirts, tote bags, pencil pouches, and crew sweatshirts. **The live catalog does not carry any of these.** Apparel is two products: Baseball Hat ($7.00, embroidery) and Small Beanies ($3.50 to $4.00, embroidery). There are no tees, no sweatshirts, no totes, no pouches.

These were included at your direction. They are tagged `ASPIRATIONAL` in each note's frontmatter and carry a `blocked_reason` field. They are usable as:
- Demand signal for a catalog expansion conversation
- Content that ships only if and when apparel expands
- Internal reference

They are not usable as seller-facing content today. A seller who follows idea 14 will try to list a product Completeful cannot print.

## Warning 3: The 2026 Timing Reality

Today is July 17, 2026. The existing notes tell sellers to "list by June for Etsy indexing." That window has closed for this season. Any idea from this batch shipping in the next three weeks should be framed for late listers, not early ones. That reframe is the actual competitive angle: domestic fulfillment from Lafayette is what lets a late seller still deliver inside the two-week August rush. Ideas 7 and 10 lean on this directly.

## Notes on Product Data

- **Lunchbox ($0.00 to $15.00)** was excluded. The catalog flags draft pricing on this SKU. It is the single most obvious back-to-school product in the catalog and it is not content-ready. Worth fixing before August.
- **Circle Pet Tag** appears in idea 11 as a backpack zipper charm. That is off-label use of a pet product. It works visually and the price is right, but confirm with the team before publishing.
- Apparel and Home are both flagged as thin categories in the catalog file. Back to school is an apparel-heavy season industry-wide. That mismatch is the strategic story underneath this whole batch.
