## What this file is

The canonical hook reference for Completeful content. Every Reel, carousel, Short, and post opens with a hook from one of the formulas below. The Instagram Writing Style prompt and the Content Month frontmatter field `hook_formula` both point here. Pair this with `Platform Hook Examples` for ready-to-use lines and `Positioning & Writing Styles` for angle ideas.

## Hard rules for every hook
- The full hook lands in the first 125 characters (everything before the Instagram "more" cut, and the first 1 to 3 seconds on TikTok).
- No emojis. No dashes used as punctuation. No ellipses. No ALL CAPS. Maximum one exclamation point, and prefer zero.
- One idea per hook. Create a knowledge gap or name a real pain. Do not explain yourself in the hook.
- Lead with the seller benefit or the seller's problem, not the product spec or the brand name.
- Approved facts only (no minimums, in-house US manufacturing in Lafayette Louisiana, 228,000 sq ft, 500 to 900+ catalog, the five print methods, the four integrations). No specific fulfillment day counts, no invented revenue, no named-competitor comparisons.

## The four core formulas

Use one of these as the default for short-form. The `hook_formula` field in each content file names which one is in play.

### 1. Numbered list
Promises a finite, scannable payoff. Best for tips, mistakes, and feature roundups.
- Template: "[Number] [things] that [benefit or consequence]"
- Examples: "7 Etsy listing mistakes that quietly kill your sales." / "5 products in our catalog sellers keep sleeping on."

### 2. Stop doing X
Pattern-interrupts a common habit, then promises the better way. Best for tips and corrections.
- Template: "Stop [common habit]. Do this instead."
- Examples: "Stop starting your design from a blank canvas." / "Stop filming products on a white sheet."

### 3. Myth vs fact
Names a belief the viewer holds, then breaks it. Best for awareness and education.
- Template: "Most sellers think [belief]. Here is what actually [happens]."
- Examples: "Most sellers think the design sells the product. The listing image is what buyers judge first." / "Custom products do not have to ship in weeks."

### 4. Measurable transformation
Promises a concrete before-to-after with a number or timeframe. Best for tutorials and outcomes.
- Template: "How to [outcome] in [time or step count]"
- Examples: "How to turn one design into 10 listings in 30 minutes." / "How to go from blank catalog to a live product in one sitting."

## Extended formula bank

Pull from these when the four core formulas feel repetitive. Keep them inside the hard rules above.

5. How to: "How to [achieve outcome] without [common pain]."
6. This or that: "Hoodies or crewnecks. Which actually sells for POD."
7. The secret to: "The quiet skill behind every listing that looks premium."
8. Why X is not working: "Why your listings are not getting found, and the fix."
9. The new way: "There is a faster way to build a product line. Here it is."
10. Proven lesson: "The pricing rule that raised this seller's margin."
11. If you are struggling with X: "If your store looks amateur, fix these five things first."
12. The bold claim: "The reason your store is not growing has nothing to do with your designs."
13. The process reveal: "Here is what happens from order placed to box shipped from Lafayette."
14. The seller callout: "If you sell on TikTok Shop and still fulfill orders yourself, watch this."
15. The outcome first: "Faster shipping, better reviews, no inventory. Here is how."
16. The question that reveals a problem: "When did you last test a product without worrying about unsold stock."

## Platform behavior cheatsheet
- TikTok: spoken or on-screen in the first 1 to 3 seconds. Bold claim, process reveal, myth bust. Maximum two lines of on-screen text.
- Instagram: earns the tap or swipe. Empathy open, aspiration, catalog tease, mini case study. Full hook inside 125 characters.
- X: one sharp quotable line, under 100 characters. Provocation and brevity.
- LinkedIn: one to two lines of business insight inside the 210-character truncation window. Industry observation, counterintuitive data point.
- YouTube: answer "why keep watching" in the first 5 to 15 seconds. Stakes statement, process tease, transformation promise.

See `Platform Hook Examples` for copy-ready lines per platform.

## How to use in a content file
1. Pick the angle from `Positioning & Writing Styles` or the idea backlog.
2. Choose a formula above and set `hook_formula` in the file frontmatter.
3. Draft the hook, then check it against the hard rules (125 characters, no dashes, one idea, benefit-led).
4. Pull a matching platform line from `Platform Hook Examples` if useful.
