## What This Prompt Does

This prompt places a Completeful product into a realistic lifestyle scene using Nano Banana Pro. Fill in the variables in brackets, paste the full prompt into the Nano Banana Pro generation interface, and iterate on the output until the scene matches the intended visual direction.

This template is designed for general lifestyle scenes. For seasonal variations (holiday, summer, back-to-school) or flat-lay compositions, copy this template and adapt the scene description block.

---

## How to Fill in the Variables

| Variable | What to Enter | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `[PRODUCT_TYPE]` | The specific product from the Completeful catalog | white ceramic mug, unisex crew neck sweatshirt, tote bag |
| `[DESIGN_DESCRIPTION]` | Brief description of the print or design on the product | minimalist mountain line art in navy, floral wreath with script text |
| `[SCENE_SETTING]` | Where the product is placed | cozy home kitchen countertop, outdoor wooden picnic table, modern apartment desk |
| `[LIGHTING]` | Type and direction of light | warm morning window light from the left, overcast soft natural light, golden hour backlighting |
| `[MOOD]` | One or two adjectives describing the overall feeling | warm and lived-in, clean and minimal, adventurous and outdoorsy |
| `[PERSON_PRESENCE]` | Whether a person is in the scene | no person, hands only (holding or using the product), person in background out of focus |
| `[PLATFORM_RATIO]` | Target output ratio for the image | 1:1 for Instagram feed, 9:16 for Reels/TikTok, 16:9 for YouTube thumbnail |
| `[EXCLUSIONS]` | Anything that must not appear in the scene | no logos other than on the product, no competing branded items, no text overlays in the scene itself |

---

## The Prompt

```
Generate a high-quality lifestyle product photography scene for a [PRODUCT_TYPE] with [DESIGN_DESCRIPTION] printed on it.

SCENE:
Place the product in a [SCENE_SETTING]. The scene should feel authentic and lived-in, not staged. Include natural background elements that complement the setting (surfaces, props, ambient objects) but keep the product as the clear visual focal point.

LIGHTING:
Use [LIGHTING]. The lighting should be consistent across the product and the background, with soft natural shadows beneath or beside the product to ground it in the scene. Avoid harsh flash lighting or overly processed HDR effects.

MOOD AND AESTHETIC:
The overall image should feel [MOOD]. The color palette of the scene should complement the product design without overwhelming it. Aim for the visual quality of a professional lifestyle photo from a mid-to-premium direct-to-consumer brand.

PERSON PRESENCE:
[PERSON_PRESENCE]. If hands are included, they should appear relaxed and natural, consistent with how a real person would interact with this product.

TECHNICAL:
Output ratio: [PLATFORM_RATIO]
Product prominence: Product should occupy 30 to 50 percent of the frame and be in sharp focus. Background elements may be slightly soft-focus to create depth.
Color accuracy: Preserve the exact colors and design of the printed product. Do not alter or reinterpret the design.
Exclusions: [EXCLUSIONS]

Do not add any text, watermarks, or overlays to the image.
```

## Example Filled Prompt

```
Generate a high-quality lifestyle product photography scene for a white ceramic mug with a minimalist mountain line art design in navy printed on it.

SCENE:
Place the product on a cozy home kitchen countertop next to a small potted succulent and a folded linen napkin. The scene should feel authentic and lived-in, not staged. Include natural background elements that complement the setting (a blurred kitchen background with soft cabinetry) but keep the product as the clear visual focal point.

LIGHTING:
Use warm morning window light from the left. The lighting should be consistent across the product and the background, with soft natural shadows beneath or beside the product to ground it in the scene. Avoid harsh flash lighting or overly processed HDR effects.

MOOD AND AESTHETIC:
The overall image should feel warm and lived-in. The color palette of the scene should complement the product design without overwhelming it. Aim for the visual quality of a professional lifestyle photo from a mid-to-premium direct-to-consumer brand.

PERSON PRESENCE:
Hands only, wrapped around the mug as if warming their hands on a cool morning, consistent with how a real person would interact with this product.

TECHNICAL:
Output ratio: 1:1
Product prominence: Product should occupy 30 to 50 percent of the frame and be in sharp focus. Background elements may be slightly soft-focus to create depth.
Color accuracy: Preserve the exact navy color and line-art design of the printed product. Do not alter or reinterpret the design.
Exclusions: No logos other than on the product, no competing branded items, no text overlays in the scene itself.

Do not add any text, watermarks, or overlays to the image.
```

## Post-Generation Checklist

Before approving any mockup output, confirm:

- [ ] Product design is accurate and not distorted or reinterpreted by the AI
- [ ] Lighting is consistent between product and background
- [ ] No unintended text, logos, or branding in the scene
- [ ] Correct aspect ratio for the target platform
- [ ] Product is the clear focal point (not buried in the scene)
- [ ] Mood and color palette align with the content brief
- [ ] Ready for Photoroom touch-up if shadows or edges need refinement
