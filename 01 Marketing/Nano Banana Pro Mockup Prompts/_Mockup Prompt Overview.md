## What This Folder Contains

This folder holds the prompt templates used to generate product mockup visuals for Completeful content. Mockups are produced using a two-tool workflow: Photoroom for background removal and basic compositing, and Nano Banana Pro for AI-generated lifestyle scene placement.

These prompts are designed to be copy-pasted into Nano Banana Pro's generation interface with variables filled in. Each prompt is structured to produce output that is on-brand, conversion-optimized, and suitable for use across Instagram, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, and the Completeful website.

---

## Why Mockup Quality Matters

Completeful's customers are POD sellers. Their buyers cannot touch or try the product before purchasing. The mockup is the product experience. A lifestyle scene that places the item in a real-world context outperforms a plain white-background render on every major platform, particularly on Etsy, TikTok Shop, and Instagram.

Completeful's own content uses high-quality mockups both to showcase catalog products and to demonstrate to sellers what their listings could look like when they use Completeful for fulfillment.

## The Two-Tool Workflow

### Step 1: Photoroom
- Upload the flat product image (print file placed on product template)
- Remove the background using Photoroom's AI removal tool
- Adjust lighting, shadows, and edges for a clean cutout
- Export as PNG with transparency

### Step 2: Nano Banana Pro
- Import the Photoroom cutout as the product layer
- Write a scene prompt using the templates in this folder
- Adjust scale, angle, and placement within the generated scene
- Select the output that best matches the intended visual direction
- Export in the required resolution and aspect ratio for the target platform

---

## What Makes a Good Mockup Prompt

A well-structured Nano Banana Pro prompt has four components:

1. **Scene description:** Where is the product placed? What is the environment? What time of day or lighting condition?
2. **Product context:** How is the product being used or displayed? Is it worn, held, placed on a surface, or visible in the background?
3. **Mood and aesthetic:** What is the overall feeling of the image? Warm and cozy, clean and minimal, outdoor and active, festive?
4. **Technical parameters:** Aspect ratio, resolution, product prominence (foreground vs. background), and any exclusions (no text in the scene, no competing branded products visible).

## Platform Aspect Ratios

| Platform | Format | Ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram Feed | Square | 1:1 |
| Instagram Reel / TikTok | Vertical | 9:16 |
| Instagram Carousel | Landscape or Square | 4:5 or 1:1 |
| X / Twitter | Landscape | 16:9 |
| LinkedIn | Landscape or Square | 1.91:1 or 1:1 |
| YouTube Thumbnail | Landscape | 16:9 |
| Blog Header | Wide landscape | 16:9 or 2:1 |
