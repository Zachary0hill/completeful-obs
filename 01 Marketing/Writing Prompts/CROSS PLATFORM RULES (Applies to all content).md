These rules override any platform-specific guidance when there is a conflict.

### Universal Hard Rules

1. **No emojis.** Not in copy, not in graphics, not in hashtags. Ever.
2. **No engagement bait.** "Like if you agree," "Tag a friend," "Comment YES" -- never.
3. **No external links in main post body** on any feed-based platform (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, TikTok). Deliver standalone value natively. Place links in comments/replies or edit them in after engagement builds.
4. **3-5 hashtags maximum** on every platform. Facebook: 1-3. Quality and niche relevance over volume.
5. **Hook in the first 125 characters.** Every platform truncates at roughly this point on mobile. If the hook is not visible before "...more" / "See more" / "Show more," the post fails.

### Universal Content Principles

- **Write for saves and shares, not likes.** Every platform now weights saves/shares/DMs 3-20x higher than likes. Ask yourself: "Would a POD seller bookmark this to reference next week?"
- **Maintain strict niche consistency.** Instagram, LinkedIn, and TikTok all algorithmically reward accounts that stay in one lane. Never post about unrelated topics. Stay in POD / fulfillment / e-commerce / seller education.
- **Dwell time is a primary signal everywhere.** Longer, well-formatted content that rewards reading outperforms shallow short content. Educational B2B content has a structural advantage here.
- **Sound human.** LinkedIn penalizes AI content. Google's raters flag it. TikTok's audience rejects it. Every post should sound like it was written by a fulfillment expert sharing what they know -- not generated by a marketing tool.
- **Target audience is always the seller, never the end consumer.** All content speaks to the person running the POD business. Route everything back to Completeful as the fulfillment backend.
