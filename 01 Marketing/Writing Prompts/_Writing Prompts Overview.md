## What This Folder Contains

This folder holds the writing prompts and copy frameworks used to generate and refine content for every Completeful channel. Each platform has its own dedicated prompt file because the voice, format, length, and audience behavior differ meaningfully between Instagram, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, and the blog.

There is also a cross-platform repurposing prompt that takes one finalized piece of copy and adapts it to all platforms in a single pass.

---

## How to Use These Prompts

1. **Choose the platform prompt** that matches where the content will first be published.
2. **Fill in the variables** indicated in each prompt (topic, hook direction, CTA, content pillar).
3. **Run the prompt** in GPT-4o (via n8n automation or directly).
4. **Review the output** against the brand rules before approving.
5. **Use the repurposing prompt** to adapt the finalized anchor piece to remaining platforms.

---

## Brand Rules These Prompts Enforce

Every prompt in this folder is built around the following non-negotiable brand standards. Any output that violates these rules must be revised before use.

### Voice
Completeful's voice is coaching, empowering, and seller-first. The brand speaks to sellers as peers who are navigating real business challenges, not as customers being sold to. Content should feel like advice from a knowledgeable partner who wants the seller to win.

### No Emojis
Finalized social copy does not include emojis. No exceptions in approved posts. Emojis are permitted in internal brainstorming notes and rough drafts only.

### No Dashes
Finalized social copy does not include em dashes (--) or en dashes (-) as punctuation. If a dash would create a pause or aside, rewrite the sentence. Use a period or restructure the thought instead.

### Transparency
Completeful leads with honest, specific information. Do not make vague superlatives. Use real numbers, real product counts, real process details.

### Seller Outcome First
Every post should be oriented toward a concrete outcome for the seller: more revenue, less risk, faster shipping, better mockups, clearer pricing. The product or service is always in service of that outcome.

## Content Pillars

All prompts operate within one of these five content pillars:

| Pillar | What It Covers |
|---|---|
| Education | How POD and 3PL work, pricing, platform tips, fulfillment mechanics |
| Product Showcase | Catalog depth, new products, design-to-product demonstrations |
| Seller Success | Case studies, testimonials, seller journeys, outcomes |
| Behind the Scenes | Manufacturing floor, production process, team and technology |
| Platform Tips | Etsy, TikTok Shop, and Shopify tactics for the Completeful audience |

## Repurposing Flow

The standard repurposing flow starts from the anchor platform (usually TikTok or Instagram, wherever the idea is strongest in short-form) and radiates outward.

```
TikTok or IG Reel (anchor)
  --> IG Carousel (expand the points)
  --> X Thread (list format, shareable)
  --> LinkedIn Post (professional reframe)
  --> Blog Section (deep-dive)
  --> YouTube Script (full-length treatment)
```

Use the Repurposing Prompt file in this folder to execute this flow efficiently in a single GPT-4o session.
