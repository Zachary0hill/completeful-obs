# Viral Reel & Shorts Script System

The single operating file for planning, hooking, scripting, and shipping short form video for Completeful. Built from 2026 platform research plus the Completeful brand system. Pair with [[Instagram Writing Style]], [[TikTok Script Prompt]], [[Hook Formulas Library]], [[Platform Hook Examples]], [[Content Pillars & Categories]], and the renamed footage library.

**Non-negotiable brand voice:** no emojis, no dashes, no hype, no ALL CAPS. First person, peer to peer. Lead with the seller benefit, not the spec. 70 percent educational, 20 percent behind the scenes, 10 percent promotional. One CTA. Max 5 hashtags. Audience: POD sellers on Etsy, TikTok Shop, and Shopify. Edge: 228,000 sq ft of in house US manufacturing, 500 plus products, no minimums, orders shipped in days not weeks.

---

## 1. The numbers that decide everything (2026)

**The decision window has collapsed.** Viewers decide to stay or scroll in roughly 0.8 to 1.5 seconds. Up to 50 percent drop in the first 3 seconds. Your job is to win three gates: the 3 second gate, the 15 second gate, and the finish.

| Metric | Instagram Reels | YouTube Shorts |
|---|---|---|
| Sweet spot length | 7 to 30 seconds | 15 to 35 seconds, algo favors 20 to 25 |
| Hard rule | a 10s reel at 80% retention beats a 60s reel at 30% | under 15s lacks hook depth, over 45s drops hard |
| Distribution cliff | over 90s sees reduced reach | over 45s sees retention drop off |
| 3 second hold target | above 60% (strong holds out reach weak ones 5 to 10x) | above 70% intro retention |
| Completion target | as high as possible, retention is the ranking input | above 60% under 30s; above 75% gets 3x push to new viewers |
| Loop trick | n/a | 20 to 25s with a seamless loop can exceed 100% retention |

**Operating principles**
- Pick the shortest length that delivers the message. Retention rate beats length every time.
- Two hooks per video: the opening hook (0 to 3s) and a secondary hook or pattern interrupt around 14 to 15s to clear the second gate.
- Captions are not optional. About 85 percent of views happen on mute. Burn in text from frame one.
- Cut every 2 to 4 seconds. Alternate wide, medium, close. Never hold one shot longer than three sentences.
- Open with the payoff, not the setup. People stop when they instantly understand what they gain.
- On screen text doubles as SEO. TikTok and Shorts read it with OCR. Put your keyword in the hook text.

## 2. The universal structure

Every short follows one spine. Use the timecodes as a checklist.

```
0.0 to 1.5s   HOOK        Payoff-first claim + matching on-screen text. The most compelling frame, no logo, no slow intro.
1.5 to 3s     REINFORCE   Restate the stakes or who this is for. Clear the 3s gate.
3 to 13s      VALUE BEATS 2 to 4 fast beats. New shot every 2 to 4s. Each beat earns the next.
~14 to 15s    RE-HOOK     A twist, the best tip, or "but here is the part nobody shows." Clears the 15s gate.
13 to 22s     PAYOFF      Deliver on the hook's promise. The reveal, the result, the answer.
last 2 to 3s  CTA         One specific action. Loop back to the first line if possible.
```

**Three interchangeable frameworks** (pick by intent):
- **Hook > Problem > Solution > CTA** — best for Education and Platform Tips.
- **Hook > Tension > Value beats > Proof > Payoff > CTA** — best for Behind the Scenes and Seller Success.
- **Result first > How > Proof > CTA** — best for Product Showcase reveals (blank to finished).

**Platform tuning**
- **Instagram Reels:** 7 to 15s is the discovery sweet spot. Hook on screen within 125 characters. Caption 150 to 300 words, hook in first 125 chars before the "more" cut. CTA for cold viewers = save and share.
- **TikTok:** 21 to 34s for educational POD content. Caption hook in first 100 chars, keyword rich. Two of three triggers every time: pattern interrupt, curiosity gap, social proof. "Follow for Part 2" drives follows.
- **YouTube Shorts:** 20 to 25s with a seamless loop. Hook in first 3s, keyword in the on screen text and the title. End on a line that flows back into the first line.

## 3. The Viral Hook Bank

A hook has one job: stop the scroll and earn the next sentence. 13 proven types, each with a Completeful template you can fill in. All dash free and emoji free. Keep spoken hooks to 8 to 15 words.

| # | Type | Why it works | Completeful template |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Curiosity Gap | opens a loop the brain cannot drop | "The reason your POD store stalled at 2K a month is not your designs." |
| 2 | Contrarian Strike | challenges accepted advice | "Stop obsessing over designs. Your fulfillment decides who reorders." |
| 3 | Identity Call | names exactly who it is for | "Etsy sellers fulfilling overseas: this is costing you five star reviews." |
| 4 | Result First | shows the outcome before the how | "This blank glass becomes a 34 dollar gift. Here is the whole process." |
| 5 | Mid Action | drops you into the moment | "We are loading 200 custom mugs right now and the press never stops." |
| 6 | Number / Stat | specificity feels credible | "500 products. Zero minimums. One partner in Louisiana." |
| 7 | Confession | raw and human, beats polish | "I lost 3,000 dollars on overseas POD before I figured this out." |
| 8 | Hard Question | makes them audit themselves | "When did you last check what your supplier is doing to your delivery times?" |
| 9 | Problem Call Out | names a pain they have not articulated | "Your best design just got a one star review for shipping. Not the print." |
| 10 | Process Reveal | "how it is made" curiosity | "Here is what happens from order placed to box shipped, all in one building." |
| 11 | Myth Bust | corrects a false belief | "Custom products take two weeks to ship. Not when they are made in house." |
| 12 | Before and After | transformation in one line | "Before US fulfillment: delays and refunds. After: orders out in 48 hours." |
| 13 | Social Proof | borrowed credibility | "We fulfill for sellers on Etsy, TikTok Shop, and Shopify. The fast growers share one habit." |

**Hook rules for 2026**
- Specific curiosity wins, generic curiosity reads as spam. "stalled at exactly 200 views" beats "grow your account."
- Lead with raw authenticity, real numbers, and confession openings over corporate intros.
- The on screen hook text must match the spoken hook word for word.
- If the hook does not name a benefit, a number, or a person, rewrite it.

## 6. Swipe Bank: pro brand and influencer formats

Proven, repeatable formats used by top creators and brands. Each maps to a pillar and to footage we already have. Rotate through these so the feed never feels samey.

1. **Blank to Finished (the reveal).** Start on the finished product, hard cut to the raw blank, show the make, land back on the finished hero. Pillar: Product Showcase. Footage: any engraving or print reveal (whiskey glasses, metal wallet, mugs).
2. **How It Is Made in 30s.** Fast montage of one product's full production with a calm expert voiceover. Pillar: BTS. Footage: UV printing, sublimation, embroidery, metal fabrication.
3. **Oddly Satisfying Loop.** One mesmerizing process clip, 20 to 25s, seamless loop, minimal text, trending ambient audio. Pillar: BTS. Footage: embroidery stitch macro, laser sparks, rotary engraving.
4. **3 Things / Listicle.** "3 products you did not know we make in house." Numbered on screen, one beat each. Pillar: Product or Education. Footage: product montage.
5. **X vs Y.** "Overseas POD vs made in the USA," split screen or before and after. Pillar: Education or BTS. Footage: shipping speed, facility wide shots.
6. **POV.** "POV: your customer opens the box you did not have to pack." Pillar: Seller Success. Footage: shipping, packing, unboxing.
7. **Myth Bust.** State the myth on screen, cut to proof. "Custom takes weeks. Watch." Pillar: Education. Footage: same day production line.
8. **Day in the Facility.** Time stamped beats through the building. Pillar: BTS. Footage: facility tour, receiving machines, drone aerials.
9. **Founder Talking Head + B-roll.** Josh or Zach delivers the hook to camera, B-roll covers the value beats. Pillar: any. Footage: needs a founder set (see gaps).
10. **Green Screen / React.** React to an Etsy or TikTok Shop screenshot, stat, or comment with a POD angle. Pillar: Platform Tips. Footage: screen recording + talking head.
11. **Number Drop.** Open on a single big stat held on screen ("500 products, 0 minimums"), then justify it. Pillar: Education. Footage: catalog montage.
12. **Client / Seller Spotlight.** Real partner visits or testimonial, framed around their journey with Completeful as the supporting actor. Pillar: Seller Success. Footage: the facility client tour set.
13. **Tutorial in 30s.** One concrete how to with on screen steps. Pillar: Education or Platform Tips. Footage: app screen recording.
14. **Trend Hijack.** Map a trending audio or format onto a POD insight, only when the angle is authentic. Pillar: any. Footage: flexible.

**Format selection rule:** match the format to the pillar and to the strongest footage you have for that week. When in doubt for Completeful, default to How It Is Made or Blank to Finished. They are the formats no overseas competitor can copy.

## 7. Quality bar before you post
- Could the hook stand alone as a thumbnail and still earn a tap?
- Is the payoff actually delivered, or did you only tease it?
- Is there a save or comment trigger, not a generic "like and follow"?
- Did you stay in the POD, fulfillment, and ecommerce lane? The last 9 to 12 posts define how the algorithm files the account.
- Zero emojis, zero dashes, under 6 hashtags?
