# App Overview and Capabilities

The Completeful app is the primary interface sellers use to manage their entire print-on-demand business through Completeful. It connects your product designs, your sales channels, and the Completeful production facility in Lafayette, LA into a single workflow — from designing a product to fulfilling a customer order.

## What the App Does

The app covers every stage of the seller journey:

- **Design and create products** — Build product designs using the built-in editor, apply them to items from the 500-900+ product catalog, and save them to your My Products library
- **Connect sales channels** — Link Shopify, Etsy, TikTok Shop, or WooCommerce stores so orders route automatically to Completeful for production and fulfillment
- **Publish listings** — Create and push product listings with titles, descriptions, pricing, variants, and images directly to Etsy and Shopify from within the app
- **Track orders and earnings** — Monitor active orders through production stages and review earnings including Etsy ledger sync that shows revenue and production costs side by side
- **Manage finances** — Add payment methods, fund your wallet, and review production cost charges per order

## Core App Features

### Design Editor
The design editor provides a canvas-based interface for building product artwork. Sellers can upload images, add text, apply custom fonts, and position elements precisely. Designs are saved to the account and linked to specific products in My Products.

### Design Templates
Pre-built design templates organized by product type and occasion give sellers a fast starting point. Templates are fully editable within the design editor.

### Custom Fonts
Sellers can upload their own font files to use in the design editor and infographic editor, enabling brand-consistent typography across all products.

### Infographic Editor
A specialized editor for creating listing images — multi-panel graphics that display product features, sizing, personalization instructions, or care details overlaid on product photography. Output is sized for Etsy and Shopify listing requirements.

### Product Catalog
A browsable and filterable view of the full Completeful catalog, organized by category and production method. Filters include price range, personalization type, and production method. Products can be added to My Products directly from the catalog.

### My Products
The personal product library where sellers manage every item they have configured for sale. Each entry shows the linked design, base cost, retail price, and listing status across connected channels.

### Connect Store
OAuth-based integration flow for linking Shopify, Etsy, TikTok Shop, and WooCommerce accounts. Once connected, orders from those platforms route automatically to Completeful.

### Listings (Etsy and Shopify)
In-app listing creation for Etsy and Shopify. Sellers can write titles, descriptions, tags, set pricing and variants, and upload images, then publish directly from the app without opening the sales channel dashboard.

### Earnings Page
A financial summary view that syncs with the seller's Etsy ledger to show sales revenue, production costs, and net earnings in one place.

### Wallet and Payment Methods
Sellers pre-fund a wallet or attach a payment method that Completeful charges as orders are produced. The wallet panel shows current balance, recent charges, and payout history.

### Sample Orders
A dedicated flow for ordering a sample unit of any product at the standard production cost. Sample orders ship directly to the seller for quality review before listing.

## Access

The app is available at completeful.com and via the Completeful mobile app. For login issues or account access, contact support@completeful.com.
