# Feature Roadmap

This page tracks the status of app features across three states: shipped and available to sellers today, actively in development, and planned for future builds. Use it to answer seller questions about upcoming capabilities and to align internal team work against current priorities.

## Shipped Features (Live)

The following features are available to all sellers in the current version of the Completeful app:

### Design and Product Creation
- Design editor with canvas-based layout, text, and image upload
- Design templates organized by product type and occasion
- Custom font upload and management
- Infographic editor for listing images
- Product catalog browse with 500-900+ items
- Catalog filtering by category, production method, and price range
- My Products library with design-to-product linking

### Channel Integration and Listings
- Shopify store connection via OAuth
- Etsy store connection via OAuth
- TikTok Shop integration
- WooCommerce integration
- In-app listing creation for Shopify
- In-app listing creation for Etsy

### Orders and Finances
- Order tracking through production stages
- Earnings page with Etsy ledger sync
- Wallet with balance display and charge history
- Payment method management
- Sample order flow (no order minimums on any product)

## In Development

These features are actively being built by the Completeful product and engineering teams.

### App Tours and AI Support
Guided in-app tours that walk first-time users through core workflows, paired with an AI-powered support layer that answers seller questions contextually without leaving the app.

### Mockup Blank Bank
A library of clean product blank images available within the app for building mockups and listing images without needing external photo assets.

### Mockup Playground
An in-app mockup creation tool powered by Photoroom and Nano Banana Pro. Sellers will be able to place their designs onto product photos, adjust composition, and export mockup images suitable for listings and social media — all without leaving Completeful.

### Profit Calculator
A margin modeling tool that lets sellers input a retail price and see the resulting net profit after Completeful production costs and platform fees are deducted. Will be accessible from both the product detail page and the listing creation flow.

### Store Health Page (Quality Analyzer)
A dashboard that evaluates the health of a seller's connected store listings — flagging weak titles, missing infographics, pricing below recommended margins, or products without sample orders. Provides actionable recommendations to improve listing quality and conversion.

## Planned (Future)

These items are on the product radar but do not yet have committed development timelines:

- TikTok Shop listing creation from within the app
- WooCommerce listing creation from within the app
- Expanded earnings analytics (revenue trends, top-selling products, margin by category)
- Bulk listing tools for high-volume catalog management
- Automated reorder and restocking alerts for sellers using managed inventory models

## Notes for Internal Use

Roadmap priorities are set by the Completeful leadership team. Features in development do not have guaranteed ship dates until they reach an active sprint. For questions about feature timelines or to request a feature, contact the product team directly or reach out at support@completeful.com.
