## Overview

Keeping your Completeful account secure protects your store, your orders, and your business. This page covers the steps you should take to keep your account safe and explains how Completeful handles your data.

## Securing Your Account

### Strong Password Practices

- Use a password that is at least 12 characters long and includes a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols.
- Do not reuse a password from another service.
- Change your password immediately if you believe it has been compromised. Go to Account > Settings > Change Password.

### Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

We strongly recommend enabling two-factor authentication on your account. When 2FA is active, you are prompted for a one-time verification code each time you log in from a new device.

To enable 2FA:

1. Go to Account > Settings > Security.
2. Select Enable Two-Factor Authentication.
3. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator or Authy).
4. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm setup.
5. Save your backup codes in a secure place in case you lose access to your authenticator app.

### Recognizing Phishing Attempts

Completeful will never ask for your password via email, chat, or phone. If you receive a message claiming to be from Completeful that asks for your login credentials, do not respond. Report suspicious messages to support@completeful.com.

## Managing Account Access

If you work with a team, avoid sharing your login credentials. Contact our support team at support@completeful.com to learn about access options for multiple users.

If you need to log out of all active sessions (for example, if you believe your account was accessed without your permission), go to Account > Settings > Security > Log Out All Sessions.

## Your Privacy and Data

Completeful collects the information needed to operate your account and process your orders. We do not sell your personal information to third parties. For full details on what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user, see our Privacy Policy Summary.

To request a copy of your account data or to close your account, contact us at support@completeful.com.

## Lost Access

If you cannot log in:

1. Click "Forgot Password" on the login screen.
2. Enter your account email address.
3. Check your inbox for a password reset link (it expires in 30 minutes).
4. If you no longer have access to that email address, contact support@completeful.com and we will verify your identity before assisting with account recovery.
