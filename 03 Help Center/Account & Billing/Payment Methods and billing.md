## Overview

Completeful charges your payment method automatically when an order is fulfilled. This page explains which payment methods are accepted, how billing works, and how to manage your payment settings.

## Accepted Payment Methods

Completeful accepts the following:

- Major credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- PayPal
- ACH bank transfers (available for accounts with established billing history; contact sales@completeful.com to inquire)

All payment information is stored and processed securely. Completeful does not store your full card number on our servers; payment data is handled by our PCI-compliant payment processor.

## How Billing Works

### Per-Order Billing

Your payment method is charged each time an order is produced and shipped. The charge covers the base production cost plus shipping for that order. You will receive an email receipt after each charge.

### Billing Timing

Orders are typically charged within 24 to 48 hours of production completion. For orders that include personalization or custom work, billing may occur at the start of production once the artwork is confirmed.

### Reviewing Your Charges

To view all charges on your account:

1. Log in and go to Account > Billing.
2. Select Billing History to see a full list of charges with dates and amounts.
3. Click any charge to view its detailed receipt, including the order number, product, and shipping cost.

You can download receipts individually or export a full billing history as a CSV.

## Updating Your Payment Method

To add or change a payment method:

1. Go to Account > Settings > Payment Methods.
2. Click Add Payment Method and enter your new card or account details.
3. Set it as your default if you want it used for future charges.
4. Remove old payment methods you no longer use.

If a charge to your payment method fails, we will notify you by email and retry the charge. If the retry fails, order production may pause until the payment issue is resolved. Update your payment method promptly to avoid delays.

## Billing Disputes

If you believe a charge is incorrect, contact us at support@completeful.com within 30 days of the charge date. Include your order number and a description of the issue. Our team will review the charge and respond within 2 business days.

For questions about a charge on your bank or card statement, you can also call the number on the back of your card, but reaching out to us first is usually the fastest way to resolve an issue.
