## Overview

As a seller using Completeful, you are responsible for collecting and remitting any applicable sales tax for the orders you process through your store. Completeful charges you the base cost of production and fulfillment; the tax obligations on sales between you and your customers are yours to manage.

## What Completeful Provides

- **Transaction records:** Your Completeful account dashboard maintains a full history of every order, including the production cost charged to you. You can export these records at any time from the Orders section.
- **Annual billing summaries:** If you need a summary of all charges for a given calendar year (for example, to provide to an accountant), you can export a CSV of your billing history from Account > Billing > Download Statement.
- **Business receipts:** Each charge to your payment method generates a receipt you can access under Account > Billing > Receipts. Receipts include the date, amount, and a line-item breakdown of the order.

## Resale Certificates and Tax Exemption

If your business holds a valid resale certificate in your state, you may be eligible for sales tax exemption on your Completeful production charges. To apply for tax-exempt status:

1. Log in and go to Account > Settings > Tax Exemption.
2. Upload a copy of your current, state-issued resale or exemption certificate.
3. Submit the form. Our team will review it and confirm your status within 3 to 5 business days.
4. Once approved, future charges will reflect your exempt status.

Tax-exempt status applies prospectively; it cannot be applied retroactively to past charges.

## Collecting Sales Tax from Your Customers

Most major platforms that Completeful integrates with (Shopify, WooCommerce, Etsy, TikTok Shop) have built-in tools to help you collect and remit sales tax. We recommend:

- Enabling automatic tax calculation in your storefront settings.
- Consulting a tax professional if your sales span multiple states, as nexus rules vary.
- Reviewing platform-specific tax guides provided by Shopify, Etsy, and WooCommerce for the most current requirements.

## Questions

For billing-related questions, reach out to our support team at support@completeful.com. For tax advice specific to your business, consult a licensed accountant or tax professional.
