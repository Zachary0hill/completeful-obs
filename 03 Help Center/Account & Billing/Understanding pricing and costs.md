## Overview

Completeful uses a simple, transparent pricing model: you pay the production and fulfillment cost, and you keep everything above that. There are no monthly fees, no setup costs, and no minimum order quantities.

## How Pricing Works

### Base Production Cost

Every product in our catalog has a listed base cost. This is the amount Completeful charges you to produce and fulfill that item. Base costs cover:

- Materials and printing or manufacturing
- Quality control
- Packaging
- Fulfillment handling

You can view the current base cost for any item on its product page inside the Completeful app.

### Your Selling Price and Profit Margin

You set your own retail price. Your profit is the difference between what your customer pays you and what Completeful charges you for production and fulfillment.

**Example:**
- Base cost (what Completeful charges you): $18.00
- Your retail price (what you charge your customer): $36.00
- Your margin: $18.00 per order

There are no royalty splits or percentage-based fees taken from your revenue. What you set is what you earn above cost.

### Shipping Costs

Shipping is charged separately at the time of fulfillment. Rates depend on the destination, carrier, and package weight. You can choose to pass shipping costs on to your customers or include them in your product pricing.

Flat-rate and tiered shipping options are available. Current shipping rates are displayed during product setup and at checkout within the app.

### No Hidden Fees

Completeful does not charge:
- Monthly subscription fees
- Per-store setup fees
- Listing fees
- Order minimums or minimums-related surcharges

The only costs you incur are production costs for orders that are actually placed and any applicable shipping.

## Viewing Your Costs

To see the full cost breakdown for any product:

1. Log in to your Completeful account.
2. Navigate to the Catalog section.
3. Select the product you want to review.
4. The product detail page shows the current base cost, available variants, and estimated shipping ranges.

## Tips for Setting Competitive Prices

- Research what comparable products sell for on platforms like Etsy and Shopify.
- Factor in any platform selling fees (for example, Etsy transaction fees or Shopify payment processing fees) when calculating your net margin.
- Consider bundling items or offering free shipping thresholds to increase average order value.
- Use your cost data to identify your highest-margin products and prioritize promoting those.

## Questions About Pricing

If you have questions about a specific product's cost or need help understanding a charge on your account, contact us at support@completeful.com.
