## Overview

Your Completeful wallet is the balance associated with your account. It is used to cover production and fulfillment costs and can be funded in advance or charged per order. This page explains how your wallet works and how credits or balances are managed.

## How the Wallet Works

When you add funds to your wallet, those funds are applied to future production charges rather than billing your card individually for each order. This can be useful for high-volume sellers who prefer predictable, pre-funded billing.

If you prefer not to pre-fund, orders are charged directly to your default payment method at the time of fulfillment. Both billing models use the same payment methods (credit/debit card, PayPal, ACH where available).

## Adding Funds

To add funds to your wallet:

1. Log in and go to Account > Billing > Wallet.
2. Click Add Funds.
3. Enter the amount you want to add and select your payment method.
4. Confirm the transaction. Funds are available immediately.

Minimum deposit amounts may apply. The current minimum is displayed on the Add Funds screen.

## Wallet Balance and Low-Balance Alerts

Your current wallet balance is visible at the top of the Billing section. You can set a low-balance alert to receive an email notification when your balance drops below a threshold you define:

1. Go to Account > Billing > Wallet > Alert Settings.
2. Enter your preferred threshold amount.
3. Save. You will receive an email alert when your balance falls below that amount.

You can also enable auto-reload, which automatically adds a set amount to your wallet when the balance drops below your threshold.

## Credits and Adjustments

If Completeful issues a credit to your account (for example, as a resolution to a production issue), it will appear in your wallet balance and will be applied to future orders. Credits are visible in your billing history with the label "Credit Applied."

Credits do not expire but are non-transferable and have no cash value.

## Requesting a Wallet Refund

If you have a positive wallet balance and want a refund of unused funds, contact support@completeful.com. Wallet refunds are reviewed on a case-by-case basis and are processed to the original funding source.

## Questions

For wallet or billing questions, contact support@completeful.com and include your account email address.
