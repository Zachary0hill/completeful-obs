## Who We Are

Completeful is a US-based print-on-demand, fulfillment, and manufacturing company headquartered in Lafayette, Louisiana. We exist to make it easy for independent creators, entrepreneurs, and brands to sell custom products without taking on inventory, equipment, or production overhead.

Our tagline says it plainly: **You Design and Sell It, We Create and Fulfill It.**

## What Makes Us Different

Most print-on-demand companies outsource their production to third-party manufacturers. Completeful does not. We produce orders in-house at our own facility in Lafayette, LA. That means:

- **Tighter quality control.** Our team manages every step of production under one roof.
- **Faster domestic fulfillment.** No middlemen between your order and the people shipping it.
- **Made in the USA.** Products are manufactured on American soil by our own team.
- **Accountability.** When something is wrong, there is no third party to blame. We own it and fix it.

## What We Offer

Completeful is more than just print-on-demand. Our services include:

- **POD (Print-on-Demand):** Custom products from a catalog of 500 to 900+ items, produced per order with no minimums.
- **3PL (Third-Party Logistics), Warehousing & Kitting:** For brands that carry inventory, we store, pick, pack, and ship on their behalf.
- **Personalization:** Products customized with individual customer names, photos, or other unique elements.
- **Private Label:** Branding and packaging solutions for sellers building their own brand identity.
- **Wholesale and Bulk:** Large-quantity production for retail, events, and corporate clients.

## Our Catalog

We offer 500 to 900+ products across apparel, home goods, accessories, and more. Our catalog is continuously expanding as we add new product categories and manufacturing capabilities.

## Transparent Pricing, No Minimums

We believe in giving sellers real information to make real decisions. Every product in our catalog lists its base production cost upfront. There are no monthly fees, no setup costs, and no order minimums. You only pay when an order is placed.

## Integrations

Completeful connects directly with Shopify, Etsy, WooCommerce, and TikTok Shop so your online store and our production floor work together automatically.

## Our Values

We are a production-first company. We believe in:
- Building things that work
- Being honest about what we can and cannot do
- Supporting independent sellers with tools and service that actually help
- Making products we are proud of, right here in Louisiana
