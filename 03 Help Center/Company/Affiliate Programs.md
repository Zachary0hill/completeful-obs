## Overview

Completeful's affiliate program lets you earn a commission by referring new sellers to the platform. If you love using Completeful and want to share it with your network — other creators, shop owners, or entrepreneurs — you can get paid for every qualified referral.

## How the Program Works

When you join the affiliate program, you receive a unique referral link. When someone signs up for Completeful through your link and begins placing orders, you earn a commission on their production activity.

The program is open to anyone: current Completeful sellers, content creators, business coaches, or anyone with an audience of potential sellers.

## Commission Structure

Affiliates earn a percentage-based commission on the production costs generated by referred sellers. Commissions are:

- Calculated monthly based on qualifying activity from your referred accounts
- Paid out once your balance reaches the minimum payout threshold
- Paid via the payout method you set up in your affiliate dashboard

Specific commission rates and payout thresholds are detailed in your affiliate dashboard after you join. Rates may vary based on your referral volume or partnership tier.

## Getting Started

1. Visit completeful.com and look for the Affiliate Program link in the footer, or contact sales@completeful.com to express interest.
2. Complete the affiliate application. Applications are typically reviewed within 3 to 5 business days.
3. Once approved, you will receive access to your affiliate dashboard, your unique referral link, and any available promotional assets (banners, copy, etc.).
4. Share your link on your website, social media, email list, or anywhere your audience will see it.
5. Track your referrals, clicks, and commissions in real time through your affiliate dashboard.

## Tips for Successful Referrals

- Share your personal experience with Completeful. Authentic recommendations convert better than generic promotions.
- Target audiences who are already interested in starting an online shop, dropshipping, or print-on-demand businesses.
- Highlight Completeful's key advantages: US-based in-house manufacturing, no order minimums, transparent pricing, and integrations with Shopify, Etsy, TikTok Shop, and WooCommerce.

## Questions

For questions about the affiliate program, commissions, or your account status, email sales@completeful.com. Our team is happy to help you make the most of the program.
