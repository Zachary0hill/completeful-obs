## Overview

Completeful is a US-based print-on-demand and fulfillment company headquartered in Lafayette, Louisiana. We design, manufacture, and ship products in-house, which means our team does the real work — from production floor to software to customer success.

If you are interested in joining a growing team that is building something hands-on and meaningful for independent sellers across the country, we want to hear from you.

## Who We Are Looking For

We hire across several disciplines as we grow, including:

- **Production & Operations:** Press operators, embroidery technicians, kitting and warehouse staff, quality control, and production supervisors at our Lafayette facility.
- **Technology & Product:** Software engineers, product designers, and QA testers working on the Completeful app and platform.
- **Customer Success & Support:** Support specialists who help sellers launch and grow their stores.
- **Sales & Partnerships:** People who build relationships with wholesale clients, brands, and platform partners.
- **Marketing & Creative:** Content creators, designers, and marketers who tell the Completeful story.

## Our Work Environment

Completeful is based in Lafayette, LA. Our production roles are on-site at our manufacturing facility. Some technology and support roles may have remote or hybrid flexibility. We are a practical, builder-first team — if you want to see the direct result of your work, you will see it here.

## How to Apply

Current open positions are listed on our website at completeful.com (look for the Careers page in the footer or navigation).

If you do not see a role that matches your background but believe you have something valuable to offer, you are welcome to send a resume and a short note about what you do and what interests you about Completeful to support@completeful.com with the subject line "Career Inquiry."

## What to Expect

1. Submit your application or inquiry through the website or by email.
2. A member of our team will review your submission and reach out if there is a potential fit.
3. Initial conversations are typically a short phone or video call.
4. Depending on the role, there may be a skills assessment, a tour of our facility, or a working interview.

We aim to respond to all applications within two weeks.
