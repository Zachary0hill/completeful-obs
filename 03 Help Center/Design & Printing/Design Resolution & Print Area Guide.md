# Design Resolution & Print Area Guide

Getting the right resolution and understanding print area boundaries for each product type will save you from blurry prints, cropped designs, and reprints. Use this guide as a reference when preparing artwork.

## Understanding DPI vs. Pixel Dimensions

DPI (dots per inch) describes how many pixels are packed into each inch of the printed output. The higher the DPI, the sharper the result — but only if you start with enough pixels.

The formula for the minimum pixel dimensions of your artwork:

**Pixel width = Print width (inches) x DPI**
**Pixel height = Print height (inches) x DPI**

**Example:** A standard front chest print on a t-shirt is approximately 12" x 14". At 300 DPI, your artwork canvas should be at least 3600 x 4200 pixels.

- **Minimum acceptable:** 150 DPI at print size
- **Recommended:** 300 DPI at print size
- **Large format (18"+ wide):** 150-200 DPI is acceptable

## Print Method and Area Reference

### DTG Apparel (T-Shirts, Hoodies, Tank Tops)

- **Chest print area:** Up to 14" wide x 16" tall (exact dimensions vary by garment size and style)
- **Full front:** Up to 15" wide x 18" tall on adult sizes
- **Back print:** Similar to front
- **Sleeve:** Up to 4" wide x 14" tall (check individual product specs)
- **Recommended canvas size:** 4500 x 5400 px at 300 DPI for a standard 15" x 18" print

DTG works on Gildan, Hanes, and Bella Canvas blanks. Prints on dark garments use a white underbase — keep this in mind for how your colors will appear.

### Sublimation — 11oz Mug

- **Print area:** Full wrap, approximately 8.5" wide x 3.75" tall (210mm x 95mm)
- **Recommended canvas:** 2550 x 1125 px at 300 DPI
- **Notes:** The design wraps all the way around. Leave 0.25" safe margin on all edges to account for placement variance.

### Sublimation — Tote Bag

- **Print area:** Typically 13" x 13" per side (front only or front and back depending on product)
- **Recommended canvas:** 3900 x 3900 px at 300 DPI
- **Notes:** Tote fabric is not perfectly flat during printing — avoid very fine detail near edges.

### Sublimation — Puzzle

- **Print area:** Varies by puzzle size (8" x 10", 11" x 14", 16" x 20")
- **For 11" x 14":** 3300 x 4200 px at 300 DPI
- **Notes:** Bold, high-contrast designs work best for puzzles because pieces are cut across the image.

### UV Printing — Hard Goods (Ornaments, Plaques, Signs)

- **Print area:** Varies significantly by product — check individual product specs in the catalog
- **Recommended resolution:** 300 DPI at print size
- **Notes:** UV printing handles fine detail well. Bright colors and detailed photography both work.

### Laser Engraving — Wood and Metal

- **Resolution requirements:** SVG or 300 DPI raster at print size
- **Color note:** Laser engraving is single-tone (engraved vs. not engraved). Designs are interpreted as grayscale — lighter areas engrave shallower, darker areas engrave deeper.
- **Best file type:** SVG with paths, or high-contrast black and white PNG

### Embroidery

- **Maximum stitch area:** Typically 4" x 4" for hat patches, up to 14" x 14" for jacket backs (check specific product)
- **File requirements:** Submit a clean, high-contrast PNG or SVG. Completeful's embroidery team converts artwork to a stitch file. Intricate designs with gradients or very fine lines may be simplified.
- **Minimum detail size:** Avoid text smaller than 0.25" tall — stitching small text is not possible at high quality.

## Safe Zone and Bleed Summary

| Zone | Measurement | Purpose |
|------|------------|---------|
| Bleed (extend into) | 0.125" / 3mm beyond edge | Prevents white gaps at cut lines |
| Safe area (keep inside) | 0.25" / 6mm from edge | Keeps critical content from being cut |

Always use the guides in the Completeful product builder to confirm your design falls within both zones before finalizing.

## Where to Find Exact Specs

Each product page in the Completeful catalog includes a **Print Specifications** section with exact print area dimensions, bleed requirements, and recommended file size. Check there before starting a new design to save yourself rework.
