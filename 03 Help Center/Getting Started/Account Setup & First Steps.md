# Account Setup & First Steps

This guide covers everything you need to do after creating your Completeful account to get your profile, billing, and preferences in order before you start publishing products.

## Create Your Account

1. Go to completeful.com and click **Get Started**.
2. Enter your email address and create a password, or continue with Google.
3. Check your email for a verification link and click it to confirm your account.
4. Log in and you will land on your Completeful dashboard.

## Complete Your Business Profile

1. Navigate to **Settings > Profile**.
2. Enter your business name. This is the name that appears on packing slips and outbound correspondence if you are using Completeful's private label fulfillment.
3. Add your business address. This is used for return address and compliance purposes.
4. Save your changes.

## Add a Payment Method

Completeful charges your payment method when an order is placed and moves into production. No charge is made until a real customer order arrives.

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**.
2. Click **Add Payment Method** and enter your credit or debit card details.
3. Optionally, set a billing notification threshold so you are alerted if your monthly charges exceed a certain amount.

You can add multiple cards and designate one as the default. If a charge fails, Completeful will notify you and hold the order until payment is resolved.

## Set Your Default Shipping Preferences

1. Go to **Settings > Shipping**.
2. Review the default carrier options. Completeful ships from Lafayette, Louisiana using major US carriers.
3. Set up domestic and international shipping rates. You can offer free shipping (built into your product price), flat-rate shipping, or pass carrier-calculated rates to customers.

## Configure Notifications

1. Go to **Settings > Notifications**.
2. Choose which events trigger an email to you: new orders, production updates, shipment tracking, and any production issues.
3. Make sure the email address on your account is one you check regularly.

## Install the Completeful App

The Completeful mobile app lets you monitor orders, check production status, and manage your products from your phone.

1. Search for **Completeful** in the App Store or Google Play.
2. Log in with the same credentials as your account on completeful.com.
3. Enable push notifications to stay updated on order status without checking email.

## Next Steps

With your account configured, you are ready to connect your store and start building products. See **Connect Your eCommerce Store** for platform-specific instructions, and **Publish Your First Product** to create your first listing.

If you run into any issues during setup, contact support@completeful.com with your account email and a description of the problem.
