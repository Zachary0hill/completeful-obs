# Connect Your eCommerce Store

Completeful integrates directly with Shopify, Etsy, TikTok Shop, and WooCommerce. Once your store is connected, orders automatically flow into Completeful for production and fulfillment — no manual importing required.

## Before You Start

- Make sure you have admin access to the store you want to connect.
- You will need to be logged into your Completeful account at completeful.com or in the Completeful app.

## Connect Shopify

1. From your Completeful dashboard, go to **Integrations** and select **Shopify**.
2. Enter your Shopify store URL (example: yourstore.myshopify.com) and click **Connect**.
3. You will be redirected to Shopify to authorize Completeful. Review the permissions and click **Install app**.
4. Once redirected back to Completeful, your store will show as connected.
5. Completeful will sync your existing products and pull in new orders automatically.

## Connect Etsy

1. From **Integrations**, select **Etsy**.
2. Click **Connect with Etsy** and log in to your Etsy seller account when prompted.
3. Authorize Completeful to access your shop.
4. Once connected, map your Etsy listings to Completeful products so the platform knows which product to produce when an order arrives.

**Tip:** Etsy does not support automatic price syncing, so update your Etsy listing prices separately to match your desired margin.

## Connect TikTok Shop

1. From **Integrations**, select **TikTok Shop**.
2. Click **Connect** and log in to your TikTok Shop seller center when prompted.
3. Authorize the connection and return to Completeful.
4. Map your TikTok Shop products to Completeful catalog items.

**Note:** TikTok Shop requires product listings to be created in TikTok Shop first. Use Completeful's product builder to generate mockup images and then upload them to your TikTok Shop listing.

## Connect WooCommerce

1. From **Integrations**, select **WooCommerce**.
2. Enter your WooCommerce store URL and generate an API key inside WooCommerce under **Settings > Advanced > REST API**.
3. Paste the API key and API secret into Completeful and click **Connect**.
4. Completeful will verify the connection and begin syncing orders.

## Confirming Your Connection

After connecting any platform, check that:

- Your store name appears in the Integrations list with an **Active** status.
- A test order (or sample order) routes correctly to Completeful.
- Shipping rates and fulfillment settings are configured under **Settings > Shipping**.

## Troubleshooting

If your connection shows as **Error** or **Inactive**, try disconnecting and reconnecting. If the issue persists, contact support@completeful.com with your store URL and platform name.
