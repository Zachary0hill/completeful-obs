# How Completeful Works

Completeful handles everything between your customer placing an order and the package arriving at their door. You focus on designing products and running your store. Completeful handles production, packing, and shipping.

## The Core Model: You Design and Sell, We Create and Fulfill

Completeful operates on a simple principle: you are the seller and the creative. Completeful is your production and logistics partner. There are no upfront inventory costs, no minimum order quantities, and no warehouse for you to manage.

### Step 1 — You Design

Create artwork for any product in the Completeful catalog. The catalog includes 500 to 900+ items: t-shirts, hoodies, mugs, tote bags, puzzles, ornaments, wood signs, metal prints, phone cases, and much more. Use the Completeful product builder to upload your design, position it on the product, and preview how it will look when printed.

### Step 2 — You Sell

Publish your products to your store. Completeful integrates with Shopify, Etsy, TikTok Shop, and WooCommerce. Once published, your listings are live and ready for customers to buy. You set your own retail prices and keep the difference between what the customer pays and Completeful's base production cost.

### Step 3 — We Create

When a customer places an order in your store, it flows automatically into Completeful's production system. Our team in Lafayette, Louisiana produces the item in-house using the appropriate print method for that product:

- **DTG (direct-to-garment)** for apparel on Gildan, Hanes, and Bella Canvas blanks
- **Sublimation** for items like 11oz mugs, tote bags, and puzzles
- **UV printing** for hard-surface products
- **Embroidery** for hats, bags, and apparel
- **Laser engraving** for wood and metal products

Everything is made at our 228,000 sq ft US facility. No overseas outsourcing. No middlemen.

### Step 4 — We Fulfill

Once production is complete, Completeful packs the order and ships it directly to your customer using your store's branding. Tracking information is automatically sent back to your store and, depending on the platform, forwarded to the customer.

## Private Label and Personalization

Completeful supports private label fulfillment — your packaging, your brand, your customer experience. You can also offer personalized products where each order includes custom text, names, or individual buyer details. This is especially popular for Etsy and TikTok Shop sellers.

## No Order Minimums, Transparent Pricing

You never need to commit to a bulk order. Every item is made on demand when a real customer order arrives. Pricing is transparent — you see the exact base cost for every product in the catalog before you publish anything.

## Getting Help

If you have questions at any point, the Completeful support team is available at support@completeful.com. For sales and partnership questions, reach out to sales@completeful.com.
