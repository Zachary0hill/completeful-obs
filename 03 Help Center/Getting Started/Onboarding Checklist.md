# Onboarding Checklist

Use this checklist to get your Completeful account ready to take orders. You can move through these steps at your own pace, but completing all of them before you go live will prevent common issues with your first orders.

## Step 1 — Create Your Account

1. Go to completeful.com and sign up for a free account.
2. Verify your email address using the confirmation link sent to your inbox.
3. Log in and complete your profile, including your business name and contact information.

## Step 2 — Connect Your Store

1. From your Completeful dashboard, navigate to **Integrations**.
2. Select your platform: Shopify, Etsy, TikTok Shop, or WooCommerce.
3. Follow the on-screen authorization flow to connect your store. See the full guide in **Connect Your eCommerce Store** for platform-specific instructions.
4. Confirm the connection is active — your store name should appear with a green status indicator.

## Step 3 — Set Up Shipping

1. Go to **Settings > Shipping** and review the default shipping profiles.
2. Set your shipping rates for domestic and international orders. You can pass actual carrier rates to customers or set a flat rate.
3. If you are on Etsy, make sure your Etsy shipping profiles are aligned to avoid overcharging or undercharging customers.

## Step 4 — Add Payment Information

1. Navigate to **Settings > Billing** and add a payment method.
2. Completeful charges your card when an order is placed and moves into production. Make sure your payment method is current to avoid fulfillment delays.

## Step 5 — Browse the Catalog and Create Your First Product

1. Open the **Catalog** to explore 500 to 900+ products across categories including apparel, mugs, totes, puzzles, home goods, and more.
2. Select a product and use the product builder to upload your artwork and configure variants (size, color, etc.).
3. Review the print preview before publishing.

## Step 6 — Publish to Your Store

1. When your product is ready, click **Publish** to push it to your connected store.
2. Verify that the listing appears correctly on your storefront, including images, pricing, and variants.

## Step 7 — Place a Test Order

1. Order a sample of your product at cost before selling to customers.
2. Check the print quality, colors, and packaging.
3. If you need adjustments, update your artwork and reorder before going live.

## You Are Ready

Once you have completed these steps your store is connected, your products are live, and Completeful will automatically receive and fulfill orders as they come in. For next steps, explore the **Design & Printing** section to learn how to get the best print results.
