# Publish Your First Product

Publishing your first product on Completeful takes just a few minutes. This guide walks you through picking a product, uploading your artwork, configuring variants, and pushing the listing live to your connected store.

## Step 1 — Choose a Product from the Catalog

1. From your dashboard, click **Catalog**.
2. Browse by category or use the search bar to find a product. Completeful carries 500 to 900+ products including apparel, mugs, totes, puzzles, home goods, wood items, metal products, and more.
3. Click a product to open its detail page. Review the print method (DTG, sublimation, UV print, embroidery, or laser engraving), available sizes and colors, and base price.
4. Click **Create Product** when you are ready.

## Step 2 — Upload Your Artwork

1. In the product builder, click **Upload Artwork** and select your design file.
2. Use a PNG with a transparent background for best results. Minimum resolution is 150 DPI at print size; 300 DPI is recommended for most products.
3. Position and scale your design using the on-screen editor. Use the print area guidelines to make sure nothing important is cut off.
4. For products with multiple print areas (front, back, sleeve), switch between areas using the tabs at the top of the editor.

## Step 3 — Configure Variants and Pricing

1. Select which sizes, colors, or styles you want to offer.
2. Set your retail price for each variant. Your profit margin is the difference between your retail price and Completeful's base price.
3. Completeful does not have order minimums, so you can offer as many or as few variants as you like.

## Step 4 — Add Product Details

1. Enter a product title and description. These can be edited directly in your store after publishing, but starting with something clear saves time.
2. Select or generate mockup images. Mockups show your design on the product and are used as listing photos.
3. Add any personalization options if the product supports custom text or individual name customization.

## Step 5 — Publish to Your Store

1. Click **Publish**.
2. Select the connected store you want to publish to (Shopify, Etsy, TikTok Shop, or WooCommerce).
3. Review the final summary and confirm.
4. The product will appear in your store within a few minutes. Check your storefront to verify the listing looks correct.

## What Happens Next

When a customer orders this product, the order is automatically sent to Completeful for production. Your design is printed, packed, and shipped from our facility in Lafayette, Louisiana — you do not need to do anything for each individual order.

For tips on getting the best print quality, see **Artwork Upload Guidelines** and **Design Resolution & Print Area Guide**.
