## Overview

Completeful produces custom products based on the artwork and designs you provide. Because we create physical goods, the content we produce carries real-world responsibility. This policy explains what you can and cannot submit for production and how we handle intellectual property concerns.

## Your Responsibility for the Content You Submit

When you upload artwork, a design, or any other content to Completeful for production, you confirm that:

- You own the content outright, or
- You hold a valid license or permission to use it commercially, including for production and resale, and
- The content does not infringe on any trademark, copyright, patent, or other intellectual property right held by a third party

Completeful is not responsible for verifying the ownership of the designs you submit, but we will act promptly if we receive a credible complaint.

## Prohibited Content

We will not produce items featuring:

- Content that infringes on a registered trademark (e.g., logos, brand names, or slogans owned by another company without a license)
- Content protected by copyright that you do not own or have rights to (e.g., band logos, movie characters, sports team marks, celebrity likenesses without consent)
- Hate speech, content that promotes violence, or content that targets individuals or groups based on protected characteristics
- Explicit sexual content involving minors under any circumstances
- Content that is defamatory, threatening, or harassing toward an identifiable person
- Content that violates any applicable federal, state, or local law

## Common IP Mistakes to Avoid

**Fan art and unlicensed designs:** Even if an illustration is your original artwork, using a character, mascot, or other protected imagery from a commercial franchise without a license is an infringement.

**Font licensing:** Not all fonts are free for commercial use. If you use a font in your design, verify that your license covers the type of use you intend.

**Stock photos and clipart:** Images downloaded from the internet are not automatically free to use on products you sell. Use only images from sources that explicitly grant commercial use rights, such as certain Creative Commons licenses or licensed stock services.

**Sports and entertainment marks:** Team names, logos, player names and numbers, and league marks are typically trademarked. Selling products with these elements without a license is not permitted.

## What Happens If You Submit Infringing Content

If Completeful identifies content that appears to infringe on intellectual property rights, or if we receive a valid complaint from a rights holder:

1. We will pause production on the affected order.
2. We will notify you by email.
3. If the claim is valid, the order will be cancelled and a refund will not be issued for production costs already incurred.
4. Repeat violations may result in suspension or termination of your account.

## Filing an IP Complaint

If you are a rights holder and believe Completeful has produced or is producing content that infringes your intellectual property, contact us at support@completeful.com with the subject line "IP Complaint." Include:

- Your name and contact information
- A description of the intellectual property you own and the claimed infringement
- The specific product(s) at issue
- A statement that your complaint is made in good faith

We take IP complaints seriously and will investigate promptly.

## Safe Harbor

Completeful operates as a service provider for sellers and follows a notice-and-takedown process consistent with applicable US law. We do not have advance knowledge of every design submitted by every seller, and we act in good faith to remove infringing content when it is identified.

## Questions

For questions about this policy, email support@completeful.com.
