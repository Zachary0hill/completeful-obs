## Overview

This page is a plain-language summary of how Completeful collects, uses, and protects your information. The full Privacy Policy is available at completeful.com. If there is any conflict between this summary and the full policy, the full policy governs.

## What Information We Collect

### Account Information

When you create a Completeful account, we collect:
- Your name and email address
- Your business name (if applicable)
- Your billing address and payment method details
- Your account password (stored in encrypted form; we never see it in plain text)

### Order and Business Data

When you use Completeful to fulfill orders, we collect:
- Order details (product, quantity, specifications)
- Shipping addresses for your customers
- Artwork and design files you upload
- Billing and transaction records

### Usage Data

We collect information about how you use the Completeful app and website, including:
- Pages visited and features used
- Device type, browser, and operating system
- IP address and general location

This data helps us improve the platform and troubleshoot issues.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Process and fulfill your orders
- Charge your payment method for services rendered
- Communicate with you about your account and orders
- Provide customer support
- Improve our products, services, and platform
- Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## Sharing Your Information

We share information only as needed to operate our services:
- **Shipping carriers** (USPS, UPS, FedEx) receive the shipping addresses for your orders
- **Payment processors** handle your billing information securely
- **Platform integrations** (Shopify, Etsy, WooCommerce, TikTok Shop) receive order and product data you authorize when connecting your account
- **Legal authorities** if required by law, valid legal process, or to protect our rights

## Your Customers' Data

When we fulfill orders for your customers, we receive their shipping addresses and, in some cases, their names. We use this information only to complete fulfillment. We do not market to your customers or sell their data.

## Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), encrypted storage of sensitive credentials, and access controls that limit who within our organization can access your account information.

## Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
- Object to or restrict certain types of processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact support@completeful.com.

## Data Retention

We retain your account and order data for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period after closure, as required for tax, legal, and operational purposes.

## Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes to our privacy practices by email or by posting a notice in your account.

## Questions

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact support@completeful.com.
