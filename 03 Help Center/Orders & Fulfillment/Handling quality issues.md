# Handling Quality Issues

Completeful stands behind the quality of every item produced at our Lafayette, Louisiana facility. If a product arrives damaged, misprinted, or defective, this guide explains how to report the issue and what to expect next.

## What Qualifies as a Quality Issue

A quality issue is a problem caused by production, not by the design file you submitted. Common qualifying issues include:

- Blurry or low-quality print that does not match the approved design
- Wrong product, size, or color shipped
- Printing on the wrong area of the garment or product
- Fading, cracking, or peeling immediately upon receipt
- Stitching errors on embroidered products
- Physical damage to the product (torn fabric, cracked mug, broken item)
- Package arrived empty or with a missing item

## What Does Not Qualify

Completeful produces your item based on the artwork you uploaded and the product configuration you set. The following are not covered as quality issues:

- Blurry print caused by a low-resolution file submitted by you (see **Artwork Upload Guidelines**)
- Color variation expected between different print methods or substrates
- Design placement that matches the mockup you approved
- Items damaged after delivery (customer misuse or washing errors)
- Customer sizing errors (the customer ordered the wrong size)

If you are unsure whether an issue is on Completeful's end or the artwork side, contact support and include photos — the team will review and advise.

## How to Report a Quality Issue

1. **Gather evidence.** Take clear, well-lit photos of the issue. Include the full item, a close-up of the problem area, and the packing slip or order label if possible.
2. **Open a support ticket.** Email support@completeful.com with the subject line: "Quality Issue — Order #[your order number]."
3. **Include the following in your email:**
   - Your Completeful order number
   - The specific product and variant (size, color)
   - A description of the issue
   - 2 to 4 photos of the defect
4. **Wait for confirmation.** The support team typically responds within 1 to 2 business days to confirm the issue and approve the resolution.

## Resolution Options

Depending on the issue, Completeful will offer one or more of the following:

- **Reprint and reship:** A new item is produced and sent at no charge to you or your customer.
- **Refund:** If a reprint is not practical (the customer no longer wants the item, for example), a refund of the production cost is issued to your account.
- **Partial credit:** For issues that are borderline (slight color variation within normal tolerance), a partial credit toward a future order may be offered.

## Timelines for Resolution

- Quality reports must be submitted within **30 days** of the delivery date.
- Once a reprint is approved, it enters production with the same priority as a new order. Reprints typically ship within the standard production window (see **Production & Shipping Timelines**).

## Tips for Preventing Quality Issues

- Always order a physical sample before listing a new product. This lets you catch file issues before real customers are affected.
- Use the file guidelines in **Artwork Upload Guidelines** to ensure your files are print-ready.
- Review your design at 100% zoom before uploading to catch any blurriness or artifacts at actual print size.

For urgent issues or orders involving multiple items, call or email the support team directly at support@completeful.com.
