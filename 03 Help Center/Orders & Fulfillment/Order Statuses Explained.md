# Order Statuses Explained

Every order in your Completeful dashboard moves through a series of statuses from the moment it comes in to the moment it is delivered. Knowing what each status means helps you answer customer questions accurately and spot problems early.

## Order Status Overview

### Pending

The order has been received from your store but has not yet been confirmed for production. This typically means:

- Payment is still being verified, or
- The order is waiting for a personalization detail (custom name or text) that has not yet been submitted.

Orders usually leave Pending status within a few hours of being placed. If an order stays in Pending for more than 24 hours, check whether any required personalization fields are unfilled.

### In Production

The order has been confirmed and is being produced at Completeful's facility in Lafayette, Louisiana. Your item is being printed, embroidered, engraved, or assembled depending on the product type.

You cannot cancel or modify an order once it reaches In Production. If you need to make a change, contact support@completeful.com immediately — the team may be able to intervene before the item is completed, but this is not guaranteed.

### Quality Check

Some orders go through an internal quality review before being packed. This is part of Completeful's standard production process and does not require any action from you. Orders in this status typically move to Shipped within a few hours.

### Shipped

The order has left the facility and is in the hands of the carrier. A tracking number is now available on the order detail page in your dashboard and has been sent back to your connected store.

The tracking link will be live within a few hours of the status update as the carrier scans the package.

### Delivered

The carrier has confirmed delivery to the destination address. If a customer reports they did not receive the package after this status is shown, ask them to check with neighbors and in any secure delivery spots. If the package is still missing, contact support@completeful.com to open a missing package investigation.

### Cancelled

The order was cancelled before production began. This may happen if:

- You manually cancelled the order from your dashboard.
- Payment failed and was not resolved within the grace period.
- The order was flagged as a duplicate.

Cancelled orders are not charged. If an order was cancelled in error, you will need to place a new order.

### On Hold

The order has been paused, usually because of a payment issue, a product availability problem, or a missing detail. You will receive an email notification explaining why the order is on hold and what action is needed.

Resolve the issue promptly — on-hold orders can delay your delivery timeline.

### Refunded

A refund has been issued for this order, either because the order was cancelled or a quality issue was resolved with a refund rather than a reprint.

## Checking Order Status

1. Log in to your Completeful dashboard or open the Completeful app.
2. Go to **Orders**.
3. Use the status filter to view orders by their current status.
4. Click any order to see its full timeline, including timestamps for each status change and the current tracking information.

## Questions About a Specific Order

If an order is stuck in a status for longer than expected, or if you see a status that does not match what you expect, contact support@completeful.com with your order number and the team will investigate.
