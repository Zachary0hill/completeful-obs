# Production & Shipping Timelines

Understanding how long it takes to go from customer order to delivery helps you set accurate expectations on your listings and respond confidently to customer questions.

## How the Timeline Works

Every order goes through two phases: **production** (making the item) and **shipping** (getting it to the customer). Your total delivery time is the sum of both.

**Total delivery time = Production time + Shipping transit time**

## Production Times

Production time starts when your order is received by Completeful and the payment is confirmed. All products are made in-house at our 228,000 sq ft facility in Lafayette, Louisiana.

| Product Category | Typical Production Time |
|-----------------|------------------------|
| DTG Apparel (t-shirts, hoodies, tanks) | 2 to 4 business days |
| Sublimation (mugs, totes, puzzles) | 2 to 3 business days |
| UV Printed Hard Goods | 2 to 4 business days |
| Embroidered Products | 3 to 5 business days |
| Laser Engraved (wood, metal) | 3 to 5 business days |
| Personalized / Custom Text Items | Add 1 business day |
| Multi-item orders | May add 1 to 2 business days |

These are standard production windows. During high-volume periods (holiday season, November through December) add 1 to 3 business days to these estimates.

## Shipping Times (Domestic US)

Once an order ships from Lafayette, Louisiana, transit time depends on the shipping service selected at checkout.

| Shipping Method | Estimated Transit |
|----------------|------------------|
| Standard Ground | 4 to 7 business days |
| Expedited | 2 to 3 business days |
| Express / Overnight | 1 to 2 business days |

Actual transit times depend on the carrier and the destination. Remote addresses (Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, rural areas) may take 1 to 2 additional days.

## International Orders

International shipping is available to most countries. Transit times vary widely by destination:

- **Canada:** 7 to 14 business days
- **UK / Europe:** 10 to 21 business days
- **Australia / New Zealand:** 14 to 28 business days
- **Other regions:** 14 to 30+ business days

International orders may be subject to customs duties and import taxes. These are the buyer's responsibility and are not included in shipping costs.

## Rush Orders

If a customer needs a faster turnaround, contact support@completeful.com before placing the order to check whether a rush production slot is available. Rush production is not guaranteed but can sometimes be accommodated for an additional fee.

## Holiday Production Schedule

Production pauses on the following US holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. Orders placed the day before a holiday will begin production on the next business day.

During the holiday peak season (approximately November 1 through December 20), add buffer time to all estimates and communicate extended timelines to your customers proactively.

## Tracking Information

Once your order ships, tracking information is automatically sent back to your connected store (Shopify, Etsy, TikTok Shop, or WooCommerce) and will appear on your order detail page in the Completeful dashboard. Customers with email confirmation enabled will also receive tracking directly.

If tracking has not updated after 5 business days from the ship date, contact support@completeful.com with your order number.
