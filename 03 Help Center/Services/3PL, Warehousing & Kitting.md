## Overview

Completeful offers third-party logistics (3PL) services for brands that carry their own inventory. If you produce or source products outside of our print-on-demand catalog — or if you need to store inventory for faster fulfillment — our warehousing and kitting services let you use our Lafayette, Louisiana facility as your fulfillment hub.

## What Is 3PL?

Third-party logistics means outsourcing storage, picking, packing, and shipping to a fulfillment partner. Instead of managing a warehouse yourself, you send your inventory to Completeful and we handle the rest when orders come in.

This is particularly useful for brands that:
- Source finished goods from manufacturers and need somewhere to store and ship from
- Produce products in large runs (e.g., seasonal drops, crowdfunded products)
- Want faster fulfillment by pre-stocking inventory rather than producing per order
- Sell bundled or kitted products that require assembly before shipping

## Warehousing

Completeful provides secure, climate-appropriate storage for your inventory at our Lafayette facility. Once your products are received, we log them into our system so you can monitor inventory levels in real time through your account.

**How it works:**

1. Contact sales@completeful.com to discuss your storage needs, product types, and estimated volumes.
2. Receive an onboarding guide with instructions for labeling and shipping your inventory to us.
3. Ship your inventory to our Lafayette address. We receive, inspect, and count your goods upon arrival.
4. Your inventory is logged and visible in your account under Inventory Management.
5. When an order comes in through your connected sales channel, we pick and ship from your stored inventory.

Storage fees are based on the space your inventory occupies and are billed monthly. Rates are provided during onboarding.

## Kitting

Kitting is the process of assembling multiple items into a single ready-to-ship package. If your product consists of multiple components (for example, a gift set, a subscription box, or a bundled product), our team can assemble those kits before orders are placed so they are ready to ship immediately.

**Kitting services include:**
- Multi-item bundle assembly
- Insertion of packing materials, inserts, or branded elements
- Poly-bagging, boxing, or custom packaging
- Applying labels and barcodes to finished kits

Kitting fees depend on the complexity of the assembly and the number of components. Contact sales@completeful.com for a custom quote.

## Receiving and Inventory Tracking

Every inbound shipment is checked in by our receiving team. You will receive a confirmation once your inventory is logged. Discrepancies between what was shipped and what was received will be flagged and communicated to you promptly.

Your inventory levels are visible in your Completeful account and can be exported at any time.

## Who This Is Best For

- Brands launching a product line who need reliable US fulfillment without a warehouse lease
- Sellers transitioning from self-fulfillment to outsourced logistics
- E-commerce businesses with seasonal demand spikes who need flexible storage capacity
- Sellers who combine on-demand production with pre-stocked inventory

## Getting Started

To set up 3PL, warehousing, or kitting services, email sales@completeful.com with:
- A description of your products and estimated inventory volume
- Your average monthly order volume
- Any specific packaging or handling requirements

Our team will put together a customized proposal.
