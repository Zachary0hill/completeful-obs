## Overview

Two of Completeful's most powerful services for sellers who want to stand out are personalization and private label. Personalization lets you offer products tailored to each individual customer. Private label lets you sell products under your own brand. This page explains both.

## Personalization

### What Personalization Means

A personalized product is one that is customized for a specific customer at the time of purchase. Common examples include:

- A t-shirt with a customer's name printed on it
- A home item engraved or printed with a custom message
- A product featuring a customer-supplied photo
- Items customized with a date, monogram, or other unique detail

Personalized products tend to command higher prices, generate stronger emotional value for buyers, and produce fewer returns from buyer's remorse (buyers are less likely to return something made specifically for them).

### How to Offer Personalized Products

1. Set up a personalized product in your Completeful account by selecting a base item from the catalog and enabling the personalization option.
2. Define what information you need from the customer (for example, "Enter the name to print" or "Upload your photo").
3. Publish the product to your connected store (Shopify, Etsy, WooCommerce, or TikTok Shop).
4. When a customer places an order, the personalization details are passed through to Completeful automatically.
5. Our production team creates the item with the customer's unique customization before shipping.

### Tips for Personalized Listings

- Be specific in your listing instructions so customers provide exactly what you need.
- Include examples of what a finished product looks like.
- Set clear turnaround expectations — personalized items may take slightly longer to produce than standard items.
- Communicate in your shop policies that personalized items cannot be returned unless there is a production defect.

## Private Label

### What Private Label Means

Private label products are produced by Completeful but branded with your own label, logo, or brand identity. Instead of generic packaging or a product that appears to come from a fulfillment company, your customers receive something that looks and feels like it came from your brand.

Private label options can include:

- Custom hang tags with your brand name and logo
- Branded packing inserts or thank-you cards
- Custom labels replacing manufacturer labels
- Branded outer packaging

### How Private Label Works

1. Contact sales@completeful.com or set up private label options through your account settings to discuss what branding elements you want.
2. Provide your logo, brand colors, and any design files needed for labels, tags, or inserts.
3. Our team will confirm specifications and produce samples for your approval.
4. Once approved, all orders for your private-label products will include your branding elements.

Private label setup may involve a one-time setup process and additional per-unit cost depending on the complexity of the branding. Contact sales@completeful.com for a quote.

### Why Sellers Use Private Label

- Builds brand recognition and customer loyalty
- Makes your store look professional and established
- Encourages repeat purchases under your brand name rather than associating purchases with a generic fulfillment service

## Questions

For questions about personalization or private label, contact support@completeful.com (for general setup help) or sales@completeful.com (for private label pricing and bulk options).
