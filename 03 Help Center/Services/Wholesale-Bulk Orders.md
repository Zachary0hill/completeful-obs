## Overview

Completeful supports wholesale and bulk ordering for brands, retailers, event organizers, and businesses that need large quantities of custom or branded products. Whether you are stocking a retail shop, supplying a corporate event, or building out a product line, our in-house manufacturing in Lafayette, Louisiana means consistent quality at scale.

## What Qualifies as a Wholesale or Bulk Order

A bulk or wholesale order generally refers to a single order for a large quantity of the same or similar item, or a recurring high-volume purchasing relationship. There is no minimum order quantity required for standard orders — bulk pricing and terms are negotiated for orders where volume justifies custom pricing.

## Products Available for Wholesale

Most products across our 500-900+ item catalog are available for bulk ordering. Common wholesale categories include:

- Apparel (t-shirts, hoodies, hats, and more)
- Home goods and soft goods
- Accessories
- Branded merchandise and promotional items
- Private label products

If you have a product type in mind that is not listed in our standard catalog, contact our sales team to discuss custom production options.

## Pricing for Bulk Orders

Bulk orders may qualify for reduced per-unit pricing based on quantity. To get a quote:

1. Identify the product(s) you need and the quantity per SKU.
2. Email sales@completeful.com with your product list, quantities, required ship date, and any relevant artwork or branding files.
3. Our sales team will respond with a quote within 1 to 2 business days.

Pricing varies based on product type, print method, quantity, and complexity of customization.

## Production and Lead Times

Bulk orders are produced at our Lafayette facility. Lead times depend on order size and current production schedule. Standard bulk lead times range from 5 to 15 business days after artwork approval. Rush production may be available; contact sales@completeful.com to request it.

## How to Place a Wholesale Order

1. Contact sales@completeful.com with your order details and artwork.
2. Receive a quote and approve the pricing and production specifications.
3. Submit payment or arrange billing terms (available for approved accounts).
4. Approve a digital proof before production begins.
5. Your order is produced in-house and shipped to the destination you specify.

## Questions

For wholesale inquiries, email sales@completeful.com or visit completeful.com to get in touch.
