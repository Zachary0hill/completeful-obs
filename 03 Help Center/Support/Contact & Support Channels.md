## Overview

Completeful's support team is here to help you resolve issues quickly and get back to running your store. This page lists every way to reach us and what each channel is best suited for.

## Support Channels

### Email Support

**support@completeful.com** — General support for all account, order, product, integration, and billing questions. This is the primary support channel for most sellers.

When emailing support, include:
- Your account email address or username
- Your order number (if the question is order-related)
- A clear description of the issue
- Any screenshots or photos that would help us understand the problem faster

Response time: We aim to respond to all support emails within 1 business day. Complex issues may take longer to investigate.

### Sales Inquiries

**sales@completeful.com** — For wholesale and bulk order quotes, private label inquiries, 3PL and warehousing onboarding, affiliate program questions, and partnership opportunities.

### App Support

If you are using the Completeful app, you can submit a support request directly from within the app:

1. Tap or click the Help icon (question mark) in the navigation.
2. Select Contact Support.
3. Describe your issue and attach any relevant files or screenshots.
4. Submit. You will receive a confirmation and a response by email.

### Help Center

The Completeful Help Center at completeful.com/help contains guides, policies, and how-to articles for the most common questions. It is organized by category:

- Account & Billing
- Services
- Orders & Fulfillment
- Integrations
- Design & Printing
- Legal

Search for your topic at the top of the Help Center before submitting a request — many questions are answered there immediately.

## What to Include When Contacting Support

Providing the right information upfront speeds up every interaction:

| Issue Type | What to Include |
|---|---|
| Order problem | Order number, description of the issue, photos if applicable |
| Billing question | Charge date, amount, and what you believe is incorrect |
| Integration issue | Platform name, description of what is not working, any error messages |
| Account access | Account email address, device/browser used |
| Product defect | Order number and clear photos of the item |

## Business Hours

Completeful's support team operates Monday through Friday, during US Central Time business hours. Emails received outside of business hours are reviewed the next business day.

## Escalations

If your issue has not been resolved after an initial response, reply to your existing support thread and ask for escalation. We do not require you to start a new ticket — continuing the same thread keeps all context in one place.

## Feedback

We welcome feedback on your experience with Completeful. If you have suggestions for how we can improve, include them in any support message or reach out directly to support@completeful.com with the subject line "Feedback."
