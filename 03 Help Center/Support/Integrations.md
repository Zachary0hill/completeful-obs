## Overview

Completeful integrates with the most popular e-commerce platforms so your store and your fulfillment operate as one. When an order comes in through your connected sales channel, it flows directly into Completeful for production and fulfillment — no manual order entry required.

## Supported Platforms

- **Shopify** — The most feature-complete integration. Sync your Completeful product catalog to your Shopify store, map variants, and have orders fulfilled automatically.
- **Etsy** — Connect your Etsy shop to route orders directly to Completeful for production. Ideal for handmade-style and personalized product sellers.
- **WooCommerce** — Works with WordPress-based stores. Install the Completeful plugin from the WooCommerce marketplace to connect your catalog and automate fulfillment.
- **TikTok Shop** — Sell on TikTok and let Completeful handle production and shipping. Ideal for sellers growing their audience on social commerce.

## How to Connect Your Store

### Shopify

1. Log in to your Completeful account and go to Settings > Integrations.
2. Select Shopify and click Connect.
3. Enter your Shopify store URL and follow the OAuth authorization prompts.
4. Once connected, go to the Catalog section to push products to your Shopify store.
5. Set your pricing and publish listings directly from Completeful.

### Etsy

1. Go to Settings > Integrations and select Etsy.
2. Click Connect and authorize Completeful to access your Etsy account.
3. Map your Etsy listings to the corresponding Completeful products.
4. Orders placed on Etsy will automatically appear in your Completeful order queue.

### WooCommerce

1. In your WordPress admin, go to Plugins > Add New and search for "Completeful."
2. Install and activate the Completeful plugin.
3. In the plugin settings, enter your Completeful API key (found under Account > Settings > API in the Completeful app).
4. Your store is now connected. Configure product sync and fulfillment settings within the plugin.

### TikTok Shop

1. Go to Settings > Integrations and select TikTok Shop.
2. Log in to your TikTok seller account when prompted to authorize the connection.
3. Sync your products and confirm your fulfillment settings.

## Managing Connected Integrations

You can view, pause, or disconnect any integration from Settings > Integrations in your Completeful account. Pausing an integration stops new orders from flowing in but does not affect orders already in production.

## Troubleshooting

**Orders are not syncing:** Check that your integration is still authorized. Tokens can expire if you change your platform password or revoke access. Reconnect the integration if needed.

**Product variants do not match:** Ensure that the variants set up in Completeful match the options in your storefront exactly (size names, color names, etc.).

**Duplicate orders:** This can happen if an order is placed during a sync cycle. Contact support@completeful.com with the order numbers and we will resolve it.

For additional help with integrations, contact support@completeful.com.
