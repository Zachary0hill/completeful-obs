## Overview

Once your order is in production and then shipped, you can track its status every step of the way. This page explains how to find tracking information and what to do if something does not look right.

## Order Statuses Explained

Your order moves through the following stages in your Completeful account:

- **Order Received** — The order has been placed and payment confirmed. Production is queued.
- **In Production** — Your item is being made at our facility in Lafayette, Louisiana.
- **Quality Check** — The finished item is being inspected before packaging.
- **Shipped** — The order has left our facility and is with the carrier. A tracking number is now available.
- **Delivered** — The carrier has confirmed delivery.
- **Issue Reported** — A support request has been submitted for this order.

## How to Track Your Order

### Through the Completeful App

1. Log in to your account at completeful.com or in the Completeful app.
2. Navigate to Orders > Order History.
3. Find the order you want to track and click on it.
4. The order detail page shows the current status and, once shipped, a tracking number with a link to the carrier's tracking page.

### By Email

When your order ships, a shipping confirmation email is sent to your account email address. It includes:
- The carrier name
- Your tracking number
- A direct link to the carrier's tracking page

If you did not receive the email, check your spam or junk folder. You can also find the same information in your account under Orders.

### Tracking on the Carrier's Website

Use the tracking number provided to check real-time delivery status directly on the carrier's site (USPS, UPS, or FedEx, depending on how your order was shipped).

## Common Tracking Questions

**My tracking number shows no updates.**
It can take 12 to 24 hours for a newly shipped package to appear in the carrier's system after the label is created. Check again the following business day.

**The tracking shows "delivered" but the package has not arrived.**
First, check with household members and around your property (porches, mailbox area, neighbor's address). If the package is still missing after 24 hours, contact support@completeful.com with your order number. We will open a trace with the carrier.

**My order has been in production for longer than expected.**
Production times can occasionally extend due to high order volume or supply availability. If your order has been in production for more than 10 business days without an update, contact us at support@completeful.com.

**I never received a shipping confirmation email.**
Log in to your account and verify the email address on file. If it is correct, check your spam folder. If the order shows as "Shipped" in your account but you still have not received the email, contact support@completeful.com.

## Questions

For order-specific inquiries, email support@completeful.com and include your order number.
