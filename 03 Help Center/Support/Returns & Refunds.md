## Overview

Because every Completeful order is custom-produced specifically for that customer, our return and refund policy differs from standard retail. This page explains what is covered, what is not, and how to request a resolution.

## What Is Covered

We stand behind the quality of every item we produce. If an order arrives with a production defect, we will replace it or issue a refund at no cost to you. Covered issues include:

- Printing errors (wrong design, significant color deviation from approved mockup, blurry or misaligned print)
- Manufacturing defects (seam failures, structural flaws in the item itself)
- Wrong item sent
- Damaged in transit (carrier damage; see below for the process)
- Order lost in transit (after the carrier's investigation period)

## What Is Not Covered

Because products are made to order, we are unable to accept returns or issue refunds for:

- Buyer's remorse or change of mind
- Incorrect size selected by the customer
- Minor color variation between a screen mockup and a printed product (screens and print outputs render color differently)
- Personalized items where the customer provided incorrect information (e.g., a misspelled name submitted by the buyer)

We recommend setting clear expectations in your store's product descriptions so your customers know what to expect.

## How to Request a Refund or Reprint

If you believe an order qualifies for a replacement or refund:

1. Log in to your Completeful account and go to Orders > Order History.
2. Find the relevant order and click Report an Issue.
3. Select the issue type and provide a brief description.
4. Attach a clear photo showing the defect or damage. This is required for production-error and carrier-damage claims.
5. Submit the request.

Our team reviews all requests within 2 business days. If approved, you can choose between a reprint at no charge or a refund to your original payment method.

## Carrier-Damaged Packages

If a package arrives visibly damaged:

1. The recipient should photograph the damaged packaging and item before discarding anything.
2. Submit a claim through the Report an Issue process above and include those photos.
3. We will work with the carrier to file a damage claim and arrange a replacement.

## Seller Responsibility to Customers

As the seller, you are the point of contact for your end customers. When your customer contacts you about an issue, gather the relevant information (order number, description of the problem, photos if applicable) and submit a claim through your Completeful account. We will resolve it with you directly.

You are free to set your own return and refund policy for your storefront, provided it does not offer broader guarantees than what Completeful can support on the production side.

## Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days and will appear on your original payment method based on your bank's processing time.

## Questions

Contact support@completeful.com with your order number and a description of the issue.
