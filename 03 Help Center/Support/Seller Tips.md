## Overview

Whether you are just getting started or looking to grow an established store, these tips will help you get the most out of selling with Completeful.

## Set Up Your Store for Success

**Connect your sales channel early.** Completeful integrates directly with Shopify, Etsy, WooCommerce, and TikTok Shop. Connecting your store before you launch means orders flow through automatically — no manual forwarding required.

**Use high-quality mockups.** Your product photos are often the first thing a buyer sees. Use the mockup tools available in the Completeful app to generate clean, professional images for every listing.

**Write clear product descriptions.** Describe the material, sizing, and any personalization options available. Buyers who know what to expect are less likely to request returns or leave negative feedback.

## Price with Your Costs in Mind

**Know your base cost before you set your price.** Check the production cost for each product in the Completeful catalog. Factor in any platform fees (Etsy charges a transaction fee and listing fee, Shopify charges payment processing fees) so you know your actual take-home margin.

**Do not undercut yourself.** Many new sellers set prices too low to compete. A better approach is to compete on quality, shipping speed, and presentation rather than price alone.

**Revisit pricing regularly.** As your store gains reviews and credibility, you can often raise prices without losing customers.

## Manage Your Products Proactively

**Check product availability.** Raw material availability can occasionally affect specific catalog items. Review your active listings periodically to make sure the products you are selling are still available for production.

**Offer personalization where it makes sense.** Personalized products often command higher prices and generate more repeat business. Completeful supports personalization on many items — see our Personalization & Private Label page for details.

**Use variants strategically.** Offering multiple sizes, colors, or styles under a single listing improves search visibility on platforms like Etsy and makes your shop easier to browse.

## Deliver a Great Customer Experience

**Set accurate shipping expectations.** Completeful produces orders in-house in the USA. US orders typically ship within 3 to 7 business days after production. Set your shop's shipping estimates to reflect actual production plus transit time.

**Communicate proactively.** If you receive an order with unclear personalization instructions, reach out to your customer before production begins. Catching issues early prevents costly reprints.

**Respond to messages quickly.** Fast response times on Etsy and Shopify improve your seller rating and build trust with buyers.

## Grow Your Store

- **Use social proof.** Ask satisfied customers to leave reviews. Reviews drive conversions more than almost any other factor.
- **Start with a focused niche.** Stores that own a specific niche (for example, gifts for nurses, custom pet portraits, gym apparel) tend to outperform stores with no clear identity.
- **Refer other sellers.** If you know other shop owners who could benefit from Completeful, check out the Affiliate Programs page — you can earn commissions on their production activity.
