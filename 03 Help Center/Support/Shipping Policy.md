## Overview

Every order fulfilled by Completeful is produced in-house at our facility in Lafayette, Louisiana. This page explains how production and shipping timelines work, which carriers we use, and how to handle common shipping questions.

## Production Time

Before an order ships, it must be produced. Production time varies by product type:

- Standard print-on-demand items: 2 to 5 business days
- Embroidered or specialty items: 3 to 7 business days
- Personalized items: 3 to 7 business days (pending artwork confirmation)
- Wholesale/bulk orders: 5 to 15 business days (see Wholesale/Bulk Orders page)

Production begins after order payment is confirmed and, for personalized orders, after any required artwork is approved.

## Shipping Carriers

Completeful ships orders via major US carriers, including:

- USPS
- UPS
- FedEx

Carrier selection is based on destination, package size, weight, and the shipping service selected at checkout. We work with multiple carriers to find the best combination of speed and cost for each shipment.

## Shipping Times (Domestic)

After production, estimated transit times within the United States are:

- Standard shipping: 3 to 7 business days
- Expedited shipping: 1 to 3 business days (where available)

Total delivery time = production time + transit time. For example, a standard print item produced in 3 business days and shipped standard would arrive in approximately 6 to 10 business days from order placement.

## International Shipping

International shipping availability depends on the destination country and product type. Where offered, international transit times vary. Duties, customs fees, and import taxes are the responsibility of the recipient. Completeful is not responsible for delays caused by customs processing.

## Tracking Your Order

Once your order ships, a tracking number is generated and sent to the email address on file. You can also find tracking information in your Completeful account under Orders > Order History. For guidance on tracking, see the Order Tracking page.

## Address Accuracy

Completeful ships to the address provided at the time of order. Please ensure your customers enter accurate shipping addresses. We are not responsible for packages delivered to incorrect addresses provided during checkout.

If an address issue is caught before the order ships, contact support@completeful.com as quickly as possible. Once a package is in transit, address correction fees charged by the carrier may apply.

## Shipping Costs

Shipping rates are calculated based on the destination, weight, and dimensions of the package. Current rates are displayed in the Completeful app when setting up a product and at the time of fulfillment. You can choose to pass shipping costs to your customers or build them into your product pricing.

## Questions

For questions about a specific shipment, contact support@completeful.com and include your order number.
