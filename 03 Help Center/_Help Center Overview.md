# Help Center Overview

Welcome to the Completeful Help Center. Whether you are just getting started or troubleshooting a specific issue, this is the place to find clear answers about how Completeful works, how to set up your products, and how to manage your orders.

Completeful is a print-on-demand, third-party logistics, and in-house US manufacturing platform built for sellers on Shopify, Etsy, TikTok Shop, and WooCommerce. Everything is made in-house at our 228,000 sq ft facility in Lafayette, Louisiana — no order minimums, transparent pricing, and a catalog of 500 to 900+ products.

## Getting Help Directly

If you cannot find the answer you are looking for in these articles, reach out to the team:

- **General support:** support@completeful.com
- **Sales and partnerships:** sales@completeful.com
- **Platform:** completeful.com or the Completeful app

The support team is based in the US and is available to help with account questions, order issues, and technical problems. When you reach out, include your order number or store name so the team can respond quickly.
