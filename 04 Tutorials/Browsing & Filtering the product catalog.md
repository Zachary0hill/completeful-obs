# Browsing and Filtering the Product Catalog

Completeful's product catalog contains 500 to 900-plus items spanning apparel, home decor, drinkware, wood products, metal goods, and more — all produced in-house at the 228,000 sq ft facility in Lafayette, LA. The catalog browser inside the app lets you explore that range, compare products, and add items to your shop without ever leaving the platform.

## Why the Catalog Matters

Because Completeful handles production and fulfillment end-to-end, every product in the catalog is one you can sell immediately with no order minimums and transparent pricing shown up front. Browsing the catalog is how you build your product lineup — from a single cutting board design to a full apparel collection.

## Navigating to the Catalog

From the app home screen, tap **Product Catalog** in the bottom navigation bar or from the main menu. The catalog opens to a default grid view showing all available product categories.

## Browsing by Category

Products are organized into top-level categories that map to the production departments:

- **Wood** — cutting boards, frames, UV-printed wood planks
- **Metal** — laser-engraved and powder-coated metal products
- **Drinkware** — sublimated 11 oz and 15 oz mugs
- **Apparel** — DTG-printed items on Gildan, Hanes, and Bella Canvas blanks
- **Home & Accessories** — mats, totes, puzzles, and more

Tap a category tile to enter it. Use the back arrow to return to the top-level view.

## Filtering the Catalog

The filter panel is accessible via the filter icon in the upper-right corner of the catalog screen. Available filters include:

- **Production method** — UV print, sublimation, DTG, laser engraving, embroidery, CNC
- **Price range** — set a minimum and maximum base cost to find products that fit your target margin
- **Personalization type** — text only, image upload, or fully custom
- **In-stock status** — shows only products currently available for ordering

Apply multiple filters at once. The catalog updates in real time as you toggle options. Tap **Clear Filters** to reset.

## Searching Within the Catalog

Use the search bar at the top of the catalog screen to find a product by name or keyword. Search terms like "cutting board," "11 oz mug," or "tote bag" return matching results across all categories.

## Viewing a Product Detail Page

Tap any product tile to open its detail page. You will find:

- Product name and description
- Base price (the cost Completeful charges you to produce and fulfill the item)
- Available sizes, colors, or variants
- Production method and estimated production time
- A sample image and, where available, a 360-degree mockup

## Adding a Product to Your Store

From the product detail page, tap **Add to My Products**. This moves the product into your My Products page, where you can apply your design, set your retail price, and connect it to a listing on Shopify or Etsy.

## Tips for Efficient Catalog Use

- Use price range filtering before you browse to surface only products that fit your margin targets
- Check the production method filter when you know a customer wants a specific finish — for example, laser engraving for wedding gifts or sublimation for vibrant full-color drinkware
- Save products to your favorites list by tapping the bookmark icon on any product tile — this creates a shortlist you can return to without repeating your filters

Reach out to sales@completeful.com if you want guidance on which products perform best in a given niche or need help narrowing down your catalog selection.
