## BODY OUTLINE

**\[Chapter Title: 0:00 Create your Completeful account\]**

Setup:
The first step is getting your account created correctly, because this is what unlocks your product catalog, integrations, mockups, and order dashboard. \[Screen Recording: Browser opens completeful.com, then clicks Sign Up.\]

Tension:
From the Completeful homepage, click Sign Up and enter your business email, password, and account details. \[Text Overlay: Step 1: Create your free account\] After submitting your information, check your inbox for the verification email and confirm your account. \[Screen Recording: Email inbox, verification click, redirect into app.completeful.com.\] Once you're inside the app, Completeful may prompt you to enter basic business information like your store name and contact details. Fill those out now so your workspace is ready for product publishing and order routing. \[Pattern Interrupt: Quick zoom-in on "Account Created" confirmation.\]

Payoff:
At this point, your Completeful account is live and ready to connect to a sales channel. That means you're one step closer to selling without holding inventory or paying upfront for stock.

Transition sentence:
Now that your account is ready, the next step is connecting the sales channel where your customers will actually place orders.

**\[Chapter Title: 2:00 Connect your store\]**

Setup:
This is where Completeful becomes your fulfillment partner behind the scenes. Once your store is connected, products and orders can sync automatically instead of being managed manually. \[B-roll: Split screen of Completeful dashboard and Shopify, Etsy, TikTok Shop, and WooCommerce logos.\] \[Text Overlay: Shopify • Etsy • TikTok Shop • WooCommerce\]

Tension:
Inside your Completeful dashboard, go to the Integrations area and choose your platform. \[Screen Recording: Dashboard navigation to Integrations page.\] If you're using Shopify, click Connect Shopify, enter your store URL, and approve the app permissions in Shopify. \[Screen Recording: Shopify connection flow.\] If you're using Etsy, TikTok Shop, or WooCommerce, the process is similar: choose the platform, sign in, and approve the requested access so Completeful can sync products and orders. \[Text Overlay: Approve permissions to enable syncing\]

This is the part many sellers rush through, but permissions matter. Completeful needs the correct access to import products, receive orders, and update fulfillment statuses. If you skip or deny part of that flow, the connection may appear active while key syncing functions fail in the background. \[Pattern Interrupt: Red X graphic over "Partial Setup"\]

Payoff:
Once permissions are approved, your store should show as connected inside Completeful. That gives you the foundation for automatic syncing and white-label fulfillment, with your brand staying front and center.

Transition sentence:
With your store connected, the next move is making sure the connection is usable, not just technically active.

**\[Chapter Title: 4:30 Verify integration settings\]**

Setup:
A connected store is great, but a verified store is what keeps you from troubleshooting during your first sale. This step helps you catch issues before customers ever see them. \[Screen Recording: Integrations page showing connected status.\]

Tension:
Open your connected store inside the Integrations section and review the status indicators. Look for confirmation that the store is active and syncing properly. \[Text Overlay: Check status • Check permissions • Check sync\] Then review any initial settings tied to product import, shipping updates, or order handling if they appear in your integration panel.

Next, head to your dashboard and confirm the platform appears in your workspace correctly. \[Screen Recording: Dashboard home, connected store name visible.\] If Completeful offers a prompt to import or begin creating products for that store, that's usually a strong sign the integration is functioning normally. \[Pattern Interrupt: Cursor highlight around "Connected" badge.\]

Here's the small mistake I mentioned earlier: sellers sometimes connect the wrong storefront, especially if they manage multiple stores under one login. Double-check the exact store name and URL now before you publish anything.

Payoff:
When the right store shows as active in your dashboard, you know Completeful is pointed to the correct sales channel and ready for the next step.

Transition sentence:
Once you've confirmed the connection, it's time to prepare the account for product creation and your first synced order.

**\[Chapter Title: 6:30 Prepare for your first product\]**

Setup:
Connecting the store is only half the workflow. Before orders can flow smoothly, you need to understand where products are created and how Completeful tracks what happens next. \[B-roll: Product catalog scrolling, then a product mockup appears.\] \[Text Overlay: 500+ customizable products\]

Tension:
From the dashboard, open the product catalog and browse the products available for your connected store. \[Screen Recording: Product Catalog navigation.\] Completeful gives you access to more than 500 customizable products, including apparel, tumblers, engraved goods, jewelry, and more, all fulfilled from Lafayette, Louisiana.

Select a product and open it in the Designer Tool. \[Screen Recording: Product opened in Designer Tool.\] This is where you upload artwork, position your design, and move toward publishing. If you want to preview the product before listing it, use the Mockup Generator to create clean visuals for your storefront. \[Text Overlay: Designer Tool + Mockup Generator\]

Even if you're not publishing today, this test run matters because it confirms your connected store is ready to receive products from Completeful. \[Pattern Interrupt: Side-by-side before and after mockup.\]

Payoff:
By the end of this step, you know exactly where product creation happens and how your connected store fits into the workflow from design to live listing.

Transition sentence:
The final core step is understanding how Completeful handles your orders once a customer buys.

**\[Chapter Title: 8:45 Understand order flow\]**

Setup:
If you want confidence in your setup, you need visibility after the sale too. This is where the order dashboard becomes one of the most useful tools in your account. \[Screen Recording: Order Dashboard opens.\]

Tension:
Inside the Order Dashboard, you'll be able to track orders through each production stage: Queued, In Production, Quality Check, Shipped, and Delivered. \[Screen Recording: Show order status stages.\] \[Text Overlay: Queued > In Production > Quality Check > Shipped > Delivered\]

Each status update tells you exactly where the order is in the production and shipping workflow. Completeful handles this in-house, which means you're not relying on a third party to tell you what's happening. \[B-roll: In-house production footage: printing, engraving, packing.\] \[Text Overlay: In-house production, Lafayette, Louisiana\]

Payoff:
When you understand the order flow, you can communicate confidently with customers and manage expectations without guessing.

## BONUS

Earlier I mentioned I'd share the fastest way to confirm your integration is actually working before you start selling. Here it is.

The quickest test is to create one sample product inside Completeful and send it to your connected store before building a full catalog. \[Screen Recording: Product created and pushed to connected store.\] Once it appears in your store admin, you've confirmed the most important part of the connection: product publishing is working.

If you want to go one step further, place a low-risk test order from your storefront using your own payment method or your platform's test mode if available. Then watch for that order inside the Completeful dashboard. \[Screen Recording: Order appears in dashboard.\] If the order moves into the queue correctly, your integration is doing exactly what it should.

That simple publish-and-test process can save you from finding setup issues after you've already started driving traffic.
