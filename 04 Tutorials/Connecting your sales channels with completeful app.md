Tension:
Start with Shopify if that's your main storefront. Click the Shopify connect option, follow the OAuth prompts, approve the requested permissions, and let the platform redirect you back to Completeful. Shopify is a strong place to start for sellers building a branded store experience with white-label fulfillment and room to scale.

For Etsy, the flow is similar. Click connect, sign in to the Etsy shop you want to link, approve access, and confirm that you return to the correct Completeful account. This matters if you manage more than one shop or more than one browser session.

For TikTok Shop, choose the TikTok Shop integration, sign into the correct seller account, and complete the authorization prompts. Pay close attention to the store name shown after the connection is complete so you know the right channel is linked.

If you're planning around WooCommerce, keep in mind that Completeful supports WooCommerce in its integration lineup, but setup availability may vary depending on the current implementation and guide availability. **Segment 4: How to verify sync and order flow**

Setup:
A connected badge looks great, but the real goal is confidence. You want to know that your orders, products, and fulfillment workflow are communicating correctly before customers start buying. \[Screen Recording: Connected sales channels page and order dashboard tab\] \[Text Overlay: Don't Stop at "Connected"\]

Tension:
First, confirm the channel status is active on the Sales Channels page. Then review any sync settings available for that marketplace. After that, move to the Order Dashboard and check whether any test or existing activity is appearing correctly. \[Screen Recording: Order Dashboard showing connected store orders\]

A common mistake at this stage is assuming the connection is working just because the status says active. The real test is whether orders and products can flow. If you have products published on your store, check whether they appear linked correctly in Completeful. If you place a test order from your storefront, verify it shows up in the dashboard.

Payoff:
Once you can confirm orders and products are flowing correctly, your integration is ready for real business. That's when you move from setup mode into selling mode.

**Segment 5: Common beginner mistakes to avoid**

Setup:
Most connection issues come from a few predictable places. Knowing them in advance saves you a lot of troubleshooting time.

Tension:
Mistake one is connecting the wrong store account. If you manage multiple storefronts or logged into a different account during the OAuth flow, you may have connected the wrong one. Double-check the store name shown on your Sales Channels page against your actual marketplace.

Mistake two is approving partial permissions. Some sellers click through permission prompts too quickly and deny access that Completeful needs for syncing. If your integration seems incomplete, revisit permissions in your marketplace account settings.

Mistake three is skipping the Order Dashboard check. The connection page showing "connected" is not the same as the fulfillment workflow being ready. Always verify by reviewing the Order Dashboard for activity tied to your connected store.

Mistake four is connecting a store before having products ready. The integration works best as part of a live workflow, so make sure you have at least one published product or listing in progress when you verify the connection. \[B-roll: Seller reviewing integration settings and order dashboard side by side\]

Payoff:
Avoiding these four mistakes means your setup is cleaner from day one, and you spend less time troubleshooting and more time building the product catalog that drives sales.

## BONUS

Earlier I mentioned I'd share the one connection check beginners skip that prevents the most common sync issues later. Here it is.

After you connect a store, do one post-connection audit immediately. Open the Sales Channels page and confirm the exact store name. Then go to your product area and make sure the intended store is associated correctly. Finally, open your Order Dashboard and verify that any existing or test activity appears under the right workflow.

This quick audit matters because most beginner issues come from one of three things: the wrong account was authorized, the seller expected products to appear without checking assignment, or the connection was approved but never verified in the order flow. Spending two extra minutes here can save hours of confusion later.

If you plan to expand to multiple channels, repeat that same audit every time. It keeps your setup clean, your operations predictable, and your brand front and center while Completeful handles production in-house with fast turnaround, typically within 2 to 5 business days. \[Screen Recording: Post-connection audit walkthrough from Sales Channels to products to Order Dashboard\] \[Text Overlay: The 2-Minute Connection Audit\]
