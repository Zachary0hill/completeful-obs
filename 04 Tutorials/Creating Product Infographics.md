# Creating Product Infographics

The Infographic Editor inside the Completeful app lets you build polished listing images — the multi-panel graphics that explain product features, sizing, care instructions, or personalization options directly on your product photos. Strong infographics reduce buyer questions and increase conversion on every channel you sell through.

## What the Infographic Editor Does

The editor is purpose-built for e-commerce listing images. You can layer text blocks, icons, callout lines, and graphic elements over a product photo or a clean background. Unlike a general design tool, the templates and presets are sized and formatted for Etsy, Shopify, and TikTok Shop thumbnail requirements.

Key capabilities include:
- Pre-built infographic templates sized for listing images
- Drag-and-drop text and icon elements
- Upload your own product photos or pull from your My Products page
- Custom font support (Inter, Montserrat, Bebas Neue, and any fonts you have uploaded)
- Export-ready output for use in Etsy listings or Shopify product pages

## Step-by-Step: Building an Infographic

**Step 1 — Open the Infographic Editor**
From the app home screen, tap the Infographic Editor tile or navigate to it from the Design section. The editor opens with a blank canvas or a template picker.

**Step 2 — Choose a template or start blank**
Browse the available templates filtered by product type (apparel, home decor, mugs, etc.). Select one that matches your product layout. You can also start with a blank canvas if you want full control over composition.

**Step 3 — Set your canvas product photo**
Tap the image placeholder and either upload a photo from your device or select a product image from your My Products catalog. The editor will center the image and apply any template cropping rules automatically.

**Step 4 — Edit text blocks**
Tap any text element to edit the copy. Use short, specific language: feature callouts ("Dishwasher Safe"), size notes ("Available in 11 oz and 15 oz"), or personalization prompts ("Add a name or date at checkout"). Select your font and adjust size and color to match your brand.

**Step 5 — Add or remove elements**
Use the element tray at the bottom of the editor to drag in additional icons, dividers, or callout arrows. To remove an element, select it and tap the delete icon.

**Step 6 — Preview and export**
Tap Preview to see the image at full resolution before saving. When satisfied, tap Export or Save to Library. Saved infographics appear in your asset library and can be attached to any listing you create inside the app.

## Tips for Effective Infographics

- Keep each panel focused on one message — feature, size, or care
- Use high-contrast text so the copy reads clearly on mobile thumbnails
- Match your brand color palette (Navy #1B2A4A, Teal #1ABC9C, Accent Blue #3A7BD5) for a consistent storefront look
- Etsy recommends a 2000 x 2000 px square; Shopify product images display best at 2048 x 2048 px

## Where to Go Next

Once your infographic is exported, attach it as an additional listing image inside the Creating Listings for Etsy or Creating Listings for Shopify tutorials. Infographics typically perform best as the second or third image in a listing — after the hero product shot.

Contact support@completeful.com if you run into issues with template rendering or font uploads.
