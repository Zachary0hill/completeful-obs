# Creating Listings for Shopify

Once you have connected your Shopify store to Completeful, the app can publish product listings directly to your storefront — complete with title, description, pricing, variants, and images. This tutorial walks through every step from connecting your store to having a live, shoppable listing.

## Prerequisites

Before creating a Shopify listing, make sure you have:

- A Shopify store (any plan)
- The Completeful app installed and your account active
- At least one product added to your My Products page with a design applied
- Your Shopify store connected to Completeful (see the Connect Store section in app Settings)

## Step 1 — Connect Your Shopify Store

If you have not yet connected Shopify, go to **Settings > Connect Store** inside the Completeful app. Tap **Shopify**, enter your Shopify store URL (yourstore.myshopify.com), and follow the OAuth authorization prompt. Once approved, your store name will appear as connected in the Settings panel.

## Step 2 — Open My Products

Navigate to **My Products** from the bottom navigation bar. This page shows every product you have added from the catalog and customized with a design. Tap the product you want to list on Shopify.

## Step 3 — Start a New Listing

On the product detail screen, tap **Create Listing** and select **Shopify** from the channel options. The listing editor opens with fields pre-populated from your product and design data.

## Step 4 — Fill In Listing Details

Complete each section of the listing editor:

- **Title** — Write a clear, keyword-rich title. Example: "Personalized Cutting Board — Custom Engraved Wedding Gift"
- **Description** — Describe the product, personalization options, dimensions, and materials. Completeful's design editor can suggest copy, or you can write your own.
- **Tags** — Add Shopify tags that help with store navigation and SEO
- **Variants** — Select which sizes, colors, or personalization fields to expose to buyers. Each variant maps to a specific SKU in the Completeful catalog.
- **Retail Price** — Set the price your customers pay. The app shows your base production cost alongside the price field so you can confirm your margin at a glance.
- **Images** — Upload or select listing images including your product mockup and any infographics created in the Infographic Editor. The first image becomes the Shopify thumbnail.

## Step 5 — Set Fulfillment to Completeful

In the listing editor, confirm that the fulfillment method is set to **Completeful**. This links the listing to the Completeful production and fulfillment pipeline. When a customer places an order on Shopify, the order will route automatically to Completeful for production and shipping.

## Step 6 — Publish or Save as Draft

Tap **Publish to Shopify** to make the listing live immediately, or tap **Save as Draft** to review it in your Shopify admin before publishing. Draft listings appear in your Shopify Products list with a "Draft" status.

## Step 7 — Confirm the Listing in Shopify

Log into your Shopify admin and navigate to **Products** to verify the listing appeared correctly. Check that:

- The title, description, and images look as expected
- Variants and pricing are correct
- Inventory tracking is set to **Do not track** (Completeful handles fulfillment on demand, so you do not hold stock)

## Managing Listings

To edit an existing Shopify listing, return to the My Products page in the Completeful app, tap the product, and tap **Edit Listing**. Changes sync to Shopify automatically when you save.

For questions about listing sync errors or pricing updates, contact support@completeful.com.
