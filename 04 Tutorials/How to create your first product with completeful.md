## BONUS

**10:45 - Bonus: The most common beginner mistake and how to avoid it**

Earlier I mentioned I'd share the beginner mistake that causes the most product setup delays. Here it is.

\[Text Overlay: Biggest mistake: ignoring artwork specs\]

\[Screen Recording: Reopen artwork guidelines and zoom into file recommendations.\] The biggest mistake is uploading artwork that looks fine on your screen but does not meet production requirements. That usually means low resolution, missing transparency, incorrect sizing, or important design elements placed too close to the edge.

Here's the fix. Before you finalize any product, do a three-point check. First, confirm the file type and resolution match the artwork guidelines. Second, preview the design at actual placement size inside the Designer Tool. Third, look at the mockup and ask one simple question: if a customer saw only this image for two seconds, would the design still read clearly?

\[Pattern Interrupt: Checklist graphic appears full screen.\] \[Text Overlay: 3-Point Artwork Check\]

That one habit will save you time, reduce avoidable issues, and help your first products move into production cleanly.
