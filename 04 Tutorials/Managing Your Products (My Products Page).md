## BONUS

Earlier I mentioned I would share the fastest way to tell which products are safe to publish and which ones still need work. Here it is.

Use this quick three-question filter directly from the My Products page.

Question one: Is the design final? If you still need to move artwork, fix text, or adjust placement, do not publish yet. Open the edit design option first.

Question two: Is the marketplace listing complete? If the product exists in Completeful but is not properly listed in your connected store, it is not launch-ready. Open the create or edit listing flow and finish the setup.

Question three: Have you validated the product with a sample or at least a final mockup review? If not, pause before pushing traffic.

If the answer to all three is yes, that product is usually safe to publish. If even one answer is no, keep it in review mode. This simple filter helps you launch cleaner, avoid rushed mistakes, and manage your catalog like a real business. \[Screen Recording: Apply the 3-question filter across several products in My Products.\] \[Text Overlay: Final? Listed? Validated?\]
