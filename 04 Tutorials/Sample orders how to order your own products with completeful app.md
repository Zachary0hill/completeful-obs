## INTRO

If you're a beginner POD seller, ordering samples can feel like an extra step you should skip until you get sales. That's a common mistake, because your sample order is where you validate print placement, check material quality, create content, and make sure your brand experience feels right before customers ever buy.

In this video, you'll learn exactly how to place a sample order inside Completeful, how to choose the right products to test first, what to review when your sample arrives, and how to use that sample to improve your listings and launch faster. I'm also going to show you a simple decision filter for picking the best sample products if your budget is tight.

\[Chapter: 8:35 Which sample products to test first\]

Setup: Not every product in your catalog is worth sampling before launch. The goal is to test the items where quality, fit, and personalization have the most impact on buyer satisfaction.

Tension: Prioritize samples for products that depend on precision, such as engraved jewelry with custom names, apparel where print placement is visible from a distance, and tumblers or drinkware where coating and readability matter. These are the categories where a small print error or placement issue is most likely to generate complaints.

For products like standard apparel or items with simple full-color prints, your mockup previews are usually reliable enough to launch without a physical sample, especially when you are working within your budget. \[Text Overlay: Sample where precision matters most\]

Also consider sampling products that will become your best sellers. If you plan to run ads, feature a product in content, or push traffic to one specific listing, you want that product tested first. \[Screen Recording: Selecting top-priority products from product catalog\]

Payoff: By targeting your samples at the right products, you get the most value from each order and move toward launch faster without overthinking every item in your catalog.

## BONUS

\[Chapter: 10:05 Bonus: the simplest sample strategy for beginners\]

Earlier I mentioned I'd share the fastest way to test multiple products without overcomplicating your launch. Here it is.

Use a three-sample rule. Pick one product you are most excited to sell, one version of that same product with a different design or color, and one product from a second category. That combination tells you three important things fast: whether your brand aesthetic translates well, whether your design system works across variations, and whether your audience should stay niche or expand.

Inside Completeful, save each design version clearly, keep your mockups organized, and compare the finished samples side by side once they arrive. \[Screen Recording: Saved products/designs, mockup files, order history\] \[Text Overlay: The 3-Sample Rule\] If only one product truly stands out in person, launch that first and hold the others until your store starts generating sales. That is a disciplined way to build with no upfront inventory, no minimum orders, and much less risk.
