## INTRO

If you're new to print-on-demand, payment settings can feel like the least exciting part of getting started, but they directly affect whether your orders move into production on time. When a customer buys from your store, Completeful handles the in-house production and fulfillment from Lafayette, Louisiana, but your account still needs a working wallet and payment method so those orders can process smoothly.

In this tutorial, you'll learn how to access your billing area, add funds to your wallet, save a payment method, and understand what happens when an order is charged. You'll also see how to confirm everything is set up correctly before you start selling. I'm going to keep this beginner-friendly and practical so you can get your store ready with confidence.

## BONUS

Earlier I mentioned I'd share the wallet habit that helps prevent fulfillment delays. Here it is.

Set yourself a minimum wallet threshold based on your average order cost. \[Text Overlay: Bonus: Set a minimum wallet threshold\] For example, if your average fulfilled order costs around twenty dollars, you might want to keep enough balance for at least three to five orders at all times. \[Screen Recording: Show wallet page while calculating sample threshold on screen\] That gives you breathing room during busy days, content spikes, or weekend sales.

The reason this works is simple. Automatic order syncing is only helpful if payment is ready when those orders arrive. A healthy wallet balance reduces the chance of a surprise delay, especially when you're selling across multiple channels. \[B-roll: Multiple storefront logos transitioning into incoming order notifications\]

One more practical tip: review your wallet and payment method anytime you add new products, run a promotion, or expect higher traffic. That small habit keeps your backend aligned with your growth. \[Text Overlay: Check wallet before launches and promos\]
