# Understanding Orders and Fulfillment: How Print on Demand Works with Completeful

Print on demand means you sell a product before it exists. When a customer places an order on your Shopify, Etsy, TikTok Shop, or WooCommerce store, Completeful receives that order, produces the item in its Lafayette, LA facility, and ships it directly to your customer — all without you touching inventory. This tutorial explains how that process works from order placement to delivery.

## The Print on Demand Model

Traditional retail requires buying inventory upfront, storing it, and hoping it sells. Print on demand removes that risk entirely:

- **No order minimums** — one unit or one thousand, each order is produced individually
- **No inventory to hold** — products are made to order, so you never overstock
- **Transparent pricing** — Completeful publishes base costs for every product so you can set your retail price and know your margin before you ever make a sale
- **Direct-to-customer shipping** — Completeful ships under your brand to your buyer; you remain the seller of record

## How an Order Moves Through Completeful

**1. Customer places an order**
A buyer purchases a product on your connected storefront. The order details — item, personalization text or artwork, shipping address, and selected variants — are captured by the platform.

**2. Order routes to Completeful**
Because your store is connected to Completeful via the Shopify, Etsy, TikTok Shop, or WooCommerce integration, the order transmits automatically to the Completeful system. You do not need to manually forward anything.

**3. Production is assigned by department**
Each item is routed to the appropriate production department based on product type:

- Laser engraving (28 machines, handling up to 600 orders per day at peak)
- Sublimation (20 auto heat presses for mugs, totes, and puzzles)
- UV printing (mats up to 600 orders/day; wood frames 150-250/day; UV planks at 1,000/day)
- DTG printing (for Gildan, Hanes, Bella Canvas apparel)
- Embroidery (16 machines for embroidered goods)
- CNC and laser cutting for wood products and cutting boards
- Fiber laser and powder coating for metal products

**4. Quality check and packaging**
Completed items go through quality inspection before packaging. Orders are packed for direct shipment to the end customer.

**5. Shipping and tracking**
Completeful ships the order and uploads tracking information to your storefront. Your customer receives tracking notifications through the platform they ordered on.

**6. Earnings recorded**
For Etsy sellers, the Completeful app syncs your Etsy ledger to the Earnings page, giving you a clear view of revenue, production costs, and net earnings without jumping between accounts.

## What You Are Responsible For

As the seller, your responsibilities are:

- Setting up your product listings with accurate descriptions and pricing
- Responding to customer questions before and after purchase
- Handling any customer service issues or refund requests
- Ensuring your designs comply with copyright and platform policies

Completeful handles production, quality, packaging, and shipping.

## Order Minimums and Sample Orders

There are no order minimums on any product in the Completeful catalog. You can order a single unit at any time.

Sample orders are available through the app. Ordering a sample before you list a product is strongly recommended — it lets you verify print quality, packaging, and delivery time from the perspective of your customer.

## Tracking Your Orders

Active orders are visible in the Orders section of the Completeful app. Each order shows its current production stage, estimated ship date, and tracking number once the item has shipped.

For questions about a specific order, contact support@completeful.com with your order number.
