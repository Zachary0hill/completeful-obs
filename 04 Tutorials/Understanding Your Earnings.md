\[Screen Recording: Apply a custom date range, then show the table and totals updating.\] Once you apply the range, watch the ledger table and totals update together. **\[Chapter 4 6:45\] How to read ledger entries and totals**

Setup: A lot of sellers jump straight to the total and ignore the details underneath. But the total only becomes useful when you understand the rows that built it. \[B-roll: Cursor hovers over total, then moves down into transaction rows.\] \[Text Overlay: Read totals through the ledger\]

Tension: \[Screen Recording: Slowly scroll through the ledger entries table.\] Start by reading the ledger one row at a time. Look for the date, the transaction description or type, the related account, and the amount shown for that entry. Think of each row as one piece of the earnings story for the selected period. \[Text Overlay: Date • Type • Account • Amount\]

\[Screen Recording: Click or highlight a few different row examples.\] Some entries may represent incoming earnings activity, while others may reflect adjustments or transaction history that changes the running picture for that period. The important thing is not to assume every row means the same thing. Read the entry label first, then the amount. \[Text Overlay: Read the label before the amount\] \[Pattern Interrupt: Freeze frame on an entry label with a red circle.\]

\[Screen Recording: Move to the totals area at the top or side of the page.\] After you review a few rows, return to the totals. The totals summarize the entries included under your active filters. So if the totals seem off, don't start with the number. Start with the filters. \[Pattern Interrupt: Highlight total card, then flash text: Totals depend on filters.\]

Payoff: Once you read the rows and the totals together, the Earnings page becomes a decision tool, not just a scoreboard.

## BONUS

**\[Chapter 6 9:36\] The fastest accuracy check before trusting a total**

The fastest way to check whether a total is trustworthy before you export or report it:

Step one: confirm the connected account. \[Screen Recording: Open account selector\] If you sell on more than one storefront, make sure you're reviewing the right Etsy account first.

Step two: reset the date range to a known period. \[Screen Recording: Switch to a preset like This Month, then Custom Range\] If you accidentally left a narrow custom range active, your totals may look lower simply because you're excluding entries.

Step three: compare totals to the visible ledger list. \[Screen Recording: Scroll from totals down into entries\] If entries appear to match the period and account you selected, the page is usually working as intended and the issue is interpretation, not missing data.

If something still doesn't look right after those checks, document the date range, account selected, and a few example entries before reaching out to support@completeful.com. That gives the team what they need to help you faster.
