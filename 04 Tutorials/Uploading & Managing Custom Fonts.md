## BONUS

Earlier I mentioned I'd share the easiest way to avoid the font mistakes that cause messy mockups and rejected artwork. Here it is.

Create a simple font approval checklist before you publish any product. \[Text Overlay: Font Approval Checklist\] First, confirm the font matches the product type and decoration style. Second, test it at the actual size your customer will receive. Third, review spacing, line breaks, and contrast on the final product color. Fourth, generate a mockup and ask one simple question: is this readable and on-brand in two seconds or less? \[Screen Recording: Run through checklist while reviewing a product in Completeful\] If the answer is no, revise before you publish.

This one habit gives you a cleaner storefront, more consistent branding, and fewer design problems later when orders begin syncing automatically through your store. That matters when you are building a business on demand, because Completeful lets you start free, sell with no upfront capital, and scale product by product instead of buying inventory first.
