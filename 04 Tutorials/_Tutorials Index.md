# Tutorials Index

This index organizes all Completeful app tutorials into four groups that follow the natural workflow of a seller: getting started, designing products, creating listings, and managing orders and earnings. Use it as a starting point when onboarding new team members or when you need to find a specific how-to quickly.

---

## Getting Started

These tutorials cover the foundational steps every seller completes before building out their product catalog or listings.

- **Connecting Your Store** — How to link Shopify, Etsy, TikTok Shop, or WooCommerce to your Completeful account via the Connect Store flow in Settings
- **Setting Up Your Account** — Profile configuration, wallet setup, and adding payment methods
- **Ordering a Sample** — How to place a sample order through the app to verify product quality before listing

---

## Designing

These tutorials cover the design tools inside the Completeful app, from the core design editor to specialized tools for listing images.

- **Using the Design Editor** — Building a product design using the drag-and-drop editor, applying text, uploading artwork, and saving your design to My Products
- **Using Design Templates** — Browsing and customizing pre-built design templates to accelerate product creation
- **Uploading Custom Fonts** — Adding your own font files to the editor for brand-consistent typography
- [[Creating Product Infographics]] — Using the Infographic Editor to create multi-panel listing images that explain product features, sizing, and personalization options
- [[Browsing & Filtering the product catalog]] — Exploring the 500-900+ product catalog, applying filters by category, production method, and price range, and adding products to My Products

---

## Listing

These tutorials cover publishing products to your connected sales channels.

- [[Creating listings for Shopify]] — Step-by-step guide to creating and publishing a product listing on your connected Shopify store, including title, description, variants, pricing, and images
- **Creating Listings for Etsy** — How to create and publish listings to your Etsy shop through the Completeful app, including tag and category recommendations for Etsy SEO
- **Creating Listings for TikTok Shop** — Setting up TikTok Shop product listings and connecting them to Completeful fulfillment

---

## Orders and Earnings

These tutorials cover the post-sale flow: how orders move through production, how to track them, and how to read your earnings data.

- [[Understanding Orders & Fulfillment aka how Print on Demand works with completeful]] — A full walkthrough of how an order moves from customer purchase through Completeful production and shipping to delivery, with no inventory required on your end
- **Reading the Earnings Page** — Understanding the earnings dashboard, including the Etsy ledger sync that pulls your sales revenue and production costs into a single view
- **Using the Profit Calculator** — (Coming soon) How to use the profit calculator tool to model retail prices and margins before creating a listing
- **Managing the Wallet and Payouts** — Adding a payment method, reviewing charges, and understanding how Completeful deducts production costs from your wallet
