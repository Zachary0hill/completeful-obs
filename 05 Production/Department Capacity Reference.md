# Department Capacity Reference

This page provides a quick-reference summary of per-department production equipment counts, output rates, and product types. Use it for internal capacity planning, seller communication about turnaround expectations, and onboarding team members who need to understand the scope of the Lafayette, LA facility.

All figures reflect normal operating conditions. Peak season (primarily October through December) increases throughput through added operators and extended shifts. See the Peak Season Operations page for peak-specific details.

---

## Metal

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Equipment | 6 fiber laser machines |
| Finish options | Laser engraving, powder coating |
| Product types | Metal signs, tags, awards, custom metalwork |
| Capacity notes | Capacity scales with laser run time; powder coating adds one additional processing step |

---

## Wood

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Equipment | CNC routers |
| Product types | Cutting boards, picture frames, wood plaques |
| UV plank output | Approximately 1,000 UV-printed wood planks per day |
| Capacity notes | CNC routing for shaping plus separate UV print pass for surface printing |

---

## UV Printing

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Primary products | Rubber mats, picture frames, rigid flat substrates |
| Mat capacity | Up to 600 orders per day |
| Frame capacity | 150 to 250 orders per day |
| Capacity notes | Mat and frame lines run independently; total UV capacity is additive |

---

## Embroidery

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Equipment | 16 embroidery machines |
| Product types | Apparel, hats, bags, fabric goods |
| Capacity notes | Multi-head machines allow concurrent stitching across multiple units per run; stitch count per design affects per-unit time |

---

## Engraving

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Equipment | 28 laser engraving machines |
| Normal operator count | Scales with volume |
| Peak operator count | 6 operators |
| Peak daily output | Approximately 600 orders per day |
| Peak event benchmark | 10,000+ orders shipped November 20 to December 20 |
| Primary category | Personalized gifts; wedding focus |
| Capacity notes | Highest single-department volume during Q4 holiday season; wedding engraving peaks in spring and fall as well |

---

## Sublimation

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Equipment | 20 automatic heat presses |
| Product types | 11 oz mugs, 15 oz mugs, tote bags, puzzles |
| Capacity notes | Automatic presses reduce operator-per-unit labor versus manual heat presses; consistent pressure and temperature improve color consistency |

---

## DTG (Direct to Garment)

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Method | Direct-to-garment inkjet printing |
| Supported blanks | Gildan, Hanes, Bella Canvas |
| Product types | T-shirts, hoodies, and other apparel items |
| Capacity notes | DTG throughput depends on design complexity and ink coverage; pre-treatment step required for dark garments |
