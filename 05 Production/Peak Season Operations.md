# Peak Season Operations

Peak season for Completeful spans the final two months of the calendar year, with the highest concentration of orders arriving in November and December. The facility's demonstrated capacity during this window — over 10,000 engraving orders shipped in the November 20 to December 20 period alone — reflects both the scale of the Lafayette, LA operation and the operational discipline required to fulfill personalized orders at volume without sacrificing quality.

## Why Peak Season Is Different

Print on demand for personalized products is inherently labor and machine intensive. Unlike apparel warehouses that pick pre-made items, every order at Completeful triggers a production run. During peak season, multiple factors converge:

- Holiday gifting demand spikes order volume across all product categories
- Personalized gifts (engraved items, custom mugs, embroidered goods) are among the most popular gift categories
- Wedding season spillover into fall adds engraving volume
- Sellers run promotions that concentrate order arrival into tight windows (Black Friday, Cyber Monday, 12 Days of Christmas)

## Capacity During Peak

### Engraving Department
The engraving department carries the largest peak load because personalized gifts — particularly wood and metal items with names, dates, and custom messages — are top-selling holiday products. Key peak stats:

- 28 laser engraving machines running at full capacity
- Up to 6 operators per shift managing machine loads, quality checks, and order flow
- Approximately 600 orders per day at peak
- Over 10,000 orders shipped in the November 20 to December 20 window

### UV Printing (Mats and Frames)
Mats reach 600 orders per day and frames run 150 to 250 orders per day. Both lines continue at full throughput during the peak window.

### Wood (UV Planks)
The wood department maintains approximately 1,000 UV-printed wood planks per day during peak operations.

### Sublimation
The 20 automatic heat presses on mugs, totes, and puzzles run at capacity. Mugs are consistently in the top product categories for holiday gifting and custom drinkware.

### DTG and Embroidery
Apparel and embroidery departments absorb increased volume driven by branded holiday apparel and gift orders on Gildan, Hanes, and Bella Canvas blanks.

## How Completeful Manages Peak Volume

- **Staffing up** — Operators are added across departments, particularly in engraving and UV printing, to cover higher machine loads and quality inspection steps
- **Extended run times** — Machines run longer daily shifts during the November and December peak window
- **Order prioritization** — Rush or time-sensitive orders are flagged and routed through the production queue accordingly
- **Quality maintenance** — All orders still pass through quality inspection before shipping, even at peak volume

## What Sellers Should Know

**Plan listings early.** Products listed and promoted before peak season sell more consistently than last-minute additions. Getting designs, listings, and infographics finalized before October gives sellers the lead time to build sales momentum.

**Set customer expectations for delivery.** During peak, production times may be slightly extended due to volume. Build realistic shipping windows into your listing descriptions and any customer communications.

**Sample orders take priority.** If you want to validate a new product before peak season, place a sample order in September or early October. Waiting until November reduces your lead time significantly.

**No order minimums apply year-round.** Even during peak season, there are no minimum order quantities. A single order for a custom cutting board receives the same production and fulfillment care as a bulk run.

## Contact for Peak Season Planning

For sellers planning large-scale promotions or anticipating high order volumes during the holiday season, contact sales@completeful.com in advance. The Completeful team can advise on realistic production windows and help structure your promotion calendar around peak capacity.
