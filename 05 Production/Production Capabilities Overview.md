# Production Capabilities Overview

Completeful operates a 228,000 square foot production and fulfillment facility in Lafayette, Louisiana, co-run by CEO Josh Goree. Unlike pure print-on-demand platforms that outsource production to third parties, Completeful handles manufacturing in-house across six distinct production departments. This vertical integration gives Completeful control over quality, turnaround times, and the ability to scale during peak demand.

## The Model: POD, 3PL, and In-House Manufacturing

Completeful operates as a hybrid of three fulfillment models:

- **Print on Demand (POD)** — Products are manufactured individually as orders arrive; no pre-built inventory required
- **Third-Party Logistics (3PL)** — Completeful handles receiving, storage, pick-pack, and shipping for sellers who want managed inventory fulfillment
- **In-House US Manufacturing** — Core production capabilities (laser engraving, UV printing, sublimation, CNC routing, embroidery, DTG) are performed at the Lafayette facility by Completeful staff and equipment

This combination allows Completeful to serve sellers across a wide range of product types, order volumes, and fulfillment needs — all with no order minimums and transparent, published pricing.

## Production Departments

### Metal
The metal department uses six fiber laser machines to engrave and cut metal products. Powder coating is available for color finishing. Metal products include custom signs, tags, awards, and other engraved metalwork.

### Wood
CNC routers handle precision cutting and shaping of wood blanks. The department produces cutting boards, picture frames, and wood plaques. The UV printing line prints directly onto wood surfaces, with a capacity of approximately 1,000 UV-printed wood planks per day.

### UV Printing
A dedicated UV printing line handles non-wood flat products including rubber mats and other rigid substrates. Mats reach up to 600 orders per day. Picture frames processed on this line run 150 to 250 orders per day.

### Embroidery
Sixteen embroidery machines handle stitched decoration on apparel, hats, bags, and other fabric goods. Embroidery is used for both custom text and logo applications.

### Engraving
Twenty-eight laser engraving machines serve the high-demand personalization segment — particularly wedding and gifting products. At peak capacity with six operators, the department handles approximately 600 orders per day. The department has shipped over 10,000 orders in a single two-day window (November 20 to December 20), demonstrating its capacity during peak holiday season.

### Sublimation
Twenty automatic heat presses handle sublimation printing on 11 oz mugs, 15 oz mugs, tote bags, and puzzles. Sublimation produces vibrant, full-color, dye-infused prints that are embedded into the product surface rather than applied on top.

### DTG (Direct to Garment)
DTG printing applies full-color designs directly to fabric using specialized inkjet technology. Completeful prints on Gildan, Hanes, and Bella Canvas blank apparel, covering a broad range of shirt styles, weights, and sizes.

## Quality and Shipping

All completed orders pass through a quality inspection step before packaging. Orders are shipped direct-to-consumer under the seller's brand — buyers receive their order from the seller's store, not from Completeful. Tracking information is uploaded to the seller's connected platform automatically.

## Integrations That Trigger Production

Orders route to production automatically when placed on any connected sales channel:

- Shopify
- Etsy
- TikTok Shop
- WooCommerce
