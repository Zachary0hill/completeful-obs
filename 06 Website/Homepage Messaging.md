# Homepage Messaging

This page documents the messaging framework for the Completeful homepage. It captures the approved tagline, core value proposition statements, supporting proof points, and the tone standards that govern how the homepage communicates to prospective sellers.

## Tagline

**You Design and Sell It, We Create and Fulfill It.**

This tagline is the clearest possible expression of the Completeful model. It divides responsibility explicitly: the seller handles design and sales; Completeful handles everything that happens after — production, quality, packaging, and shipment. Use this line as the primary headline or an anchor statement on the homepage. It should appear above the fold.

## Core Value Proposition

The homepage must answer three questions for a first-time visitor within the first scroll:

**What is Completeful?**
Completeful is a print-on-demand and fulfillment platform that manufactures products in-house at a 228,000 sq ft facility in Lafayette, Louisiana. Sellers connect their Shopify, Etsy, TikTok Shop, or WooCommerce store, design products in the Completeful app, and start selling — with no inventory risk and no order minimums.

**Why is it different from other POD platforms?**
- In-house US manufacturing across six production departments (not outsourced)
- No order minimums on a catalog of 500 to 900-plus products
- Transparent pricing: sellers see the exact base production cost before listing
- Integrated design tools, listing creation, and earnings tracking in one app

**Who is it for?**
Independent e-commerce sellers building their own brand on Shopify, Etsy, TikTok Shop, or WooCommerce who want to sell custom, personalized, or branded products without holding inventory.

## Supporting Proof Points

These statements can be used as secondary callouts, feature highlights, or section headers throughout the homepage:

- "No order minimums — ever." (Reinforces accessibility for new and small sellers)
- "500+ products. One platform." (Communicates catalog breadth)
- "Designed in the app. Shipped from Louisiana." (Emphasizes in-house US manufacturing)
- "Connected to Shopify, Etsy, TikTok Shop, and WooCommerce." (Confirms platform compatibility)
- "Transparent pricing. Your margins, your business." (Positions the cost model as honest and seller-first)

## Calls to Action

Primary CTA: **Start Selling** or **Get Started** — leads to sign-up flow
Secondary CTA: **Browse the Catalog** — leads to the product catalog page without requiring a sign-up

Both CTAs should be present above the fold. The primary CTA should use the brand Teal (#1ABC9C) button style. The secondary CTA can use a lighter treatment (outlined button or text link in Accent Blue #3A7BD5).

## Tone Standards

Homepage copy follows the broader Completeful voice:

- **Clear** — no jargon, no vague platform language like "seamless synergy"; every sentence earns its place
- **Helpful** — the reader is an e-commerce seller trying to build a business; speak to their goals and problems
- **Seller-first** — position features in terms of what they mean for the seller, not for Completeful's operational efficiency
- **No emojis** in finalized copy — the brand communicates through words and design, not emoji shorthand
- **No dashes** in finalized social copy or headlines — use sentence construction that does not rely on punctuation for rhythm

## What the Homepage Should Not Do

- Should not bury the pricing model or require visitors to dig for base cost information
- Should not over-promise turnaround times without qualification
- Should not use competitor names or make comparative claims that require legal review
- Should not use stock photography that does not reflect the actual Lafayette facility or the real product range

## Brand Colors for Homepage Use

- Navy #1B2A4A — primary background or text on light surfaces
- Teal #1ABC9C — primary action elements (buttons, highlights, key statistics)
- Accent Blue #3A7BD5 — secondary links, supporting highlights

Type stack: Inter for body, Montserrat for subheadings, Bebas Neue for large display text.
