# Site Map and Key Pages

This page documents the structure of the Completeful website (completeful.com) and describes the purpose and key content of each major page. Use it to orient new team members, plan content updates, or identify gaps between what the site currently shows and what it should communicate.

## Top-Level Navigation

The primary navigation of completeful.com includes:

- **Home** — Main entry point; communicates the value proposition, tagline, and primary seller benefits
- **Products / Catalog** — Browsable listing of available print-on-demand products organized by category
- **Pricing** — Transparent base cost structure; reinforces the no-hidden-fees positioning
- **How It Works** — Explains the end-to-end POD + fulfillment workflow for sellers new to the platform
- **Blog** — Educational content for e-commerce sellers at completeful.com/blog
- **Login / Sign Up** — Entry point to the Completeful app

## Key Pages

### Home
The homepage delivers the Completeful brand promise in the first scroll. The tagline "You Design and Sell It, We Create and Fulfill It" anchors the page. The home page must communicate:

- What Completeful does (POD + 3PL + in-house US manufacturing)
- Who it is for (independent e-commerce sellers on Shopify, Etsy, TikTok Shop, WooCommerce)
- Why Completeful is different (no order minimums, transparent pricing, in-house production in Lafayette, LA)
- A clear call to action to sign up or explore the catalog

### Products / Catalog Page
Mirrors the in-app catalog experience for web visitors who have not yet created an account. Displays the 500-900+ product catalog organized by category (Wood, Metal, Drinkware, Apparel, Home and Accessories). Each product entry shows the product name, a representative image, base price, and production method.

### Pricing Page
Completeful's transparent pricing is a key differentiator. The pricing page explains:

- Base production costs by product category
- No order minimums on any product
- How sellers set their own retail price and keep the margin above base cost
- Any applicable shipping or platform fee structures

### How It Works Page
A step-by-step explanation of the seller workflow:

1. Browse the catalog and select products
2. Design using the app's design editor or upload artwork
3. Connect a sales channel (Shopify, Etsy, TikTok Shop, WooCommerce)
4. Create listings and publish
5. Customer orders; Completeful produces and ships
6. Seller receives the margin between retail price and production cost

### Blog (completeful.com/blog)
The blog serves both SEO and seller education purposes. See the Blog Strategy and SEO page for full content direction.

### Contact and Support
- Support inquiries: support@completeful.com
- Sales and onboarding: sales@completeful.com
- Contact form available on the website for general inquiries

## Social and External Presence

In addition to the main site, Completeful maintains active profiles on:

- Instagram: @completeful
- X (Twitter): @completefultech
- TikTok: @completeful
- LinkedIn: /company/completeful
- YouTube: @Completeful

These channels drive traffic to completeful.com and support seller acquisition and community building. Social content strategy should align with the brand voice guidelines: clear, helpful, seller-first, no emojis or dashes in finalized copy.

## Notes for Internal Use

When updating site pages, check that base cost data and product counts (currently 500-900+) are current. The catalog count changes as new products are added, so page copy referencing specific numbers should be reviewed quarterly. Brand colors Navy #1B2A4A, Teal #1ABC9C, and Accent Blue #3A7BD5 govern all site design elements.
