# Standing Agendas

These are the recurring meeting formats used across Completeful's marketing, content, and operations teams. Use these as the default Agenda section when filling out [[_Meeting Notes Template]].

---

## Weekly Marketing Sync

**Cadence:** Weekly (suggested: Monday morning)
**Duration:** 45–60 minutes
**Core Attendees:** Zach (marketing/content/automation), Josh Goree (CEO), and any relevant contributors
**Purpose:** Align on the week's content priorities, review performance from the prior week, unblock any campaign or automation issues, and confirm active publishing deadlines.

### Agenda

1. **Prior week recap** (10 min)
   - Top-performing posts or content by engagement, saves, shares
   - Any notable platform changes (TikTok algorithm, IG reach shifts, etc.)
   - Automation wins or failures from the Content Orchestra pipeline

2. **Content calendar review** (15 min)
   - What is scheduled and confirmed for this week across IG, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, YouTube
   - Any gaps that need to be filled
   - Upcoming product launches, promotions, or seasonal moments to address

3. **Campaign or project updates** (10 min)
   - Status of active campaigns, paid promotions, or collaborations
   - Creator or partner outreach in progress

4. **Blockers and decisions needed** (10 min)
   - Anything stalled in the Notion content DB or n8n pipeline
   - Tool or API issues requiring a decision

5. **This week's priorities** (5 min)
   - Confirm top 3 priorities for the week
   - Assign any outstanding action items

---

## Dev & Strategy Meeting

**Cadence:** Bi-weekly or as needed
**Duration:** 60–90 minutes
**Core Attendees:** Zach, Josh, and any product/dev contributors
**Purpose:** Review the state of the automation stack, evaluate new tool integrations, align on product-side marketing needs (app updates, new catalog items, integrations), and plan medium-term roadmap items.

### Agenda

1. **Automation stack review** (15 min)
   - n8n workflow status and any workflow failures from the past period
   - Agent updates: Conductor, Strategy, Hook Generator, Social Writer, Long-Form Writer, QC, Formatter, Atomizer, Publisher, Analyst
   - Any OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Remotion, Canva, or Photoroom API changes or quota issues

2. **Product and platform updates** (15 min)
   - New Completeful catalog additions (approaching or at 900+ SKUs)
   - Integration updates: Shopify, Etsy, TikTok Shop, WooCommerce
   - App feature changes that need marketing support

3. **New tool or integration evaluation** (15 min)
   - Any tools under consideration for the stack
   - Cost, complexity, and value assessment
   - Decision: adopt, trial, defer, or reject (log in [[Decision Log]])

4. **Roadmap alignment** (15 min)
   - Next major content or marketing initiative
   - Any infrastructure work required before the next campaign
   - Timeline and ownership confirmation

5. **Open items** (15 min)

---

## Content Review Meeting

**Cadence:** Weekly or before a major publishing push
**Duration:** 30–45 minutes
**Core Attendees:** Zach and any content contributors or QC reviewers
**Purpose:** Review content generated by the AI pipeline before it goes live, ensure brand voice compliance, check for accuracy in product or pricing references, and approve the publishing queue.

### Agenda

1. **Pipeline output review** (15 min)
   - Walk through content items in the Notion content DB that have passed QC agent review but await human approval
   - Flag any items that do not reflect Completeful brand voice (coaching tone, seller-first, no emojis or dashes in finalized social copy)
   - Flag any factual errors (product names, pricing, supported platforms)

2. **Visual and mockup review** (10 min)
   - Review Photoroom and Nano Banana Pro mockup outputs
   - Confirm Canva-generated carousel layouts match brand colors (Pink #ED128C, gradient #FF2B92 to #FF8433, Navy #34424E) and typography (Owners or Archivo headlines, Inter body)
   - Review any Remotion video outputs

3. **Publishing queue confirmation** (10 min)
   - Confirm scheduled posts across all channels for the next 5–7 days
   - Confirm captions, hashtags, and links are correct
   - Approve or send back any items requiring revision

4. **Notes for the pipeline** (5 min)
   - Feedback to refine agent prompts or workflow logic based on today's review
   - Any recurring errors to address in the QC agent
