### Livestream & Marketing

- Goal is to create case studies and success stories from the start

### Cold Outreach Progress

- Currently have 18 cold outreach emails sent, already received positive response from someone interested in outsourcing personalization
- Main bottleneck is time split between Shopify work and obtaining quality data for pipeline
- Existing data source from last spring has same problem - only generic store emails (info@storename format)
- Testing RocketReach which shows much better hit rate than other tools like Apollo
- Apollo data insufficient for finding small businesses
- Will likely need to pay for validated email source rather than risk high bounce rates that could shut down outreach accounts

### Product Catalog Management

- Product project is ongoing and continuously evolving as new details are identified
- No firm deadline - will be a perpetual project with semi-annual audits of all products
- This is considered the most important project that has never been done well before
- Starting with products currently live on app, then moving to highest volume items for integrated stores
- Not prioritizing low-volume dead inventory initially - will address when time permits
- Currently have approximately 300 unique SKUs that create over 2,000 product variants
- The 900+ number is partly marketing positioning and partly for better user experience (organizing designs by occasion like Mother's Day, Father's Day, birthdays)
- App currently shows 99 products, though this number changes daily with new products, variants, and designs

### Business Performance & Outlook

- Valentine's Day performance was disappointing with minimal rush
- Hoping for better results from Mother's Day and Father's Day while building evergreen business for sustainability
- All SLAs now completed, freeing up team (especially Haley) to focus on generating new business
- New billable customer Punk Sauce shipping today - similar size to H&H with same rates and pick rates

### Pricing & Monetization Strategy

- Ongoing debate about offering tools for free vs. paid access
- Concerns about market dilution if everything is free, but also need to avoid pricing out potential users
- Considering tiered approach: free for certain users (possibly based on revenue benchmarks), paid for others
- Josh advocates for free to win through speed; others concerned about capturing revenue for time/effort invested
- Viewpoint that software is going to zero - need to differentiate through logistics and operations
- Goal is competitive pricing without being cheapest or most expensive
- Considering higher entry price with membership that includes software + discount, still better than Printify/Printful
- May offer free access for sellers doing under $50K-$100K/month

### CompleteFull App Vision & Features

#### Listing Marketplace

- Plan to pull all Guru listings into app as ready-to-publish perfect listings
- Pricing model: $1 per listing for users to add to their store
- Example: If 10 stores use 1,000 listings = $10K/month; 100 stores = $100K/month
- Listings will be exclusive to CompleteFull - not available on Printify/Printful
- Users can quickly browse and launch listings with simple clicks

#### Design Marketplace

- Enabling collaborators to create and sell designs through the app
- Creators set their own prices, CompleteFull takes a cut
- Users can apply designs to products, use AI to create listing, and publish
- Designers earn royalties on every sale of their design

#### Brand Automation Tools

- Building brand guide feature where users input logo and brand details
- System automatically applies branding across all products
- Working on local theme development for instant Shopify store creation with exchangeable brand elements

#### Store Creation Process

- Goal: Enable complete beginners to have a live store within 4 hours or half a day
- Vision includes automated setup through app with simple button clicks
- Considering AI chat interface similar to Whop's "start a business" feature that asks questions and sets everything up

#### Sample/First Order Program

- Planning discount program for first orders, similar to Printful's model
- Example cited: 3 items once per month at 30% off to encourage engagement

### Frontend Design Work

- Currently building through Shopify, considering switching to local development with Cursor
- For immediate Friday deadline, may stick with Shopify approach
- Focusing on replicability rather than custom work for each product
- Setting up interchangeable brand colors (e.g., dark green to dark blue) and fonts for easy replication across stores

### Go-to-Market Strategy: Creating a "Seller Farm"

- Core vision is to be in the business of creating sellers, not just selling products
- Promise: Follow steps, within 6 months make minimum $5,000/month
- Target market: 17-30 age range
- Strategy compared to giving "first bump for free" - get people hooked on entrepreneurial cash flow, they keep coming back
- Addressing stigma that dropshipping is a scam by showing it's legitimate print-on-demand with real facility and real results
- Everything should be done within CompleteFull app without leaving the ecosystem

- Already has TikTok following and is in the right demographic
- Plan for Zach to be the young entrepreneur showcasing success to attract peers
- Zach acknowledges challenge with public speaking - gets nervous and stumbles when speaking to groups despite knowing the material

- Story shared about learning sales through immersion and just doing it
- Zach has YouTube equipment and plans to create tutorials
- Has experience with dropshipping since age 14, rode the wave of trends, good perspective for target audience
- Showing real sales numbers from Etsy and TikTok Shop to potential sellers
