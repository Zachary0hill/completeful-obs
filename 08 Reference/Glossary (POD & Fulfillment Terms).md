# Glossary — POD & Fulfillment Terms

This glossary defines industry terms used across Completeful's operations, marketing, and content. Use this as a reference when writing content, onboarding contributors, or explaining Completeful's services to a new audience. Terms are listed alphabetically within categories.

---

## Print-on-Demand (POD)

**Print-on-Demand (POD)**
A fulfillment model in which products are printed only after an order is placed — no inventory is pre-produced or held. Completeful operates as a POD + 3PL + in-house US manufacturing business, meaning sellers list designs and Completeful produces and ships the order when a customer buys.

**DTG (Direct-to-Garment)**
A printing method that uses inkjet technology to apply ink directly onto fabric. Best for full-color, photo-realistic, or complex designs on cotton garments. Produces soft, high-quality prints.

**DTF (Direct-to-Film)**
A printing method where ink is printed onto a transfer film and then heat-pressed onto a garment. DTF works on a wider range of fabric types than DTG and handles detailed designs well.

**Sublimation**
A dye-infusion process where heat converts solid dye into gas, which bonds permanently with polyester fabric or polymer-coated items. Produces vibrant, edge-to-edge prints that will not crack or peel. Only works on light-colored, poly-based substrates.

**UV Printing**
A printing method using ultraviolet light to cure ink instantly as it is applied to a surface. Used for hard goods like phone cases, tumblers, and rigid products. Enables printing on non-porous materials that DTG or DTF cannot handle.

**Embroidery**
Decorating fabric with needle and thread using automated embroidery machines. Common on hats, polos, and apparel where a structured, premium look is desired. Limited to designs that translate well into thread paths — very fine detail or gradients do not reproduce cleanly.

**Engraving**
Removing material from a surface — typically via laser — to create a permanent, recessed design. Used on hard goods such as drinkware, wood, acrylic, or metal. Often associated with high-perceived-value personalized gifts.

## Fulfillment & Operations

**3PL (Third-Party Logistics)**
A company that handles storage, picking, packing, and shipping on behalf of brands or sellers. Completeful operates as its own 3PL from its 228,000 sq ft Lafayette, LA facility, meaning it controls the fulfillment process end-to-end rather than outsourcing to a separate warehouse partner.

**Fulfillment**
The complete process of receiving an order, producing or picking the product, packaging it, and shipping it to the end customer. Completeful's model combines in-house production and fulfillment under one roof.

**Kitting**
Assembling multiple individual products into a single packaged set or bundle before shipping. For example, combining a custom t-shirt, a mug, and a card into one gift box. Kitting is often used for bundles, subscription boxes, or promotional packages.

**Private Label**
Producing products under a seller's own brand identity — using their custom tags, packaging, inserts, or branding rather than the manufacturer's. Sellers using Completeful can ship orders with their own branding rather than Completeful's, preserving the seller's customer relationship.

**Pick and Pack**
The warehouse operation of selecting (picking) the correct items from storage and packaging (packing) them for shipment. In a POD context, most items go directly from production to pack rather than being shelved first.

**Dropshipping**
A retail fulfillment method where the seller does not hold inventory. Orders are forwarded to the supplier (in this context, Completeful), which produces and ships directly to the end customer. POD is a form of dropshipping.

## E-Commerce & Seller Terms

**AOV (Average Order Value)**
The average dollar amount a customer spends per order. Calculated as total revenue divided by number of orders over a given period. Sellers track AOV to evaluate upsell effectiveness and pricing strategy.

**SKU (Stock Keeping Unit)**
A unique identifier assigned to each distinct product variant. For a t-shirt available in 3 colors and 5 sizes, that is 15 SKUs. Completeful's catalog contains 500 to 900+ SKUs across all product categories.

**Storefront**
A seller's online store — on Etsy, Shopify, WooCommerce, TikTok Shop, or other platforms — where they list products and process orders. Completeful integrates with all of these platforms to receive and fulfill orders automatically.

**Order Minimum (MOQ — Minimum Order Quantity)**
The smallest quantity a supplier will accept per order. Completeful operates with no order minimums, meaning sellers can place single-unit orders without any volume commitment.

**Mockup**
A digital representation of a product with a design applied, used for product listings before any physical item is produced. Completeful's pipeline uses Photoroom and Nano Banana Pro to generate mockup images for seller and content use.

**Listing**
A product page on a marketplace or storefront. A seller's listing typically includes mockup images, a title, description, price, and variants (size, color, etc.).

## Platform & Integration Terms

**Shopify**
A hosted e-commerce platform. One of Completeful's primary integration targets, allowing Shopify store owners to connect their storefront and route orders directly to Completeful for fulfillment.

**Etsy**
A marketplace focused on handmade, vintage, and custom goods. A large portion of Completeful's seller base runs Etsy shops, making it a primary channel reference in content strategy.

**TikTok Shop**
TikTok's native commerce feature allowing sellers to sell directly through TikTok content and a storefront tab. Completeful integrates with TikTok Shop and has identified it as a primary growth channel for 2026.

**WooCommerce**
An open-source e-commerce plugin for WordPress. Completeful integrates with WooCommerce, giving self-hosted WordPress store owners access to the fulfillment platform.

**Integration**
A technical connection between Completeful's fulfillment platform and a seller's storefront, enabling orders to flow automatically without manual entry.
