01

Brand foundation

Completeful is the only 3PL built for personalized products. Traditional fulfillment centers ship pre-made inventory. Completeful engraves, prints, embroiders and assembles on demand, then ships from a single warehouse in Lafayette, Louisiana.

Positioning statement

3PL and print on demand under one roof. One vendor, one warehouse, one point of accountability, for brands whose products carry a customer's name on them.

Capabilities to name by their real names

Laser engraving. UV printing. Embroidery. DTG apparel. Custom assembly and kitting. Warehousing and pick, pack, ship. Integrations with Shopify, TikTok Shop and Etsy.

Voice

Sounds like Completeful Direct and operational. Specific numbers over adjectives. Names the failure the customer already had with another vendor, then names the fix. Short sentences. Second person. Confident without hype.

"Stop juggling multiple vendors. We personalize on demand and ship direct, all under one roof."
Does not sound like Completeful Corporate logistics filler. Unbacked superlatives. Vague promises with no number attached. Startup hype language. Exclamation points.

"We are passionate about revolutionizing your fulfillment journey!"

Naming

Completeful, one word, capital C, no space, no hyphen. Never "Complete Ful", "CompleteFul" or "Completefull". Social handle @completefultech.

Logo

The mark is a circular arrow closing a loop around a checkmark: an order going out and coming back complete. It works locked up with the wordmark, or alone once the audience knows the brand.

Clear space

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the emblem's circle, on all four sides. Nothing enters that zone: no text, no rules, no photo edges, no other logos.

Backgrounds

On white and light neutrals use the gradient or pink lockup. On black, navy or pink use the white knockout. On photography, place the white knockout over a dark, low detail area, or add a solid brand-color band behind it. Never place the gradient logo on a colored background.

Do
  • Use the supplied files at their supplied proportions
  • Scale from a corner so the aspect ratio holds
  • Switch to white knockout the moment contrast drops
  • Use the SVG for anything above 800 px wide or anything printed
Do not
  • Recolor, add strokes, shadows, bevels or glows
  • Stretch, condense, rotate or skew
  • Put the gradient lockup on colored or busy backgrounds
  • Rebuild the wordmark in another typeface
  • Box the logo in a shape that is not in this kit
  • Use the emblem and wordmark at different tints

Rules

Do
  • Set headlines uppercase with tight negative tracking
  • Keep body copy sentence case at 1.6 line height
  • Use gradient text on one word per headline at most
  • Let Inter carry every paragraph, table and form label
Do not
  • Set body copy in Owners
  • Mix Inter and Work Sans in the same block
  • Add a third display face for one campaign
  • Set headlines in all caps under 14px without tracking
  • Use light weights, the brand is bold or regular

All buttons: 8px radius, uppercase, 1px tracking, Owners Bold, 14px padding block and 24px inline. Press state drops to scale 0.98. Transitions run 300ms ease out.

Cards

White fill, 1px #E5E7EB border, 1.5rem radius, 1.5rem padding, no resting shadow. On hover the border shifts to rgba(237,18,140,.2), a soft pink shadow appears and the card lifts 2px. Radius ladder: xl 1rem, 2xl 1.5rem, 3xl 2rem.

Motion

300ms, ease out, on transform and shadow. Lifts are 2px. Scales are 1.02 up and 0.98 down. Nothing bounces, nothing spins.

Iconography

134 line and glyph icons covering apparel, packaging, shipping states and manufacturing equipment. Three color builds ship: black, pink and currentColor.

Rules

ContextBuildColor
Web and app UISVG_CurrentColorInherits text color, recolor with CSS
Brand marketing, decks, one pagersSVG_Pink#ED128C
Print, labels, documentsSVG_Black#000000

Sit icons on a 24px box in UI. Pair them with a text label unless the meaning is universal. Do not fill icons with the gradient, do not outline them, do not mix icon sets inside one screen.

Known gapThe set was assembled from several sources, so viewBoxes vary across 26, 32, 50 and 64 units and stroke weights are not uniform. Icons look consistent within a group but not always across groups. Standardizing to a single 24px grid with one stroke weight is on the open items list.
07

Photography and graphics

Real facility, real product, real hands. Completeful sells operational credibility, so the imagery has to look like a working warehouse, not a stock photo.

Subjects that work

The Lafayette warehouse floor and racking. Machines mid-run: laser engraver, UV printer, embroidery head, DTG. Personalized product close ups where the customer's name is legible. Packed boxes with branded inserts. People working, hands in frame.

Treatment

Natural light, neutral white balance, no heavy filters. Keep pink as an accent inside the frame, never as an overlay across it. Text over photography needs either a dark low detail area or a solid brand color band.

Process graphics

The four step "order to doorstep" infographic set lives in 07_Photography_Graphics/. Keep the four step structure: Connect your store, Send us your inventory, We pick, personalize and pack, Fast shipping.
