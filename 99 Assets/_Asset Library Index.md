## Logos & Brand Files

**Drive folder:** https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1VDTGZLhvCBgocyynfCfZgIwrvmeuxsFM

Contains the full set of Completeful logo variants — primary, reversed, icon mark, horizontal, vertical, and any co-branded or campaign-specific treatments. File formats include PNG (transparent background), SVG, and EPS where available.

Always use files from this folder for any external-facing work. Do not recreate logos from memory or screenshots. See [[Logos & Brand Files]] for a breakdown of variants and usage rules.

---

## Clothing Mockups

**Drive folder:** https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1F56li7zjuPpfA0u6oHkKmkYXH1MjWTSi

Contains finished product mockup images generated through the pipeline (Photoroom and Nano Banana Pro). Organized by product category where possible. These are ready-to-use assets for social content, blog posts, ad creatives, or seller-facing educational material.

See [[Mockup & Content Library]] for organization details and how to request new mockup styles.

---

## Bulk Content

**Drive folder:** https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1QHup-aFkeONCFBIq8naOAPGkRDkXTXzf

Contains bulk exports from the Content Orchestra pipeline: social captions, carousel graphics, video exports, and other multi-format content batches. This folder is the output destination for the Publisher and Formatter agents when producing content at volume.

Content in this folder has passed QC review and is approved for use unless a subfolder is labeled "Review Required" or "Draft."

## On-Brand Usage Reminders

- Brand colors: Pink #ED128C, gradient #FF2B92 to #FF8433 (135deg), Orange #FF6B35 (accent), Navy #34424E
- Typography: Owners (headlines, buttons, fallback Archivo), Inter (body)
- Tagline: "You Design & Sell It, We Create & Fulfill It."
- No emojis or dashes in finalized social copy
- Full brand guidelines: [[00 Brand/Visual Identity/_Completeful Brand System|Completeful Brand System]]

---

## Adding New Assets

When new assets are created (new logo treatments, new mockup batches, new content exports), place them in the correct Drive folder and update the relevant detail page ([[Logos & Brand Files]] or [[Mockup & Content Library]]). Do not store production-ready assets locally only — they must live in Drive for team access.
